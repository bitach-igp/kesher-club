# T-0727 [INS] A linked institution's fee and allowance legs go through the member chapter's checking account, because Kesher's collection counterparty points at it
REQ: REQ-INS-003, REQ-INS-006 (after T-0721, T-0722) · Sprint: later (ADR-018 decision of 2026-11-11, proposed). UNBLOCKED 2026-11-11 (README question 45, answered by Crowded's reply, the docs and our sandbox): money moves between two chapters by an ACH to an internal counterparty, instant and free in both directions, so the collection account need not be in the member chapter by nature; it is in it BY CONFIGURATION: `collectionCounterpartyRef` (`apps/api/src/institutions/us-funding.ts:306`) must resolve to the member chapter's checking account, and an institution's ACH to it then lands there at once. A book payment still needs "within the same chapter".
Files: apps/api/src/config.ts, apps/api/src/config.test.ts, apps/api/src/institutions/allowance/workflow.test.ts, apps/api/src/institutions/collections.test.ts, specs/tasks/T-0727.md, specs/tasks/README.md
Source: ADR-018, M8. `allowance` posts `institution_master -> member wallet` (`allowance/activities.ts:108`) and `fee` posts `member wallet -> institution_master` (`collections.ts:146`); the institution's money is a ledger balance (`institution_master`), its cash is in the collection account. If that account is the member chapter's checking, the planner's single leg (checking to member, member to checking) is all there is. If not, a hop between chapters is needed and no card exists for it. This card adds no module code: the mirror is generic, so it proves the two flows end to end and refuses an unsafe setup at boot.
Assumption: M8 (unconfirmed), M4 (an ILS allowance or fee is excluded), M1.
Test first: TST-INS-003, TST-INS-006. With `wallet.mirror` and `institutions.usBanking` on, `CROWDED_COLLECTION_CHAPTER_ID` (no default) must equal `CROWDED_MEMBER_CHAPTER_ID` or boot refuses naming both keys (never a value) until a hop card exists; an allowance run of 25 USD students posts 25 `allowance` journals and queues 25 `from_checking` legs, each once, and the run's second approver rule and credit-line rule are unchanged; a run in ILS queues none and reports them excluded; a member without a Crowded account (M10) is refused up front inside the run's existing per-member failure path (retried 3 times, then reported, the others still paid); a fee paid from a USD wallet queues one `to_checking` leg; a replayed run or fee queues nothing more; money asserts compare `bigint`.
Done when: listed tests pass; `pnpm typecheck`, `pnpm lint`, `pnpm test`, `pnpm trace` green; only listed files touched
Out of scope: the cross-chapter hop (a new card if Crowded says the chapters differ); funding the institution (T-0654, T-0617); Israeli institutions (ILS).


## Update 2026-11-11 (question 45 answered)
No hop card is needed if the saved collection counterparty is the member chapter's checking account (a one-time setup step in docs/setup, to add when this card is built). The boot guard in the Test-first line stays: `CROWDED_COLLECTION_CHAPTER_ID` must equal `CROWDED_MEMBER_CHAPTER_ID`, and now means "the collection counterparty was made from the member chapter's checking account". Still to confirm with Crowded: that the instant conversion is guaranteed in production (the docs say "may"), and its limits.
