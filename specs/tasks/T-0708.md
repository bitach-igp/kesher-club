# T-0708 [LED] A nightly funding-side proof: what we credited from loads equals what Crowded moved into member wallets, with balance drift reported
REQ: REQ-LED-004 (after T-0698, T-0705, T-0707) · Sprint: later (ADR-018, proposed)
Files: apps/worker/src/recon/funding-balance.ts (new), apps/worker/src/recon/funding-balance.test.ts (new), apps/worker/src/recon/index.ts, apps/worker/src/jobs.ts, apps/worker/src/jobs.test.ts, specs/tasks/T-0708.md, specs/tasks/README.md
Source: ADR-018 (A7 and Findings, item 4) and the T-0654 note that credits and balances are not compared. The brief asks that member wallets plus pending in our ledger equal Crowded's wallets plus held funds. That cannot hold while P2P sends, card spend, FX and payouts move our wallet balances and Crowded sees none of them (a book payment needs the chapter checking account on one side). What can be proved is the funding side, and the balance difference is reported as drift until the owner decides what moves at Crowded (ADR-018, open question 1).
Assumption: A7 (balances and transactions are the vendor-side record), A4 (credits arrive by a book payment whose description is our load reference), A1 (the member's account ref from `ops.external_ref`). Unconfirmed: that the chapter's `hold` product account is where held collection funds sit and is readable with the accounts list (`product: hold`).
Test first: TST-LED-004. A clean day (three released loads, one refund) opens no case; each of these opens one case, keyed so a rerun opens none: a released load with no matching credit at Crowded (`load_credit_missing`), a credit from our book payments with no load (`credit_without_load`), an amount mismatch, a refund with no debit; the aggregate proof (our ledger's net `partner_settlement` for provider `crowded` in USD, less loads still held, equals the sum of Crowded credits to member wallets from our book payments, plus the Crowded held pool, less refunds) holds on a clean day and breaks by exactly the amount of a credit removed at the fake; a member whose Crowded `balance` differs from our wallet by more than what our non-load movements explain is NOT a break: it is reported as `driftMinor` per member and in total in the job report and a gauge `recon.funding_balance_drift_minor`, with no case, unless the option `strict` is on (set only after open question 1 is decided); a Crowded outage fails the run with nothing partial, logged `job_failed` and retried next tick; the run is done before 06:00 IL or the existing deadline case opens; the whole read is paged and bounded (members are read through `FundingAccountPort.account`, at most the platform's limits per minute, with a ceiling and a case if the member count exceeds it).
- The job reads, through `FundingAccountPort` (T-0698): chapter balances (`checking`, `held`), every member account's `balance`, `hold`, `available`, and the account transactions for the statement day, each with `collectPaymentId` where Crowded gives one. Our side is `ledger.journal` by `transfer_id` for kind `load` and `refund` transfers of provider `crowded`, as T-0684 reads them.
- Cases use the existing `recon_break` insert with `recon:crowded-balance:{kind}:{ref}:{runDate}`.
- Money is `bigint` in USD minor units throughout; `Money` for any amount that leaves the job.
Done when: listed tests pass; `pnpm typecheck`, `pnpm lint`, `pnpm test`, `pnpm trace` green; only listed files touched
Out of scope: mirroring P2P, spend, FX and payouts at Crowded (an owner decision, then its own cards); auto-correcting a break; alerts on the checking account's float; the card-processor and bank-feed partners.
