# T-0708 [LED] A nightly same-day balance proof: each member's ledger wallet plus pending equals their Crowded balance, loads credited equal what Crowded moved, and a difference is a break
REQ: REQ-LED-004 (after T-0698, T-0705, T-0707, T-0719, T-0722) · Sprint: later (ADR-018, proposed; rewritten 2026-11-11 after the owner's decision to mirror every wallet movement)
Files: apps/worker/src/recon/funding-balance.ts (new), apps/worker/src/recon/funding-balance.test.ts (new), apps/worker/src/recon/index.ts, apps/worker/src/jobs.ts, apps/worker/src/jobs.test.ts, apps/api/src/admin/cases.ts (adds `mirror_balance_break` and `load_credit_missing`), apps/api/src/admin/cases.test.ts, specs/tasks/T-0708.md, specs/tasks/README.md
Source: ADR-018, M6 (this SUPERSEDES the A7 finding that balances cannot match; the first version of this card reported drift because P2P, spend, FX and payouts were not mirrored. The owner decided on 2026-11-11 that they all are, so equality is now a break) and the T-0654 note that credits and balances are not compared. Crowded cannot hold a member's `pending` (no endpoint places a hold) and a held ACH load sits in Crowded's collection pool, not in the member's account, so the comparison is on `balance`, never `available` or `hold`.
Assumption: M6 (per-member equality, load-held pending against `Succeeded` unreleased payments, aggregate identity), A7 (balances and transactions are the vendor-side record), A4 (credits arrive by a book payment whose description is our load reference), A1 (the member's account ref from `ops.external_ref`), M4 (USD only; ILS is excluded and reported). Unconfirmed: that the accounts list returns `balance` for every member in a paged read within the rate limits (README question 41), that collect payments carry the contact so held money is attributed per member, and that the chapter's `hold` product account is where held collection funds sit.
Test first: TST-LED-004. A clean day (a load released, a P2P, a fee, a card clearing, an allowance, a closure refund, all mirrored at the fake) opens no case; each of these opens one case, keyed `recon:crowded-balance:{kind}:{ref}:{runDate}` so a rerun opens none: a member whose Crowded `balance` differs from `ledger wallet + pending (USD) - load-held pending - the member's unsettled mirror items` by any cent opens `mirror_balance_break` (member, ledger, Crowded, difference; amounts in cents, no names); a released load with no matching credit at Crowded opens `load_credit_missing`; a credit from our book payments with no load or journal opens `credit_without_load`; an amount mismatch on a credit; a refund with no debit; the load-held proof: per member, load-held `pending` equals the sum of that member's `Succeeded`, unreleased collect payments (`isReleased: false`), and a difference is a break; the aggregate identity (Crowded's total USD, that is chapter checking plus every member account plus the collection pool, equals the net debit of our USD `partner_settlement`, within the unsettled items) holds on a clean day and breaks by exactly the amount of a book payment removed at the fake; a member with an unsettled mirror item is compared net of it, and the item is covered by T-0732 after the cutoff; non-USD balances are not compared and are reported per currency as `excludedMinor`; a Crowded outage fails the run with nothing partial, logged `job_failed` and retried next tick; the run is done before 06:00 IL or the existing deadline case opens; the whole read is paged and bounded (members are read through `FundingAccountPort.account` and the mirror port, at most the platform's limits per minute, with a ceiling and a case if the member count exceeds it); the job report and the gauge `recon.mirror_balance_break_minor` carry counts and cents only.
- The job reads, through `FundingAccountPort` (T-0698) and `MirrorPort` (T-0718): chapter balances (`checking`, `held`), every mirrored member's `balance`, and the day's book transactions; our side is `ledger.account` balances for the member's USD `wallet` and `pending`, the load-held part found by the `load_hold` journals not yet released, and `ops.mirror_item` for items in flight.
- Cases use the existing `recon_break` insert. Money is `bigint` in USD minor units throughout; `Money` for any amount that leaves the job.
