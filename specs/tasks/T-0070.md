# T-0070 [SETUP] Prod is reachable only from the prod environment job: per-environment CI roles or deploy-role trust on the OIDC subject
REQ: — (enabling; found in the T-0050 review; after T-0050) · Sprint: 16 deploy
Files: infra/terraform/modules/oidc/main.tf, infra/terraform/modules/oidc/variables.tf, infra/terraform/envs/shared/main.tf, infra/terraform/envs/staging/main.tf, infra/terraform/envs/prod/main.tf, infra/terraform/tests/oidc.tftest.hcl, .github/workflows/deploy.yml, .github/workflows/release.yml, .github/workflows/preview.yml, docs/setup/release.md, docs/setup/deploy.md, specs/tasks/T-0070.md, specs/tasks/README.md
Test first: `modules/oidc` lets `ref:refs/heads/main`, `pull_request`, `environment:staging` and `environment:prod` all assume ONE CI role, and that role may assume every deploy role, prod's included (`kesher-deploy` trusts the CI role's ARN). So the GitHub approval gate (required reviewers, wait timer) and the deploy workflow's staging soak are not enforced in AWS: any workflow in the repository, including one from a pull request, can assume the CI role and then the prod deploy role. Split the trust so prod is reachable only from a job on the `prod` environment: either one CI role per environment (the prod role trusts `environment:prod` only, and only it may assume prod's `kesher-deploy`), or conditions on the deploy roles' trust policy on the session's OIDC subject (propagated as a session tag through `sts:TagSession`). Pull requests keep only what preview needs (staging, ECR push). `terraform test` asserts the trust conditions of each role; the workflows use the matching role variable.
Done when: listed tests pass; CI's terraform job green; a `pull_request` run cannot assume a prod role (checked by a failing `aws sts assume-role` in a throwaway workflow); only listed files touched
Out of scope: changing the deploy roles' permissions; the break-glass role. Stop and ask on conflict with the spec.
