# T-0617 [INS] US institutions pull USD by ACH, held until the bank's hold date
REQ: REQ-INS-009 (after T-0614) · Sprint: 12

Test first: TST-INS-009. A US institution's finance staff pull USD from the institution's bank account through the FundingPort's ACH method (the member ACH path of flow 2, with the institution as payer), with a step-up and an Idempotency-Key. The money posts to the institution's own pending (held) account until the provider's hold date. The ACH release job moves it to the institution's account. An ACH return during the hold reverses it. Held money counts neither toward a payout nor toward an allowance run's headroom. The tests cover: a $10,000.00 pull raises holds by 1,000,000 cents and leaves available unchanged; the release job releases it only after the date; a return reverses it with available never changed; another institution's staff get 404; `funding.ach` or `institutions.deposits` off answers FEATURE_OFF.
Done when: listed tests pass; Spectral clean; `pnpm typecheck`, `pnpm lint`, `pnpm test`, `pnpm trace` green; only listed files touched
Out of scope: linking the bank account (Plaid, with the ACH contract), ILS direct debit. Stop and ask on conflict with the spec.

## As built
- Sliced: the console screen (`apps/console/app/deposits`, `src/deposits.ts`, i18n) moved to T-0630, so this card is
  the API, the worker and the export, within a day's review.
- A pull is the member ACH load (`runLoad`) with the institution as payer and payee: a transfer of kind `deposit`,
  workflow id `ach-pull:{institutionId}:{Idempotency-Key}`. `LoadService` picks the destination by the transfer's
  kind. A member's load goes to the wallet or the pending purse. An institution's pull goes to its own hold account
  (a `pending` account owned by the institution, `AccountService.institutionHoldAccount`) and is released into its
  `institution_master`. So the funding webhook, the release job (now `load` or `deposit`), the stale-load sweep
  (deposits with a vendor ref) and a return's reversal all apply unchanged.
- The settlement and release journals are kind `deposit` and carry `institutionId` and `rail: 'ach'`, so the
  monthly export reads a released pull as a deposit. Its member column skips the institution's own hold account.
  The hold, and a return's reversal, never touch `institution_master`, so they never appear in the export.
- Held money is not spendable by construction: payouts and allowance runs read `institution_master` only.
- `POST` answers FEATURE_OFF before it asks for a step-up. A non-US institution is 422 `not_us_institution`. KYB must
  allow deposits. The first pull of a key is audited (`institution.ach_pull`).
