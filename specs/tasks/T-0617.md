Test first: TST-INS-009. A US institution's finance staff pull USD from the institution's bank account through the FundingPort's ACH method (the member ACH path of flow 2, with the institution as payer), with a step-up and an Idempotency-Key. The money posts to the institution's own pending (held) account until the provider's hold date. The ACH release job moves it to the institution's account. An ACH return during the hold reverses it. Held money counts neither toward a payout nor toward an allowance run's headroom. The tests cover: a $10,000.00 pull raises holds by 1,000,000 cents and leaves available unchanged; the release job releases it only after the date; a return reverses it with available never changed; another institution's staff get 404; `funding.ach` or `institutions.deposits` off answers FEATURE_OFF.
