Test first: TST-INS-006 (billing rows). Found while slicing REC-15 (T-0923): members can pay fee items (T-0606) and I08 lists them (T-0611), but nothing creates `org.fee_item`, so no member ever owes anything.
- `POST /institutions/{id}/fee-items` is for the institution's finance staff, with an Idempotency-Key. It takes a description, an amount a member (bigint minor units, the institution's currency), a due date, and the members: every active member, or a listed few by roster member id. One call is one batch: one fee item per member, all or none.
- A retried key answers the same batch; the same key with a different body answers 409.
- I08 gains "Bill members". It checks the amount and the due date before the call.

The tests cover:
- staff (not finance) get 403; another institution's finance gets 404; `institutions.money` off answers FEATURE_OFF;
- an amount of 0 or more than 10,000.00, a due date in the past, or a listed member who is not active answers 422;
- a batch for every active member makes one due item each, which the member lists and can pay;
- a retried key makes nothing new.
