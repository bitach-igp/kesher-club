Decision (2026-11-23, coordinator, on the owner's delegation; reversible, nothing is deleted):
- KEEP `DunningService` and WIRE LATER behind `billing.stripe`, in T-0793 to T-0795. The earlier recommendation here ("do not delete yet and do not wire yet; decide with T-0736/T-0737") is resolved as: keep, and build only what cannot be wasted now.
- Reasons. (1) Dunning is the only code that moves a membership to `past_due` or `paused` on a failed charge; without it a funder whose card fails keeps the plan's FX markup, entitlements and card cap indefinitely, once Stripe billing is live. (2) The service is built and unit-tested; deleting throws away working, reviewed code for a path (T-0737, wallet fee) that is itself BLOCKED and being re-cut after the Crowded answers (T-0749, T-0759). (3) T-0736 keeps the Stripe adapters in the repo "Reversible" (ADR-018) and its option is to turn the three billing flags off for the first deploy, which means nothing runs dunning today either way. (4) If T-0737 later replaces Stripe billing, deleting `dunning.ts` is a small follow-up (two-release column drop for `dunning_attempts`, club-ddl rule 1).
- Premise check against origin/main f4c803e (2026-11-23). TRUE: `DunningService` is referenced only in `apps/worker/src/billing/dunning.ts` and its test, not in `jobs.ts`, `main.ts` or `app.ts`; no `stripe` or `billing` webhook binding exists (`app.ts` binds only a `simulator` route for a sim billing binding, with a no-op processor); `StripeBillingAdapter.parseWebhook` has no caller. `billing.stripe`, `billing.applePay` and `billing.googlePay` are STILL `defaultProd: true` on main (T-0736 is not built, so the T-0043 guard still refuses a prod boot with billing on a simulator). CORRECTED/ADDED: an architecture constraint the earlier text missed: `DunningService` lives in `apps/worker`, which imports `@kesher/api/...`; `apps/api` therefore cannot import it. The receiver must only verify and store the event, and a worker job consumes it (the pattern of `card-token-events` in `jobs.ts`). That is why the wire-up is three cards (T-0793 service fixes, T-0794 receiver in the API, T-0795 worker job) and not two.
What stays dead until then: `DunningService`, `StripeBillingAdapter.parseWebhook` as a receiver, `membership.dunning_attempts` and the `past_due` and `paused` statuses in production. 
What is true TODAY (risk of leaving it unwired): with `billing.stripe` on, a failed renewal changes nothing in Kesher (entitlement-side only, no ledger effect, money-state not touched). With the flags off, as T-0736 proposes for the first deploy, no membership exists and the risk is nil. So the exposure is: the day Stripe billing is turned on without T-0793 to T-0795, failed payers stay `active`. Compensating control: the flag itself (guarded by T-0043; a prod boot refuses while billing is a simulator) and staff can pause a membership by hand through `MembershipService`.
