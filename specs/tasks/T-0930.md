- **Reads.** `GET /admin/cases` lists `ops.case` rows by kind and status, newest first, cursor-paged: suspense, deposit not on statement, recon and balance breaks, payout account mismatches. The monitoring kinds stay in the compliance alerts queue (T-0908). `GET /admin/cases/{id}` returns one case.
- **Writes.** Assign, then resolve or dismiss with a note of 1 to 500 characters. Each write is audited and needs the ops role; support reads only.
- **Schema.** `ops.case` gains `assigned_to`, `assigned_at`, `decided_by` and `note`, with a desktop handoff. A suspense case resolves only through its deposit (T-0631), so here it can only be dismissed.
- **Payload.** Member text in a case (a remitter's reference) is returned as data.
**Tests.**
- the filters;
- assign then resolve, with the audit entries;
- support answers 403;
- a resolved case takes no second decision (409);
- a suspense case answers 422 to resolve and accepts dismiss.
