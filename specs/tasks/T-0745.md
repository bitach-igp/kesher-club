# T-0745 [WAL] An ACH load reads `succeeded` once its hold date has passed, so the credit and the release sweep can finish it
REQ: REQ-WAL-003 (fix to T-0741; found by the independent review of T-0741, 2026-11-12) · Sprint: fix
Files: packages/adapters/src/crowded/funding.ts, packages/adapters/src/crowded/credit.ts, packages/adapters/src/crowded/funding.contract.test.ts, specs/tasks/T-0741.md, specs/tasks/T-0705.md, specs/tasks/T-0745.md, specs/tasks/README.md
Source: `CrowdedFunding.getLoad` answered `on_hold` for every Succeeded ACH, with `holdUntil` = first-seen success + `CROWDED_ACH_HOLD_DAYS` business days, and never `succeeded` afterwards. The T-0704 credit port refuses `on_hold` (`NOT_SUCCEEDED`) and T-0705 (the release sweep re-reads after the hold date, then `creditWallet`, then the `{wf}:release` journal) needs `succeeded` once the date passes, so no ACH load could ever be credited. The plain `FundingSimulator.settle` already reads `succeeded` after the hold.
Test first: Crowded adapter tests: an ACH Succeeded before its hold date reads `on_hold` with `holdUntil`; at the date and after it, `succeeded` with no `holdUntil`; two adapter instances on one ledger agree; a returned ACH after the hold date is still `failed` / `RETURNED`; card loads are unchanged; a zero hold reads `succeeded` at once. Credit tests: an ACH load before its hold date is refused `NOT_SUCCEEDED` with no money call, and past it is credited once. Follow-ups: a `release-funds` 403 whose message is not the "not enabled" one, a 401, a 400 and a 422 are all plain `PROVIDER_UNAVAILABLE` (never the fund-holding text, never a final `refused`, so the key is not burned).
- `getLoad`: for an ACH, `holdUntil = addBusinessDays(firstSeenSuccess, achHoldDays)` from the stored first-seen value (stable across processes); `holdUntil <= this.now()` answers `{ status: 'succeeded' }` with no `holdUntil`, else `on_hold` as before.
- Wording: T-0741 ("an ACH load asked before its hold date is not checked here") and T-0705 now agree: `on_hold` is `NOT_SUCCEEDED`; `succeeded` only after the hold date.
Done when: listed tests pass; `pnpm typecheck`, `pnpm lint`, `pnpm test`, `pnpm trace` green; only listed files touched
Out of scope: any change in `apps/api` (see As-built); instant access for ACH (T-0733); the live vendor's behaviour (README q29, q35); a webhook-driven credit.
