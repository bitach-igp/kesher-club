# T-0715 [WAL] Add money opens the Crowded payment page and shows the load as pending until the vendor says it settled
REQ: REQ-WAL-002, REQ-WAL-003 (after T-0714, T-0738) · Sprint: later (ADR-018, proposed). NEEDS A DESIGN: the Tab 2 screen spec for P07 (Add money) has no hosted-page state; a person confirms the states and copy before this is built.
Files: apps/mobile/app/fund/add-money.model.ts, apps/mobile/app/fund/add-money.model.test.ts, apps/mobile/app/fund/add-money.tsx, apps/mobile/app/fund/add-money.test.tsx, apps/mobile/src/api.ts, apps/mobile/src/i18n.ts, specs/tasks/T-0715.md, specs/tasks/README.md
Source: ADR-018 and T-0524 (the P07 screen, which today presents Stripe's payment sheet when a load is `requires_action`). UI cards use the tokens package and the Tab 2 spec; no new colours or spacing values. A hosted load differs: the member leaves the app (or an in-app browser) to pay, and the load settles later by webhook, so the screen must be honest about "payment started, not yet received".
Assumption: A3 (a payment page, card or bank transfer chosen there; Apple Pay and Google Pay are not offered, so the existing wallet buttons are hidden for a hosted load), A4 (a bank transfer is held after it succeeds, so the screen shows the existing "on hold until" state), A8 (the action).
Test first: TST-WAL-002, TST-WAL-003 (screen rows). The model tests cover: choosing "Pay on Crowded's page" (the copy is from the design, not invented here) calls `POST /loads` once per attempt key with consent and opens `action.url` through an injected `openPayment(url)` seam (an in-app browser, never an embedded WebView holding the app's tokens); returning from the page shows `pending` ("We are waiting for your payment to be confirmed") and polls `GET /loads/{id}` (T-0714) on an interval with backoff and a stop, never claiming success; `settled` shows the amount credited; `on_hold` shows "on hold until {date}"; `failed` shows the reason with a retry that makes a NEW attempt key; closing and reopening the app finds the load through the member's loads and resumes the pending state; a `requires_action` load with `clientSecret` still opens the Stripe sheet (unchanged); an `action.url` that is not `https` is refused and shown as the generic error; English and Hebrew copy is mirrored and the Hebrew screen is right-to-left; no amount is a `number`.
- Real-device and web evidence (screenshots under `handoff/done/`) is requested through `handoff/desktop/` per `handoff/README.md`, as T-0524 did.
Done when: listed tests pass; `pnpm typecheck`, `pnpm lint`, `pnpm test`, `pnpm trace` green; the design is attached to the card; only listed files touched
Out of scope: an embedded payment form (needs Crowded's answer on the embedded token); Apple Pay and Google Pay on the page; the funder-mode member picker (T-0524); auto-reload (it needs a saved method and stays Stripe-only until Crowded has one); the closure screen (T-0526).

Amended 2026-11-12 (scout): the flow starts with a details step when `GET /me/funding-details` is 404 (legal name, email, date of birth, T-0738; the fields and copy are the owner's, T-0738 I1 and I4) and the screen must show a clear refusal for a member whose funding account is rejected, frozen or closed (T-0740 answers `FEATURE_OFF` naming the status, no vendor text). The details step is the likelier thing to push this card past 400 lines: if it does, cut it into its own card after T-0738 and keep this one for the payment page and the pending states. The first slice is self top-up in USD only (T-0714).
