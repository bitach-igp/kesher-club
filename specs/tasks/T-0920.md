# T-0920 [CMP] Recorded walkthroughs of money flows REC-01 to REC-05 on the running product, and the recording harness

Test first: the REC files in `handoff/done/recordings/` show each flow's automated tests, not the product (docs/bank-review/README.md, "Recordings"). This card builds the harness and scripts the first five flows against the T-0031 stack with the T-0032 personas, in Playwright with `recordVideo`. The browser records its own pages, so no screen capture is needed and nothing outside the page is in the video. It runs in a cloud session or on the PC; CI only once Actions minutes allow. Each video has three panes: the member app (T-0033) where a screen exists, the simulator panel for the vendor's side (and the clock for holds), and the console's Customer 360 (T-0919) for the booked result. Where no member screen exists, the step is an `@kesher/sdk` call printed as a caption ("API: POST /wallet/loads"), never presented as a screen. `flows.ts` holds the shared steps: sign in as a persona, open the three panes, advance the clock, read Customer 360. Coverage: REC-01, 02 and 03 (load by card, ACH hold and release, other purse): P07 add money in funder mode (T-0524), then the member's wallet; REC-04 (auto-reload): the P07 auto-reload tab (T-0525), a spend below the threshold from the panel, then the top-up; REC-05 (send): the pay screens. Each spec asserts that the amounts on screen and in Customer 360 equal the `money-flows.md` worked example (bigint, minor units), so a recording cannot pass while showing a wrong number. Output: `REC-NN-<name>.webm` under `apps/demo/out/`, which `apps/demo/.gitignore` excludes: videos never enter git.
