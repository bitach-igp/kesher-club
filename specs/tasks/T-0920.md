Test first: the REC files in `handoff/done/recordings/` show each flow's automated tests, not the product (docs/bank-review/README.md, "Recordings"). This card builds the harness and scripts the first five flows against the T-0031 stack with the T-0032 personas, in Playwright with `recordVideo`. The browser records its own pages, so no screen capture is needed and nothing outside the page is in the video. It runs in a cloud session or on the PC; CI only once Actions minutes allow. Each video has three panes: the member app (T-0033) where a screen exists, the simulator panel for the vendor's side (and the clock for holds), and the console's Customer 360 (T-0919) for the booked result. Where no member screen exists, the step is an `@kesher/sdk` call printed as a caption ("API: POST /wallet/loads"), never presented as a screen. `flows.ts` holds the shared steps: sign in as a persona, open the three panes, advance the clock, read Customer 360. Coverage: REC-01, 02 and 03 (load by card, ACH hold and release, other purse): P07 add money in funder mode (T-0524), then the member's wallet; REC-04 (auto-reload): the P07 auto-reload tab (T-0525), a spend below the threshold from the panel, then the top-up; REC-05 (send): the pay screens. Each spec asserts that the amounts on screen and in Customer 360 equal the `money-flows.md` worked example (bigint, minor units), so a recording cannot pass while showing a wrong number. Output: `REC-NN-<name>.webm` under `apps/demo/out/`, which `apps/demo/.gitignore` excludes: videos never enter git.

As built (in progress: REC-01 to REC-04 pass in one run, about 1.3 minutes; REC-05 is blocked, see below):
- The harness page is served by a real local HTTP server, one per recording on a free port. It used to be served by Playwright's `page.route`; Chromium counted that intercepted page as public, and its Private Network Access checks refused to frame the member app and the panel on localhost (`ERR_BLOCKED_BY_PRIVATE_NETWORK_ACCESS_CHECKS`), so the sign-in button never appeared.
- REC-01: P07 by card, $100.00 (10,000 cents) into Dana's dollar purse. The receipt, Home and Customer 360 agree.
- REC-03: Noa pays $100.00 by card into her shekel purse. The recorder reads the API's own quote and load from the member app's network. It asserts that the quote card, the receipt, Home and the Customer 360 delta all equal the quote's total, and that total + markup is $100.00 at the quoted rate, to the agora. The live club rate is not the 3.70 of the money-flows example; on the first runs it credited 36,822 agorot.
- REC-04: Tamar sets the auto-reload rule on P07 (threshold $50.00, target $200.00, the dev build's `pm_ok` card). A $155.00 tap at a café comes from the vendor's side: the panel's card-issuing `authorize` hook builds the signed JIT request, and it is delivered to `/v1/jit/card-authorizations`. The processor card id comes from the recording stack's readiness port (`?cardOf=`), development only, because no member or staff surface shows processor ids. Available falls to 4,500 cents. The auto-reload job runs twice; it loads exactly 15,500 cents once, which is money-flows §4's worked example.
- REC-02 runs last because it moves the shared clock 5 days, past what the console's real-time staff sign-in accepts. ACH $100.00: the hold is shown in Customer 360 (holds +10,000 cents, available unchanged). The panel clock then moves and `ach-release-holds` runs: available +10,000 cents, holds back to before. After the move, the booked result is read through the staff API at the API's clock and shown as text in the console pane. The member pane is reopened ("five days later Dana opens the app again"), because the app judges token expiry by the browser's real clock and would not refresh a token the moved API clock has expired.
- REC-05 is `test.fixme`. The pay screens (M13 to M15) call `useDevice()`, and the root layout never mounts a `DeviceProvider`, so every one of them throws "DeviceProvider is missing" on web (`apps/mobile/src/device.ts:59`; `apps/mobile/app/pay/index.tsx:19`; also `app/card/index.tsx:17`, `app/club/esim.tsx:17`, `app/help/emergency.tsx:15`). It needs a product fix in `apps/mobile/app/_layout.tsx` before it can be recorded on its screens. The scripted flow, Dana → Avi $25.00 with deltas asserted on both sides, is in the spec, ready.
