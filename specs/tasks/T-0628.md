# T-0628 [PO] An institution payout keeps its requester through a cool-off
REQ: REQ-INS-004, REQ-PO-002 (after T-0527, T-0625) · Sprint: 13
Files: apps/api/src/payouts/service.ts, apps/api/src/payouts/handlers.test.ts, contracts/openapi.yaml, packages/sdk/src/schema.generated.ts, specs/tasks/T-0628.md, specs/tasks/README.md
Test first: TST-INS-004 (a cooled-off payout's requester cannot approve it). Found recording REC-13 (T-0927).
- An institution payout's approval used to be asked for only after its cool-off, by the worker's sweep. The sweep
  rebuilds the payout's input from the stored approval, which did not exist yet, so it named the institution as
  the requester: the list read "Asked by Former staff", and the finance member who asked was offered Approve on
  their own withdrawal, breaking the two-person rule above ₪5,000.
- The approval is now asked for when the payout begins, before any cool-off, with the real requester; their
  request is their approval. Nobody decides while it cools off: `decide` now refuses on the hold itself, since
  the approval exists from the start.
- During the cool-off the list now shows who asked, the approvals so far and the approvals needed.

The tests cover: a ₪6,000 payout to a new account cools off and names its requester; nobody can approve it then;
after 24 hours and the sweep it waits for a second approver; the requester's approve answers 409; a different
finance member's approve submits it; the account goes from ₪20,000.00 to ₪14,000.00.

Done when: listed tests pass; Spectral clean; `pnpm typecheck`, `pnpm lint`, `pnpm test`, `pnpm trace` green; only listed files touched
Out of scope: member payouts (their requester is the member, and their approval keeps the old order). Stop and ask on conflict with the spec.
