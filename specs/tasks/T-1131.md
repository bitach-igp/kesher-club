1. Two concurrent SSO flows from the same IdP subject take the audit advisory lock and the link row lock in opposite orders. In 1 of 12 rounds one answers `unavailable` through 40P01 (deadlock). Make the lock order consistent (take the same lock first in both paths).
2. Every class-23 error (including 23502 not-null, which could mask a bug) now maps to `refused`. Narrow it to 23505 (unique) and 23503 (foreign key); anything else is an internal error.
3. The audit advisory lock is now held for the whole accept plus link transaction. Measure the hold time under concurrent sign-ins and say whether it is acceptable.
Test first: a repeated concurrent race (at least 100 rounds on real Postgres) with zero 40P01; a forced 23502 is not `refused`.
