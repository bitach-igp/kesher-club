# T-0624 [INS] Keeping KYB current: daily sanctions rescreen and the refresh sweep

Test first: TST-INS-013, TST-INS-014. Two jobs:
- **Daily:** screens every approved institution and its people through the port. A new potential match writes a hit check and suspends payouts and credit draws through the gate until it is disposed.
- **Daily:** sets any decision past its refresh date (12, 24 or 36 months by risk, configurable) to `approved_with_limits`, with reason `refresh_overdue`.

Both are idempotent per day. The tests cover:
- a potential match on the chair of an institution holding ₪1,000.00 with a ₪5,000.00 limit refuses a ₪500.00 payout and a ₪3,000.00 run, while a ₪800.00 run still pays;
- a medium-risk institution approved on 2024-10-01 reads `approved_with_limits` after the 2026-10-02 sweep;
- running either job twice on a day writes nothing more.

Done when: listed tests pass; `pnpm typecheck`, `pnpm lint`, `pnpm test`, `pnpm trace` green; only listed files touched
Out of scope: registry and IRS polling (added to the same jobs once a vendor is bound). Stop and ask on conflict with the spec.

As built:
- **The gate.** An open sanctions match suspends `payouts` and `credit` whatever the status, and answers FEATURE_OFF with `sanctionsMatches: [checkId…]`. An open match is a subject's latest sanctions check that is a hit, either not yet disposed of or disposed of as a true match. Deposits and allowances within the balance stay open, so the institution's own money keeps reaching its members. An allowance run that needs credit is refused with `creditClosed: true`. Its message still says "no credit until the institution's KYB is fully approved" (credit.ts, outside this card); T-0926's console words should name the match instead.
- **The rescreen** (`kyb-rescreen`, 05:00 Israel time, one run per market per day):
  - It asks the KybPort about every approved institution in the market and the people in its record. It screens the institution under the legal name its identifier check used, falling back to the institution's name.
  - It writes a sanctions check only when the answer differs from the latest one: a new match is a `hit`, a delisting is `clear`, and a subject never screened gets its first row. Each row carries `detail.rescreen: true`.
  - The same answer as last time writes nothing. A false positive that compliance has disposed of is therefore not raised again each morning, and a second run on a day writes nothing.
  - A provider outage skips that institution until the next day.
  - It sits behind `institutions.kyb.checks`, like the checks themselves.
  - It records sanctions only; PEP rescreening is not part of REQ-INS-013.
- **The refresh** (`kyb-refresh`, 05:15, club-wide):
  - `InstitutionKyb.refreshOverdue(now, months = REFRESH_MONTHS)` lapses each `approved` decision whose `decided_at` plus 12, 24 or 36 months (high, medium or low risk) has passed. The table is configurable.
  - A lapse writes `approved_with_limits`, keeping the risk, with reason `refresh_overdue`. The key is `kyb-refresh:{institution}:{day}`, the actor is the system owner with role `system`, and it is audited as `institution.kyb.decide`.
  - A decision that is already limited is left alone, so a rerun writes nothing.
