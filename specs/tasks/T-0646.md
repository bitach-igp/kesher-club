## As built
- `/account` (nav "Account") is for finance staff and admins only; the nav hides it from other staff and the page
  sends them home. It shows:
  - how to fund the account: the payment reference, plus the ACH pull under Deposits for US institutions;
  - a month picker (the last 12 months, never one that has not started; a future or malformed ask falls back to the
    current month);
  - the month's statement in the market's currency;
  - CSV and Excel downloads that save the API's bytes under its filename.
- **The statement is checked before it renders.** Opening plus every row must equal the closing balance in bigint,
  and each row's running balance must follow from the one before. A statement that does not add up shows an error,
  never numbers.
- **I12 reports** is a section on the same page. It holds the statement downloads, plus links to the month's
  collections export (I08) and the allowance run history (I07), the exports that exist.
- **Outside the card's list:** `src/collections.ts` `decodeExport` now takes any envelope with `base64`, generic so
  the collections callers type-check unchanged.
