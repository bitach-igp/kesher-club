## Open question (autopilot, 2026-11-05): needs a design decision before building
The card says to reserve an institution payout's money `institution_master` → a new payout-reserve account. Read from the code, that has consequences the card's file list does not cover:
- `institutions/export.ts` and `institutions/statement.ts` net every posting on `institution_master`; a reserve debit would show in the month's export/statement as a payout (and its release as an inflow) before any money leaves, and `credit.ts` headroom and `dashboard.ts` balance read `institution_master`/the holds account too.
- Reusing the existing `pending` hold account (T-0617) would show payout reserves as "ACH holds" in `institutionHolds`.
Options: (A) add the new account type and teach the export, statement, dashboard and credit readers to treat the reserve as the institution's own money still (a larger card, several readers, one migration); (B) no money reserve: count waiting payouts as a claim in `assertFunds` under the institution row lock (no ledger movement, no migration), plus the approval expiry; (C) leave institution payouts as they are and only add the expiry. The member side (T-0540) is done. Not started: choose A, B or C.
