## Open question (autopilot, 2026-11-05): needs a design decision before building
The card says to reserve an institution payout's money `institution_master` → a new payout-reserve account. Read from the code, that has consequences the card's file list does not cover:
- `institutions/export.ts` and `institutions/statement.ts` net every posting on `institution_master`; a reserve debit would show in the month's export/statement as a payout (and its release as an inflow) before any money leaves, and `credit.ts` headroom and the `dashboard.ts` balance (through `institutionBalance`) read `institution_master` too.
- Reusing the existing `pending` hold account (T-0617) would show payout reserves as "ACH holds" in `institutionHolds`.
Options: (A) add the new account type and teach the export, statement, dashboard and credit readers to treat the reserve as the institution's own money still (a larger card, several readers, one migration); (B) no money reserve: count waiting payouts as a claim in `assertFunds` under the institution row lock (no ledger movement, no migration), plus the approval expiry; (C) leave institution payouts as they are and only add the expiry. The member side (T-0540) is done. Not started: choose A, B or C.

## Decision (Zvi, 2026-11-05): option A, split in three
Reserve the money in the ledger, in a distinct `payout_reserve` account, so reserved money is visible in the ledger at any instant and enforced by it (as T-0540 does for members), and teach every reader that the reserve is still the institution's own money, so no statement or export ever shows a payout before money leaves. Built in this order, each mergeable on its own:
1. **T-0554**: approval expiry for institution payouts (no reserve yet).
2. **T-0555**: the `payout_reserve` account type (migration), its provisioning, and the readers (statement, export, dashboard; credit headroom stays on available). Safe to merge alone: the new account is empty until 3.
3. **T-0550** (this card, narrowed): reserve and unreserve in the payout workflow, posting to the T-0555 account.
