Test first: TST-INS-007. Once the bank is chosen, its incoming-credit notifications arrive through a new BankPort (simulator first, ports before vendors). The adapter matches the payment reference (T-0614) and posts the same `deposit` journal under the same key, so a credit already recorded by hand does not post twice. A credit whose reference matches no institution opens a suspense case for ops and is never guessed onto an institution. A daily job reconciles the bank statement against posted deposits and opens a case for any difference. The manual record of T-0614 stays as the fallback.

## As built
- Built ahead of ADR-006 with the simulator, ports before vendors. `institutions.bankFeed` (default off) gates it. Its
  trigger is the bank chosen and its adapter passing `bank.contract.ts`.
- **BankPort.** One signed notification per incoming credit, and the statement for a value date. A credit carries
  the rail, the amount, the bank's line reference, the value date and the remitter's text (untrusted data, at most
  140 characters). Nothing personal.
- **Matching** (`InstitutionDeposits.applyBankCredit`). A credit quoting exactly one payment reference that names
  an institution is recorded by the feed (`recorded_by` the system party, note "Bank feed"). It is posted through
  the same `post()`: the same `deposit:{id}` transfer and journal keys, and the same `(rail, bank_reference)`
  uniqueness. A bank-verified credit needs no second person; the two-person rule is for lines people type.
- **Suspense.** These open an `ops.case` of kind `suspense` and post nothing, never a guess: no reference, an unknown
  one, two references, the wrong currency for the rail, a line recorded by hand with another amount, or KYB not
  allowing deposits. Ops then record it through T-0614. The credit id goes to `ops.external_ref`.
- **Daily reconciliation** (`deposit-reconcile`, 06:30 Israel, per market). It applies every credit on
  yesterday's statement, so a lost notification still posts, once. A deposit recorded for that day that the bank
  does not show opens a `deposit_not_on_statement` case. A bank outage fails the run, and the scheduler retries it.
- **Wiring.** `/hooks/bank` is bound only where a bank is bound for the market; the job resolves the port only when
  it runs. `InstitutionDeposits` moved into the app's services, so the webhook and the job share it.
- **Extra files.** `packages/ports/src/{bank.simulator.test,index,contracts,catalogue,catalogue.test}.ts` (20 ports
  now), `apps/simulators/src/host.ts` (the `bank` simulator and its `credit` hook), `apps/api/src/app.ts`,
  `apps/worker/src/jobs.ts`, `jobs.test.ts` and `main.test.ts`, and the port counts in
  `apps/api/src/admin/handlers.test.ts` and `apps/admin/src/providers/release-gates.test.ts`. `apps/api/src/webhooks/handler.ts` needed no change.
- Noticed, not changed: the webhook store keeps the first delivery of an event id, so a forged copy that arrives
  first makes the genuine one answer 401. For the bank feed the daily statement still posts the credit.
