Test first: TST-INS-007. Institutions pay into Kesher's bank account by Masav, wire or ACH, quoting their own payment reference. Kesher finance reads the statement internally (decision 2026-10-20) and records each transfer against the institution: amount, currency, rail, bank reference, value date. A second, different finance member approves it. Only then does it post, as journal `deposit`: Dr `partner_settlement`, Cr the institution's own account (T-0613), keyed by the rail and the bank reference, so one statement line posts once whoever records it.
- **API** (staff with the `finance` role; behind `institutions.deposits`):
  - `POST /admin/institutions/{id}/deposits` records one and is the first approval;
  - `POST /admin/deposits/{id}/approve` and `/reject` (with a reason);
  - `GET /admin/institutions/{id}/deposits` lists them with the account's balance.
- **Payment reference.** Each institution gets a stable reference derived from its id (`KC-` + 8 characters), shown to its staff in `GET /me/institution` so they know what to quote.
- **Export.** The monthly export reads deposits as their own kind with a `depositsIn` total.
- **Tests cover:**
  - nothing posts until B approves, and then the account rises by exactly the amount (bigint);
  - the recorder cannot approve their own;
  - the same rail and bank reference recorded again, for any institution, answers 409;
  - a rejected deposit posts nothing;
  - Masav is ILS only and ACH USD only;
  - the export and the console balance show the deposit.
