# T-0700 [WAL] The Crowded funding adapter starts a load as a collection and an intent and reads it back
REQ: REQ-WAL-002, REQ-WAL-003 (after T-0697, T-0698, T-0699) · Sprint: later (ADR-018, proposed)
Files: packages/adapters/src/crowded/funding.ts (new), packages/adapters/src/crowded/funding.contract.test.ts (new), packages/adapters/src/crowded/fake-crowded.ts, packages/adapters/src/crowded/map.ts, packages/adapters/src/crowded/index.ts, specs/tasks/T-0700.md, specs/tasks/README.md
Source: ADR-018 and the snapshot ("Collections", "Create Collection", "Create Payment Intent", "Get Intent", "Payment Status Lifecycle", "Collect Payments"). The adapter reuses `CrowdedClient` and the key ledger of the institution adapter; it is a separate class implementing `FundingPort` (hosted only) so the institution port is untouched.
Assumption: A3 (one collection per load, claimed by title, plus one intent for the paying contact; Apple Pay and Google Pay unavailable), A4 (the hold starts at a bank transfer's `Succeeded`; hold days come from config, no default), A8.
Test first: TST-WAL-002 and TST-WAL-003 (the port's `fundingContract` with `hosted: true`, plus the Crowded-only block, against the fake). `createLoad({ method: 'hosted', ... }, key)` makes one collection titled with the load reference (at most 50 characters, no payer text) with a fixed `requestedAmount` and one intent for the payer's contact, and answers `requires_action` with `action.url` set to the intent's `paymentUrl`; the same key twice makes one collection and one intent (a collection with that title and amount, claimed by another key, is not reused); `getLoad` maps the payment statuses: `Pending` and `Processing` to `processing`, a card `Succeeded` to `succeeded`, a bank-transfer `Succeeded` to `on_hold` with `holdUntil` = the payment's success instant plus the configured hold business days, `Failed` and `Cancelled` to `failed`, `Returned` to `failed` with `failureCode: 'RETURNED'`, `Refunded` to `failed` with `failureCode: 'REFUNDED'`, and an intent with no payment to `requires_action`; a non-USD amount is `VALIDATION_FAILED`; `payerIp` and consent are passed to the intent, `successUrl` and `failureUrl` carry the `{intentId}` placeholder; a Crowded 5xx is `PROVIDER_UNAVAILABLE` with the key kept so a retry finds the same collection; an unknown load is `UNKNOWN_LOAD`.
- The load id is `crowded:{chapterId}:{paymentRef or intentId}` built with `crowdedRef`; until a payment exists it is the intent. `getLoad` reads the intent with its `payments` and applies the fixed-amount rule from the docs: the intent status is authoritative, an intent `Returned` or `Cancelled` maps as above. A second payment attempt on one intent after a `Failed` one is the same load.
- The amount is what the member intended (`requestedAmount`); the processing fee (`fee`, `feeCovered`) is recorded on the load's metadata and never changes what the member is credited. A `Succeeded` payment whose net amount differs from the load's after fee rules is not guessed at: `getLoad` answers `processing` and the load stays open for people (the fee payer is an open question to Crowded).
- The fake gains collections (existing), intents with a payment list, payment status controls (`pay(intentId, 'card' | 'ach')`, `settle`, `fail`, `returnPayment`, `cancel`), and `refundCard`. The hosted payment lifecycle is fake-only: the sandbox cannot drive it.
- Hold days: the adapter option `achHoldDays` (an integer, set by config in T-0709; no default in the adapter).
Done when: listed tests pass; `pnpm typecheck`, `pnpm lint`, `pnpm test`, `pnpm trace` green; only listed files touched
Out of scope: webhooks (T-0703); the release step and `creditWallet` (T-0704); refunds (T-0706); the statement listing (T-0707); registry wiring (T-0709); the embedded token; payment plans.

## Open questions
- Crowded states no lifetime for a `paymentUrl`. `expiresAt` is therefore our own page lifetime (`pageTtlMs`, persisted per load at creation), absent when not configured: confirm with Crowded whether the page expires, and set the option to match (T-0709).
- Which timestamp is the success instant of a bank transfer? The adapter reads `completedAt`, else `updatedAt`, else `createdAt` of the payment (README question 35); the hold is measured from it.
- Who bears the processing fee (README question 33)? Until answered a Succeeded payment whose net differs from the load is `processing` for people.

## As-built
- `funding.ts`: `CrowdedFunding implements FundingPort`, hosted only; other methods, `refund`, `getRefund` and `parseWebhook` answer `UNSUPPORTED_METHOD` with no request. Options: `client`, `chapterId`, `hmacKey` (32+ characters), `achHoldDays` (integer 0 to 365, no default), `redirectHosts` (required, bare lower-case hosts, no wildcard), `ledger`, `contactOf` (customer id to a `c:<id>` contact ref; default the id is one), `pageTtlMs`, `now`. A bad option throws in the constructor.
- `createLoad`: the title is `Load <key>` cut to 50; a collection with that title and amount is reused only if no other key claimed it (`load:claim:<collection>` in the ledger), else one is made, then one intent for the contact with `payerIp`, `userConsented: true` and the redirect urls. Two calls at once with one key make one collection (in-process) and a replay finds the key in the ledger. A reused key with another input is `VALIDATION_FAILED` (the port's error set has no conflict code). A 5xx or transport error is `PROVIDER_UNAVAILABLE` and records nothing, so the same key finds the same collection. A non-USD amount, under $1.00 or above 2^53-1 cents is `VALIDATION_FAILED` with no request.
- Carried from T-0697: the payer IP must pass `isPublicIp`; the adapter cannot see the connection, so the caller that terminates the member's request must take it from the socket or trusted proxy header, never the body (to be enforced where the route is built); redirect urls pass `assertHttps`, an exact `host` match on `redirectHosts`, are sent as the normalised `new URL(...).href` with `intentId={intentId}` added (a caller-made `intentId` parameter is dropped, the fragment is kept); `expiresAt` is stamped at creation and stored in the ledger, so reads and replays answer the same instant. The vendor's `paymentUrl` must be https or the call is `PROVIDER_UNAVAILABLE`.
- Refs: load id `crowded:<chapter>:<intent>` (`crowdedRef`); the ledger maps it to its collection (`load:intent:<id>`). A payment ref `crowded:<chapter>:<payment>` is accepted once a read has seen that payment (`load:payment:<id>`); an answer carries the id it was asked by, and `metadata.paymentLoadId` names the payment form. Unknown, foreign or malformed ids are `UNKNOWN_LOAD` with no request.
- `getLoad`: the standing payment is the latest Succeeded, else the latest Pending or Processing, else the latest; the table of the card applies. A bank transfer Succeeded is `on_hold` with `holdUntil = addBusinessDays(success instant, achHoldDays)`; a Succeeded payment whose net (amount, less the fee when `feeCovered`) is not the load amount is `processing`; the fee and `feeCovered` are on `metadata` (`CrowdedLoad`, a `Load` plus metadata), never in `amount`. A payment method other than Card or Ach is never read as paid. No payment: `requires_action` with the page (and `expiresAt` when set); an intent `Returned` or `Cancelled` with no payment is failed.
- No personal data at rest: the ledger holds vendor ids and HMAC-SHA256 values (key binding over customer, amount, payer address and urls); messages are fixed text and carry no address, url or token; nothing logs.
- `fake-crowded.ts`: intents carry `contactId`, the redirect urls as sent, the payer IP and a payment list; `GET .../intents/{id}` with `payments`; controls `payIntent(ref, 'card' | 'ach', { amount? })`, `process`, `settle`, `fail`, `returnPayment`, `cancel`, `refundCard`, `intentRequest`, `failIntents`, `paymentUrlBase`, and `count('intents')`. The legacy `pay(intentRef)` and the intent upsert by email are unchanged.
- `funding.contract.test.ts`: the port's `fundingContract` with `hosted: true` runs in full, but the adapter is hosted only, so its card, Apple Pay, Google Pay, ACH, webhook and refund cases are served by the port's `FundingSimulator` behind a small router in the test (`HostedPlusSim`); every hosted case and every `crowded:` id goes to the Crowded adapter. Plus 38 Crowded cases (one collection and intent per key, claims, 5xx, the status table, hold across a weekend, fee rule, allowlist and normalisation, `expiresAt`, unknown loads, no personal data, constructor config).
- No shared file touched, no flag, route, registry or OpenAPI change; `packages/ports` is untouched.
