As built:
- `infra/terraform/modules/alarms` (variables.tf, main.tf, outputs.tf, versions.tf), wired into `envs/staging/main.tf` and `envs/prod/main.tf` as `module.alarms`. Provider pin `~> 5.80` as every module. Two SNS topics, `<env>-pages` and `<env>-tickets`, encrypted with their own CMK (module kms; the key policy lets cloudwatch, events, budgets and sns service principals encrypt, and the topic policy lets the first three publish with `aws:SourceAccount`), never `alias/aws/sns`. ONE subscription, on pages, protocol https, endpoint `var.pager_endpoint`: a required sensitive variable with no default in the module and in both roots (declared in each `main.tf`, because the roots' `variables.tf` are not on the card; CI or an operator passes `-var pager_endpoint=...` / `TF_VAR_pager_endpoint`). The tickets topic has no subscriber: the team picks its tracker.
- Metrics match `packages/domain/src/metrics.ts` exactly (namespace `Kesher`): `jit.decision_ms` p99 and `jit.error` average, no dimensions; `webhook.lag_s{market}`; `transfer.stuck{state,market}` with per-state thresholds (created 300 s, submitted 600 s, pending 3600 s, pending_hold 10800 s, above its normal 2 hours); `job.failure{job}`; `job.age_s{job}`; `recon.break_open_usd` and `recon.break_oldest_h` (no dimensions); `provider.error{port}`. A job table in the module lists all 24 worker jobs with cycle and money flag; the contract test fails when it differs from `apps/worker/src/jobs.ts`. `job.age_s` alarms use `treat_missing_data = "breaching"` (periods 300 s, 900 s, 3600 s, 86400 s by cycle; threshold 3 cycles, 25 h for a daily job), so a dead worker pages. Money jobs page on failure and staleness, the rest open tickets.
- Infrastructure: ALB target 5xx and unhealthy-host alarm per ingress service (api and jit per market, admin), ECS desired-minus-running (Container Insights metrics, metric math) per service including workers, RDS CPU, free storage, connections and replica lag (only when a replica exists), ACM `DaysToExpiry` under 14, an EventBridge rule on AWS Backup and copy job FAILED, EXPIRED or ABORTED to the pages topic, an AWS Budget (monthly, USD, actual spend) with 50, 80 and 100 percent notices to tickets (staging default 1000, prod 10000: placeholders, the owner sets real figures), and two dashboards, `<env>-money-path` and `<env>-infrastructure`.
- RUN to alarm, per row of the alert table: JIT latency RUN-02 `jit_latency`; JIT errors RUN-02 `jit_errors`; Webhook lag RUN-05 `webhook_lag` per market; Reconciliation break RUN-03 `recon_break_amount` and `recon_break_age`; Workflow failure RUN-01 or RUN-03 `workflow_failure` per money job and `transfer_stuck` per state and market; Temporal backlog RUN-05 `temporal_backlog`; RDS CPU RUN-02 `rds_cpu`; Certificate expiry RUN-07 `cert_expiry`; Port contract check RUN-01 `port_errors` per port; US banking provider RUN-09 `us_banking_errors` and the `job_stale` alarm of `us-banking-recon`. Also `job_stale` (RUN-05), `alb_*` and `ecs_tasks` (the RUN passed per service), `rds_*` (RUN-02), the backup rule (RUN-08).
- Tests: `infra/terraform/tests/alarms.tftest.hcl` (apply against a mocked provider: each row by alert name and RUN, missing data breaching, exact dimension sets, ALB suffixes, CMK topics, one pager subscription, budget 50/80/100, backup rule, dashboards, no replica no lag alarm); `apps/api/src/alarms-contract.test.ts` parses the table and fails when a row has no alarm naming its RUN, when a worker job has no stale alarm, or when an alarm's metric or dimension set differs from `METRIC_DEFS`. `markets.tftest.hcl` gained the `pager_endpoint` variable and overrides `module.alarms` (its own test covers it).
- Gaps, stated plainly: (1) Temporal backlog: nothing emits it and Temporal Cloud has no CloudWatch metric; the alarm listens for `Kesher/Temporal` `task_queue_backlog`, and missing data does not breach, so it is silent until a forwarder publishes it. (2) Port contract check: the nightly suite runs in CI and publishes no metric; the alarm is on `provider.error{port}` (only `card-issuing` emits today), a count of 5 in 5 minutes, not the suite's result. (3) US banking: `provider.error` has no call count, so 5 errors in 5 minutes stands for "over 5%"; the port name `institution-banking` is assumed and nothing emits it yet; webhook signature failures have no metric. (4) The `us-banking-recon` stale alarm stands for "unfinished at 06:00 IL" with a 24 hour window, not a 06:00 check. (5) Budget notices reach the tickets topic only; no email subscriber.
