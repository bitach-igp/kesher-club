The catalogue sync prices nothing and the club lists no add-on. Decided: admin price rules first (not a demo-only rule).
- `GET /admin/price-rules` lists every rule with its state (proposed, active, rejected) for staff.
- `POST /admin/price-rules` is for Kesher finance: kind (esim, insurance, concierge), provider, a vendor-SKU pattern, a margin in basis points (0 to 20,000), a floor price, rounding to .99, a priority and a reason. The proposer is the first approver; the rule is inactive until a second, different finance member approves it (`POST /admin/price-rules/{id}/approve`), or either rejects it with a reason (`/reject`). Activation is audited.
- The next catalogue sync prices with the active rules; nothing reprices on approval.

The tests cover:
- support gets 403 proposing; a proposal is inactive and prices nothing;
- the proposer cannot approve; a second finance member activates it, audited, and the rules the sync reads then price the vendor's plans ($9.50 cost at 2,500 bps is $11.88, rounded up to $11.99);
- a bad pattern, margin, floor, kind or missing reason answers 422; a rejected rule never activates.
