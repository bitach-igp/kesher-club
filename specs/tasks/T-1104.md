# T-T-1104 [PAY] Invite someone to receive: a send to a phone number with no account starts an invite and moves no money until they have joined

Test first: Today a phone that is not a verified member answers an indistinguishable 404 (T-0530) so nobody can probe who is a member, and the sender is stuck. Test first: the sender explicitly chooses 'invite them' (the 404 stays the default, so probing stays closed); the invite records the sender, a hash of the phone, an amount hint and the category the sender names, expires in 14 days (like REQ-PAY-004) and sends one text through the notify port (a rate limit per sender per day); NO money moves, no hold and no journal exist until the invitee has joined and passed T1, then the sender is told and the send is the ordinary send (limits, new-pair hold, idempotency unchanged); an invite to an existing member behaves as a normal send. This avoids holding customer money for someone with no account, which ADR-018 says we may not do. The phone is stored hashed and sealed, never logged.

Crowded cross-reference (2026-11-25, docs only, no vendor call): Crowded has an email-invite route for a sub-account owner, but it creates a treasurer with full account access and the owner self-onboards; it does not hold money for a person who has not joined, and Crowded banks entities only. The 'no money moves until joined' rule here stands. A hosted-page payment needs a contact with a mobile number and an intent without one gets a placeholder mobile that cannot receive SMS (api-inventory section 7).
