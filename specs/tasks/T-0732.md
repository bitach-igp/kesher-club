# T-0732 [LED] The same-day coverage proof: every journal that touches a mirrored account has its legs settled at Crowded by the cutoff, or the run opens a case
REQ: REQ-LED-004 (after T-0708, T-0719, T-0721, T-0722) · Sprint: later (ADR-018 decision of 2026-11-11, proposed)
Files: apps/worker/src/recon/mirror-coverage.ts (new), apps/worker/src/recon/mirror-coverage.test.ts (new), apps/worker/src/recon/index.ts, apps/worker/src/jobs.ts, apps/worker/src/jobs.test.ts, apps/api/src/admin/cases.ts (adds `mirror_missing` and `mirror_unmatched`), apps/api/src/admin/cases.test.ts, specs/tasks/T-0732.md, specs/tasks/README.md
Source: ADR-018, M6 (coverage) and M9 (cut-over). T-0708 proves balances per member and in aggregate; this card proves the movement behind each balance. For the statement day it lists every `ledger.journal` touching a mirrored member's USD `wallet` or `pending`, classifies each with the planner's `classify` (T-0721), and matches the resulting legs to the queue rows (`ops.mirror_item`) and to Crowded's book transactions (`MirrorPort.movements`, two rows per payment).
Assumption: M6.1 (coverage is a break after the cutoff), M2 (each book payment is two transaction rows), M4 (excluded non-USD is reported, not a break), M9 (a non-zero balance at the moment `wallet.mirror` first ran is refused, not mirrored).
Test first: TST-LED-004. A clean day (a load, a P2P, a fee, a clearing, an allowance, a closure refund, all settled at the fake) opens no case; per case kind, keyed `recon:mirror:{kind}:{ref}:{runDate}` so a rerun opens none: a journal whose leg row is `pending` or `dead` after `MIRROR_CUTOFF_UTC` opens `mirror_missing` (journal id, seq, member, amount); a settled leg with no matching transaction at Crowded (by the idempotency key in the description, then by amount, account and time) opens `mirror_unmatched`; a Crowded book transaction on a member account that no leg of ours explains opens `mirror_unmatched` with `source: crowded`; a journal classified `excluded_non_usd` is NOT a break and is counted per currency in the report (the ILS gap shown daily); a journal classified `internal` or `vendor_first` is matched to nothing and is not a break; a journal for a member with no Crowded account (`unmirrored_member`) opens `mirror_missing`; the first run with `wallet.mirror` on and a non-zero USD wallet or pending balance for any member with no opening step opens one `mirror_cutover` case and writes nothing else (M9); a Crowded outage fails the run with nothing partial (`job_failed`, retried next tick); the run finishes before 06:00 IL or the existing deadline case opens (REQ-LED-004); reads are paged and bounded, with a ceiling and a case when the member count exceeds it; all amounts `bigint`; the report carries counts and cents only.
- Runs after T-0708 in the nightly sequence and shares its Crowded reads (the accounts list and the transactions listing are read once).
Done when: listed tests pass; `pnpm typecheck`, `pnpm lint`, `pnpm test`, `pnpm trace` green; only listed files touched
Out of scope: fixing a break (a person, or the P2P repair of T-0723); per-member balance equality and the aggregate identity (T-0708); an opening-balance step; the institution accounts' reconciliation (T-0654).

Note (from T-0735): `MirrorPort.payments()` and `readBack` can throw `PROVIDER_UNAVAILABLE` once a chapter's book payments pass `READBACK_MAX_PAGES` (about 2000 payments), because the early stop at `since` is gone. The coverage proof and any repair job built on them must treat that throw as retryable and alertable, never as a terminal verdict on the journal or a reason to open a coverage case.
