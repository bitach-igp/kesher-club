Note (T-0752 review): a sub-account whose owner failed has a `funding_subaccount_owner` ref (`o:{partyId}:failed`) in `ops.external_ref`. The credit and webhook path must refuse, or open a case for, such a sub-account rather than credit it blindly.

Built: `subAccountChapters(db, provider)` in `crowded.ts` answers `isMemberChapter` from `funding_subaccount` refs (`s:<chapter>` or `s:<chapter>:<account>`, matched on the chapter boundary with no LIKE) and `ownerFailed`; `app.ts` wires both. The answer is cached per chapter for 10 s (`CHAPTER_CACHE_MS`), since replay hands `process` one event at a time and there is no batch boundary to cache over. A sub-account whose owner failed is not credited: the funding handler is skipped, the T-0752 case (`subaccount-owner:{party}`) is opened if it is missing, and the sweep through `getLoad` still sees the load. The refs passed to funding are built from the event's own chapter, so a member's event cannot name another member's load.
