## As built
- **Job** `us-banking-recon` (`apps/worker/src/recon/us-banking.ts`, class `UsBankingRecon`), scope `market`, nightly at 03:00 Israel time (`NIGHTLY` in `jobs.ts`, run recorded per market), behind
  `institutions.usBanking`. It reads the Israel day before the run for each institution whose OWN US account the bank approved (a pooled account has none; a platform sub-account is read like an own one)
  and compares: provider payments with our payment pages and `org.fee_external_payment`; our rows recorded that day with the provider's list (read back a week, so an earlier payment recorded
  yesterday is not called missing); and every outbound debit with our US funding requests.
- **Breaks**, each one `recon_break` case (so the admin alerts list shows it) keyed by provider and reference and reason, `ON CONFLICT DO NOTHING`, so a rerun opens none: `payment_row_created` (the hint
  missed a payment of one of our pages: it is recorded through the T-0652 insert, `UsCollections.applyPayment`, and noted), `payment_without_page` (a payment on a page we never made),
  `row_without_payment`, `amount_mismatch`, `outbound_without_request`. Breaks are never corrected beyond recording the missed payment. Credits (money in) are not compared: the Kesher-side deposit
  reconciliation (T-0618) owns those.
- **Outage:** a `PROVIDER_UNAVAILABLE` is retried with 1 s and 2 s back-off (three tries), then ONE case per institution per day (`provider_outage`); the other institutions still run. A defect
  (not a provider error) lets the others finish, then fails the run, which the daily scheduler retries.
- **Deadline:** a run that finishes after 06:00 Israel time opens one `deadline_missed` case for the day (RUN-03). The report carries `withinDeadline`.
- **Matching a debit** to a funding request is by our provider reference, the payment's reference or the request's KC- reference appearing in the bank's description: provisional until Crowded
  answers how a transaction names an ACH (docs/vendors/crowded/README.md). Anything else the bank reports going out is reported, as the card asks, which includes withdrawals made in the bank's own app.
- **Files outside the card's list:** `apps/worker/src/jobs.ts` (the registration and `NIGHTLY`), `jobs.test.ts` and `main.test.ts` (the job count, schedule and run log), and
  `apps/api/src/institutions/us-collections.ts` (`applyPayment`, a one-line public wrapper). `recon/recon.ts` and `scheduler.ts` are unchanged: the card lists them, but the partner-statement
  reconciliation stays excluded and `daily()` already does what this job needs.
