## As-built
- The card said the funding port already lists settled charges and payouts. It did not: `FundingPort` had create/get/refund/webhook only. The listing was added as allowed, minimal: `FundingStatementPort.listSettled({ from, to, cursor?, limit? })` returning `{ entries, nextCursor? }` of `SettledEntry { kind: 'charge' | 'refund', id, loadId, amount (positive), settledAt }`, `from` inclusive, `to` exclusive. It is a separate interface, not a new method on `FundingPort`, so the existing `FundingPort` test doubles (`load-workflow.test.ts`, `loads.test.ts`) keep compiling and no money-moving caller has to implement it. The simulator and `StripeFundingAdapter` implement both; `fundingStatementContract` is the shared suite and both pass it.
- Payouts are not on this statement: the funding vendor has none (payouts are `PayoutPort`), so charges and refunds only. A payout partner source is separate work.
- Stripe reads `/v1/balance_transactions` (the settlement record) with `created[gte]`/`created[lt]`, `limit`, `starting_after` and `expand[]=data.source`, so an ACH debit lands on the day it cleared, not the day it was created. Only charge/payment and refund/payment_refund rows are kept; fees and payouts are skipped. A charge or refund with no PaymentIntent or an unsupported currency fails secure with PROVIDER_UNAVAILABLE rather than being dropped (a dropped row would hide a break). The fake Stripe gained the endpoint and records a balance transaction when an intent or refund succeeds. The real endpoint has not been called.
- `FundingStatementSource({ provider, port, pageSize? })`: charge `externalId` is the loadId and refund `externalId` the refundId (what `ops.external_ref` records in `loads.ts` and `closure.ts`); a refund is negative; `type` is `charge` or `refund`. Pages are followed to the end; any failure throws and nothing partial is returned; a repeated row or a cursor that never ends is PROVIDER_UNAVAILABLE. A provider other than the one it was built for, or a date that is not a calendar day, is VALIDATION_FAILED, never an empty list. `businessDayWindow(runDate)` is local midnight to local midnight in Asia/Jerusalem (23 and 25 hour days on the DST changes tested).
- Not wired: nothing constructs a `FundingStatementSource` yet (T-0684 schedules it). `ReconJob` still matches on `ops.external_ref` entity_type `journal`, while loads record `transfer` and `refund` refs; T-0684 has to settle how the two meet.
