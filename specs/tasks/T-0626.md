Test first: TST-INS-002 (activation rows). Found while slicing REC-14 (T-0922). `InviteService` (T-0602) has `accept` (invited → verified) and `activate` (verified → active), but no route reaches either, and nothing calls `activate`, so nobody on a roster ever becomes active and no allowance can pay anyone.
- `GET /invites/{token}` is public. It shows the institution's name, the roster name and whether the invite is open, expired or accepted. An unknown token answers 404.
- `POST /invites/{token}/accept` takes the member's bearer. The adult student accepts for themself: their party becomes the roster member's. Linking a funder stays with memberships (the seed's other open gap).
- An accepted member is active at once if they have approved KYC at T1 or above, which is what receiving money already needs; otherwise they become active when that approval lands.
- M04 (the app's invite screen) calls the real endpoints instead of the unbound extra.
- The demo seed has three personas accept the yeshiva's roster invites, so its roster has active members.

The tests cover:
- a T1 member's accept makes them active;
- a member without KYC is verified until their T1 approval, then active;
- an expired, used or unknown token answers 404;
- the funnel counts still add up.
