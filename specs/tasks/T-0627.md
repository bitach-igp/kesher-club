Test first: TST-INS-003 (schedule rows). T-0609 left creating schedules to "a later card"; until now, nothing creates `org.allowance_schedule`.
- `POST /institutions/{id}/allowance-schedules` is for the institution's finance staff, with an Idempotency-Key. It takes a name, an amount (bigint minor units and a currency), a cadence (weekly, monthly or once) and the cohort: every active member, or a chosen list of active members.
- I07 gains "New schedule". It checks the amount before the call and shows the next date it would run.

The tests cover:
- staff (not finance) get 403;
- another institution's finance gets 404;
- an amount of 0, or more than ₪10,000.00 a member, answers 422;
- a cohort naming a member who is not active answers 422;
- a retried key answers the same schedule;
- the new schedule lists with its next date.

## As built
- `org.allowance_schedule` had no place for an idempotency key, so migration org/0006 adds
  `org.allowance_schedule_request`: one row per key, naming the schedule it made and the body's fingerprint
  (name, amount, currency, cadence, sorted member ids). A retry answers the same schedule (201 both times); the
  key with a different body answers 409. A new table rather than a column, so the existing one takes no new
  index. The real Postgres apply is requested in `handoff/desktop/T-0627-atlas-apply.md`.
- The amount is in the institution's currency (IL → ILS, US → USD), above zero and at most 10,000.00 a member.
  A listed cohort names roster member ids (what accepting an invite returns); the schedule stores their parties,
  as the runs already read it. A refused cohort lists the ids in `notActive`.
- Staff who are not finance get 403 whatever the institution; another institution's finance gets 404; the
  call sits behind `institutions.money` like the runs.
- I07's form makes "every active member" schedules only: the console has no member list to choose from, and the
  API's listed cohort waits for one. It checks the amount before the call and shows the first run (today, as the
  list shows a schedule that has not run) and the one after. Verified in Chromium against the T-0031 stack:
  12,000 refused before the call, 250.00 monthly created and listed for the 3 seeded students, Hebrew copy shown.
- The demo's "Allowance schedule: no endpoint creates it" gap is gone; REC-14 makes the schedule in I07 on camera.
