Test first: every flow in `docs/bank-review/money-flows.md` starts from someone who already exists: a US member and an IL member with both purses, a funder linked to a member, a pair who have never paid each other (REC-06), an institution with a roster, a finance requester and a second approver (REC-14), a member with an open card (REC-12) and one ready to close (REC-17). `seed.ts` creates them through the running API (`@kesher/sdk` client, sign-up with the simulator OTP `SIM_OTP_CODE`, KYC decided through the panel hook `kyc/decide`) and through the panel for vendor state, never by writing tables. Every call carries an idempotency key derived from the persona name, so a second run creates nothing and prints the same table. Names, phones and emails are obviously fictional (`+1 555 01xx`, `@example.test`). `seed.test.ts` runs it twice against the T-0031 process on PGlite: the second run makes no new rows in `ops.transfer` or `ledger.journal`, and each persona can sign in.
Done when: listed tests pass; `pnpm typecheck`, `pnpm lint`, `pnpm test`, `pnpm trace` green; `docs/setup/demo.md` lists each persona, its market and which REC uses it; only listed files touched
Out of scope: new endpoints (a persona the contract cannot make yet is listed in the doc as missing, not created through SQL). Stop and ask on conflict with the spec.
As built: `personas.ts` holds the eight personas, the four the contract cannot make yet, and `SeededPasskey`. `SeededPasskey` is an ES256 key derived from the persona key, in the simulator's wire format. Its signature counter follows the wall clock, so a persona signs in from any run or process. A persona that already exists is signed in, not made again. KYC goes through the panel's `kyc/decide` hook, then the vendor's webhook on `/v1/hooks/kyc`, because the hook alone changes only the simulator. Not seeded, and listed in `docs/setup/demo.md`:
- the funder link (no plan exists for `POST /memberships`);
- the institution, its finance staff and its allowance schedule (no endpoint).

Contract gap found: the server reads `deviceId` on `POST /auth/passkeys/register`, but `PasskeyRegisterRequest` does not declare it.
