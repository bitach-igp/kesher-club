## As built
- **Route** (flag `institutions.usBanking`; finance staff and admins): `POST /institutions/{id}/fee-batches/{batchId}/us-collection`, Idempotency-Key, no step-up (no money moves from Kesher),
  body `{ funders: [{ feeItemId, name, email, mobile }] }` (1 to 200). The contact is the card's "funder name, email and mobile": fee items carry none, so the institution supplies it
  per call, it goes to the provider and is stored nowhere. Answers `{ batchId, items: [{ feeItemId, status: collecting|paid, paymentUrl }] }`.
- **Provider objects.** One collection per batch (key `us-fee-batch:<batch>`, titled with the batch id's tail so a provider that reuses a collection by name keeps two batches apart) and
  one payment page per item (`us-fee-item:<item>`); their ids live in `ops.external_ref` (`fee_batch_us_collection`, `fee_item_us_intent`). USD batches only; the account must be approved and
  open in KYB; a fee that is not due is 409.
- **Paid** is `org.fee_external_payment` (insert-only, unique on the provider's payment ref per kind) written by `UsCollections.reconcileEntity`, which re-reads the entity's payments through the
  port. The T-0649 `collected` hint wires to it (`app.ts`, last 7 days); T-0654's nightly sweep calls the same method. The fee item's `status` goes `due`→`paid` in the same transaction.
  A payment of another amount, one for a fee the purse already paid, and a refund each open one `us_fee_collection` case (keyed by payment ref and reason) and do not mark the fee paid
  (a refund appends its row and leaves the fee as it was: seen refunded first, the fee stays due; a person decides). A payment on a waived fee is recorded and opens a case. Declined payments are ignored. Payments for pages we did not make are left to T-0654.
- **Beyond the card's file list:** `apps/api/src/institutions/collections.ts` (the purse refuses a fee collected on a payment page: 409), `db/migrations/org/0013_fee_external_payment.sql`
  (the card names 0012, taken by T-0653's `us_funding`) and `atlas.sum`, `apps/api/src/institutions/us-funding.ts` (exports `asDomain`). `fee-billing.ts` is unchanged: billing stays as it was.
  Shared plumbing: `app.ts`, `routes.ts`, `contracts/openapi.yaml`, the SDK and `HEADER_OPS`. Desktop handoff: `handoff/desktop/T-0652-fee-external-payment.md`.
- **Not done.** The parent-portal view of the link, as the card says.
- **Review hardening.** The marker (`fee_us_collect`) is written under a lock on the fee items, with a purse-journal check, BEFORE the provider is called, so a failed call leaves the marker and a repeat finishes
  the job. The purse's `pay()` check and a funder's payment can still cross in a window of one request; the journal check in `reconcile` (under the item lock) opens a `paid_twice` case when it sees both.
  The `collected` hint acts only for an entity we hold (an institution's own, or the platform's), resolves the port from that institution's market, filters intents by provider, and re-reads from the oldest
  collected page so a late refund is found; an entity the provider does not answer for is ignored. A repeat with a corrected contact is refused by the provider (a page's key is the item): the contact of a
  page cannot be changed. An unapproved link answers 422 (`VALIDATION_FAILED`) as T-0653 does, not the 409 the card names.
