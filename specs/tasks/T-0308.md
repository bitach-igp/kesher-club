## As built
- **Entry.** Home gains a card, "Pay for someone's membership", for any signed-in adult; it opens `/funder/plans`.
- **P01–P03 (`app/funder/plans.tsx`).** The plans offered in the chosen market (US first: funders are mostly in the
  US), each with its price a month, its price for the term and what it includes (eSIM data, concierge calls; other
  entitlements are not put in words yet). Add a card opens the billing vendor's sheet through
  `POST /me/billing/setup-sheet`; the card never reaches the app. This build completes the simulator's sheets only
  (`simulatorCardSheet`); a live sheet binds with the live billing adapter (T-0301), as Add money's does. Continue
  goes to `/kyc` until T1 is approved, then asks for a plan and a card, then opens P04 with the plan.
- **P04 (`app/funder/invite.tsx`).** Name, by text message (the phone starts at +972) or email, Send; the sent invites
  with their state in words, and Send again or Cancel on an open one. A retry keeps its Idempotency-Key.
- **Plumbing outside the listed files.** `apps/mobile/src/api.ts` gains `FunderApi` (plans, setup sheet, invites)
  and its SDK binding.
