## As built
- `GET /plans?market=IL|US` (`listPlans`) picks per plan exactly what `PlanService.current()` would join a member on: the
  latest active version in force. A version made before T-0932 names no market, so its currency says which (ILS for IL,
  USD for US). The monthly price is the term's price over its months, rounded up to the minor unit.
- No staff fields reach a member: no proposer, reason or state.
- Listing charges nothing, so it is not behind `billing.stripe`; the invite that saves a payment method is (T-0306).
- `app.ts` builds one `PlanCatalogue` and exposes it as `services.planCatalogue` (the admin handlers and tests share it).
