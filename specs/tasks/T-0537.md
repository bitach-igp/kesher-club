## As built
- `GET /households/{id}/members/{partyId}` (`getHouseholdMember`): only a member the caller funds through an active,
  past-due or paused membership, in the caller's own household; any other answers 404 alike (THR-09).
- **Transactions:** the latest 50 across both purses, T-0536's rows (`TransactionService.forAccount`). Those rows carry
  no names, notes or counterparties at all, so a P2P reads as a P2P, out or in, and nothing more.
- **Spend by category:** this calendar month (UTC), card authorisations held or cleared and not released, grouped by
  category from the merchant code (`categoryOf`: food and drink, groceries, transport, shopping, travel,
  entertainment, other) per currency, largest first.
- **Cards:** id, type, status, last4 and the controls the funder set, so P09 can open one through the existing
  `/cards/{id}` paths, which already let the funder in. The test covers the empty list; card issue has its own tests.
