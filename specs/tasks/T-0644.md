## As built
- `/team` (nav "Team", for every staff role) shows one table of people: open invites (state "Invited", with the
  channel and the expiry day), then staff active (by role) and removed. A person shows as the last four digits of
  their party id; names are data, never copy.
- **Non-admins read.** Staff and finance see the list and the pending grants, without any control.
- **Invite (admins).** Role select with email and phone fields; typing in one clears the other. The draft needs a
  role and exactly one address, checked before the call; the invite POST keeps its Idempotency-Key for a retry of
  the same draft.
- **Change role (admins).** A select and "Save role" per active staff row. The call keeps its key per person and
  role; a finance or admin answer reads "Waiting for a second admin." and the raise appears in the pending list;
  staff applies at once. Nobody raises their own role: their own row says "This is you".
- **Remove (admins).** The last active admin shows "The last admin cannot be removed." instead of the button.
  Otherwise Remove arms a question ("Remove {who} from the staff? They lose access at once."); only a deliberate
  confirm acts, and the removal keeps its key for a retry. A cancel lets go.
- **Pending grants.** Each reads "{who} is being raised to {role}." with who proposed it, and "Waiting for a second
  admin." Approve is offered with a passkey step-up to an admin who is neither the person raised nor the proposer —
  read from their access token's sub claim, unverified, as the API still enforces every rule.
- **403 read as a sentence**: "Only admins of this institution manage the team.", mirrored in Hebrew; other
  refusals read as sentences too. Copy is in English and Hebrew.
