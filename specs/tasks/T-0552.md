# T-0552 [LED] Reversals carry the transfer and the original effective time
REQ: REQ-LED-001, REQ-LED-004 · Sprint: fix (low, follow-up of T-0546 review)
Files: apps/api/src/ledger/post.ts, apps/api/src/ledger/post.test.ts, specs/tasks/T-0552.md, specs/tasks/README.md
Source: Opus review of PR #201: `ledger.reverse` passes neither `transferId` nor `effectiveAt`, so unreserve and other reversal journals carry post-time defaults and do not appear with their transfer (affects T-0540's unreserve too).
Test first: reverse a journal that belongs to a transfer: the reversal has no `transfer_id` and an `effective_at` of the time it was posted, not the original's.
- `reverse` copies the original journal's `transferId`; `effectiveAt` is the caller's when given, else the original's.
- The ledger stays insert-only; the reversal key and idempotency are unchanged.
**Tests.** the reversal carries the original's transfer id; effective time follows the rule; replay is idempotent; the books balance.
Done when: listed tests pass; `pnpm typecheck`, `pnpm lint`, `pnpm test`, `pnpm trace` green; only listed files touched
Out of scope: folding the FX reserve out of the wallet history (`transactions.ts`, own card if wanted). Stop and ask on conflict with the spec.

## As-built
- `PostgresLedger.reverse(journalId, key, opts?)` now posts the reversal with the original's `transferId` and, unless `opts.effectiveAt` is given, the original's effective time. `PostedJournal` gains an optional `transferId` (read from `ledger.journal.transfer_id`). The ledger stays insert-only; the reversal key, idempotency and the balance check are unchanged.
- Existing callers pass no options, so their reversals (unreserve, hold/fail reversals) now carry the transfer and the original's time. No caller passes `effectiveAt` yet.
- Test (`post.test.ts`): a reversal carries the original's transfer id and effective time; a caller's own time wins; a replay answers the same reversal; a journal with no transfer reverses with none; the books still balance. The full suite is green with the change.
