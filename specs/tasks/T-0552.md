# T-0552 [LED] Reversals carry the transfer they undo

Source: Opus review of PR #201: `ledger.reverse` passes neither `transferId` nor `effectiveAt`, so unreserve and other reversal journals carry post-time defaults and do not appear with their transfer (affects T-0540's unreserve too).
Test first: reverse a journal that belongs to a transfer: the reversal has no `transfer_id`.
- `reverse` copies the original journal's `transferId`; `effectiveAt` is the caller's when given, else the posting time (a reversal is not backdated into a month already reported: statements, exports and history group by effective time).
- The ledger stays insert-only; the reversal key and idempotency are unchanged.
**Tests.** the reversal carries the original's transfer id; effective time is the posting time or the caller's; replay is idempotent; the books balance.

## As-built
- `PostgresLedger.reverse(journalId, key, opts?)` posts the reversal with the original's `transferId` (so it shows with its transfer). `PostedJournal` gains an optional `transferId`. The ledger stays insert-only; the reversal key, idempotency and the balance check are unchanged.
- **Deviation from the card's first draft:** the original's effective time is NOT the default. Backdating a reversal to the original's month would rewrite a statement, export or history page already sent (`institutions/statement.ts`, `institutions/export.ts`, `wallet/transactions.ts` group and page on `effective_at`); the correction belongs to the month it is made in. `opts.effectiveAt` lets a caller choose; none does yet. The card text above was changed to match.
- Existing callers (unreserve, hold and fail reversals) now carry the transfer id and are dated as before.
- Test (`post.test.ts`): the reversal carries the transfer id and is dated at posting, not at the original's time; a caller's time wins; a replay (also with a different time) returns the first reversal; a journal with no transfer reverses with none; the books balance.
