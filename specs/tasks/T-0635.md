  is unavailable right now", never zeros.

    Amounts stay bigint until the formatter.

  - Fees due are listed per currency.
  - An empty roster reads "No students yet. Import your roster to invite them."
- **Links.** The tile links open Allowances, Collections and Payouts, which every staff role reads. I04 roster and
  I10 account add their own navigation in their cards, so this landing never links to a page that does not exist
