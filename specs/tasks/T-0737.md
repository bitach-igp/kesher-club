Source: verified 2026-11-12 at 90fe882. `MembershipService` (`apps/api/src/billing/memberships.ts`) creates a vendor subscription through `BillingPort` and keeps `billing.membership` rows with `status`, `dunning_attempts`, `current_period_end`, `price_minor`, `cancel_at`; the vendor's webhooks (`invoice.paid`, `invoice.payment_failed`, `subscription.updated`) drive the status. Nothing in it can charge the ledger. Entitlements count only while `status = 'active'` (`billing/entitlements.ts`).
How this fixes the T-0043 guard for good: with fees charged from the wallet no Stripe flag is ever turned on, so the three `billing.*` flags stay off (T-0736) and the guard never fires. The new switch is a NEW flag, `billing.wallet` (default false, in `config/flags.json` like any flag; it is not in `PORT_FLAGS` because no vendor port backs it). A `BillingPort` adapter that charges the ledger (so the `billing` port stops being a simulator and the Stripe flags could stay on) is the alternative F5(b) and is not recommended: the port is a vendor-held subscription model with webhooks, and an adapter inside `packages/adapters` cannot post a ledger journal.
OWNER DECISIONS (marked; recommendation first):
- F1. Whose wallet pays. The owner's words say the member's. The existing model has a FUNDER (parent) pay (`funder_party_id`). RECOMMENDED: the member's wallet when the member is the payer, the funder's wallet when a funder pays; a payer with no wallet cannot use this path.
- F2. When the waiver ends: a date, a trigger (wallet balances live), or per plan version; whether members who joined free keep a free term (grandfathering).
- F3. Price and purse: the plan version's USD price against the USD purse only (RECOMMENDED while Crowded is USD only, ADR-018 M4), or the ILS purse with FX.
- F4. Insufficient balance: RECOMMENDED a grace of N days then `past_due`, then `paused` (entitlements off, which `can()` already does), the money loaded stays the member's; N and the notification copy are the owner's.
- F5. Shape: (a) RECOMMENDED a ledger-native charge workflow beside `BillingPort` (a worker job and a table); (b) a `BillingPort` adapter backed by the ledger.
- F6. Mirror: the fee is a wallet movement (member wallet to `fee_income`). With `wallet.mirror` on (D1) it is one `to_checking` leg classified in the T-0721 planner, so this card waits for T-0716 to T-0722; with the mirror off the Crowded wallet balance would drift above the ledger balance by the fees, which D1 and M6 forbid for a mirrored member.
- F7. A requirement row (a new row, numbered by the skill, "a member's plan fee is charged from the wallet at most once per period") via `club-requirements`, plus the cancel rule of REQ-BIL-004 (end of the paid period, money kept) in the new world.
