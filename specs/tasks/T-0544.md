## As-built
- `ledger/post.ts`: `postNoOverdraft(input, extraGuard?)` takes sorted per-wallet advisory locks, re-checks idempotency under the lock, refuses with `INSUFFICIENT_FUNDS` if a debited wallet would go negative, then runs `extraGuard(tx)`. Balance upserts now run in account-id order (fixes a real-Postgres deadlock between opposite sends).
- Used by P2P send and hold, card JIT hold and member payout reserve. `p2p/limits.ts` `recheckRisk` re-counts the monthly limit under the lock.
- Files outside the card list: `p2p/limits.ts`, `p2p/holds.ts`, `payouts/service.ts`, `handoff/desktop/T-0544-wallet-lock-race.md`.
- Real Postgres 16 run locally: `balance-lock.pg.test.ts` 5/5 (one of two, 10 of 30, same key, opposite sends, p99). Desktop handoff still requested.
- Review round 1: the hold path now also runs the monthly-limit recheck (passed in from `P2pSendService.hold`); a JIT test drains the wallet between the early check and the hold and expects `INSUFFICIENT_FUNDS` with nothing held. `withWalletLock` was not created as a separate file: the lock lives in `postNoOverdraft`. The latency test is serial, not the 300 ms p99 under concurrent load (still to measure).
