## As built
- **Routes** (flag `institutions.usBanking`; finance staff and admins of the institution): `POST /institutions/{id}/us-funding/quote`, `POST /institutions/{id}/us-funding`
  (step-up, Idempotency-Key, `{ quoteId }`), `GET /institutions/{id}/us-funding`, `POST …/{requestId}/approve` and `…/cancel` (step-up). Cancel is beyond the
  card's list; the card asks for "provider cancel while cancellable", and the console (T-0651) needs the list.
- **Quote.** `org.us_funding_quote` (migration `org/0012`): the amount, the bank's fee (and arrival date where it gives one; the platform paths cost nothing), valid
  ten minutes. A request *spends* the quote (`quote_id` is unique), so a second approver who is slow cannot fail on expiry; an expired quote is 409 `QUOTE_EXPIRED`,
  a spent one 409. The same key and quote answer the same request; the key with another quote is 409.
- **Approval.** A second finance user or admin of the institution; the person who asked is 403. It is this card's own rule (an `approved` event, one at a time under a
  row lock), not an `ApprovalService` kind: a new kind needs a ledger migration and its own role rules, and the admin console's queue does not need to see these.
  KYB (`us_banking`) and the linked account are checked before the approval is written.
- **Three paths on approval,** by how the account is held (T-0650): the institution's own entity sends one ACH to Kesher's collection counterparty
  (`usCollectionCounterpartyRef`) with the KC- reference as `addenda` and the provider key `us-fund:<institution>:<key>`; a platform sub-account **books a transfer** to
  `usCollectionAccountRef` (memo = the reference); a pooled account moves nothing. A book transfer or a pooled request has no bank credit to wait for, so the card
  **records a deposit** for it (`InstitutionDeposits.recordSystem`, pending, recorded by the system) and the deposit path's approval by Kesher finance posts it.
  Both options are `AppOptions` (set up by ops); without them the request is refused when asked. Nothing is posted by sending.
- **Linking.** The deposit path now tells this card when a deposit posts (`InstitutionDeposits.afterPosted`). A recorded deposit is found by its id; an ACH that
  arrived (the bank feed ties the credit to the institution by its KC- reference) is matched only among requests the bank has sent (`sent`/`settled`), and only when exactly one has that amount. The request is then `received`.
  A near miss is not guessed: an amount that matches nothing links nothing.
- **Status.** An insert-only event log (`org.us_funding_event`). Before the provider is called, `execute` takes a **claim** (`sending`, under a row lock), so the sweep,
  a second worker and a cancel take turns; a cancel after the claim is 409. The bank's ACH status arrives by the T-0649 `ach` handler (`onProviderAch`, mapped through
  `ops.external_ref`, type `institution_us_funding`) and by the sweep (`us-funding-sweep`, every 5 minutes, per market; one request's error never stops the rest).
  "Provider unavailable" leaves the claim to go stale (10 minutes) and the sweep sends again under the same key. An answer we cannot read, or a 409 from the bank
  (`OUTCOME_UNKNOWN`, a new port code), stops the request: `send_unknown`, status `needs_review`, a case, no resend; so does a request still unsent after 24 hours (the bank
  dedupes a key for about two days). A return or a rejection (also one the send itself answers) opens a case; a return after the deposit posted opens a separate case.
  A refusal is final (`failed`, with a case).
- **Not done.** "Tells the requester" is the status on the list and the case; a message needs a notification template, which is a follow-up. The real Crowded adapter
  refuses a transfer *out of* a sub-account (its book payments are checking ↔ contact accounts, see T-0648), so the book-transfer path works against the simulator
  and fails with a refusal (and a case) against Crowded until Crowded answers questions 2 and 12. `deposits.test.ts` is unchanged: the hook and `recordSystem` are
  tested through `us-funding.test.ts`. Unrelated gap: `institutionByReference` scans every institution (existing).
