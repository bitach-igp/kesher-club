# T-0699 [INS] The Crowded adapter creates a member's contact and wallet account, against the fake and the gated sandbox suite
REQ: REQ-INS-022 (after T-0698) · Sprint: later (ADR-018, proposed)
Files: packages/adapters/src/crowded/member-accounts.ts (new), packages/adapters/src/crowded/member-accounts.contract.test.ts (new), packages/adapters/src/crowded/fake-crowded.ts, packages/adapters/src/crowded/map.ts, packages/adapters/src/crowded/index.ts, packages/adapters/src/sandbox.contract.test.ts, specs/tasks/T-0699.md, specs/tasks/README.md
Source: ADR-018 and `docs/vendors/crowded/postman-2sBYArTsS6.md` ("Contacts", "Bulk Create Contacts", "Accounts", "Bulk create accounts"). Contact create has no idempotency key, so the adapter looks the contact up first (`GET /chapters/{id}/contacts?search=<mobile>`, matched on the exact E.164 mobile) before creating, as the other Crowded creates do. Account create is idempotent twice over: a `idempotencyKey` UUID and "a contact that already has the product returns success with `accountCreated: false`".
Assumption: A1 (contact plus `wallet` account in the platform chapter; a contact `externalId` is not in the documented fields, so the mapping is ours), A2 (the contact status `Active` or `Failed`, the account status `Open`, `Frozen` or `Closed` are the only KYC signals; a `Failed` contact is `rejected`).
Test first: TST-WAL-002 (the port's `fundingAccountContract`, run against the fake). Two calls with one party make one contact and one account at the fake (`count` shows 1 and 1); a contact the fake already holds under the same mobile (made by a lost response) is found, not created again; a duplicate mobile that belongs to another party in our records is `DUPLICATE_MOBILE` without a create; a bulk-create answer with `error: true, errorCode: PROVISION_FAILED` is `PROVIDER_UNAVAILABLE`, `CONTACT_NOT_FOUND` after a create is `PROVIDER_UNAVAILABLE`; a contact the fake marks `Failed` is `rejected`; a `Frozen` account is `frozen`; `account()` returns `balance`, `hold`, `available` as bigint cents and never returns the account or routing number (not even in an error); a 503 is `PROVIDER_UNAVAILABLE` and is not retried here.
- Gated sandbox: `sandbox.contract.test.ts` gains a Crowded member-accounts block, skipped unless `SANDBOX_CONTRACTS=1` and the Crowded sandbox variables are set (the T-0690 workflow runs it). It creates one contact with a unique mobile and one wallet account in the sandbox platform chapter, and asserts the second call is a no-op. It cannot test KYC outcomes or a payment (the sandbox has no collect payment simulator).
- It also implements `chapter()` (the chapter's `checking` account and the `hold` product account, unconfirmed) and `transactions()` (`GET /chapters/{id}/accounts/{id}/transactions`, with `collectPaymentId` kept).
- The fake gains `contacts` and `accounts` (bulk create, get, list with `product`, `balance`, `hold`, `available`), and the controls `failContact(mobile)`, `freezeAccount(id)`, `setBalance(id, cents)`.
- Staging: wallet accounts bill at Crowded; the sandbox allows few sub-accounts (README question 22). Whether a wallet account count is limited is an open question to Crowded, recorded in the ADR.
Done when: listed tests pass; `pnpm typecheck`, `pnpm lint`, `pnpm test`, `pnpm trace` green; only listed files touched
Out of scope: the funding adapter (T-0700); a wallet account for the platform's own funders who are not members (a contact only); archiving contacts; digital cards (`withDigitalCard` stays false); wiring it in the registry (T-0709).

## Carried from the T-0698 review
- Crowded contact ids and account ids are numeric and live in separate id spaces. For `account(contactRef)` to answer UNKNOWN_ACCOUNT, the adapter's opaque refs must be self-describing (a `c:` and an `a:` prefix, say), so a contact id is never looked up as an account id.
- The documented port contract does not require hashing a member's contact fields. The simulator keeps an unsalted hash in memory only; the adapter must not persist an unsalted hash of a mobile, email or name (a salted, keyed hash or nothing).
- Open question for the owner or a later card: a member whose contact was `rejected` (Failed at Crowded, for example a typo in the email) can never be re-ensured with corrected data, because a changed mobile, name or email is `PARTY_CONFLICT` and the explicit update call does not exist yet. Decide how a rejected member re-applies.
- Nits: DOB validation still accepts future dates and year 0001 (an unconfirmed assumption about Crowded).
