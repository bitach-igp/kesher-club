## Carried from the T-0698 review
- Crowded contact ids and account ids are numeric and live in separate id spaces. For `account(contactRef)` to answer UNKNOWN_ACCOUNT, the adapter's opaque refs must be self-describing (a `c:` and an `a:` prefix, say), so a contact id is never looked up as an account id.
- The documented port contract does not require hashing a member's contact fields. The simulator keeps an unsalted hash in memory only; the adapter must not persist an unsalted hash of a mobile, email or name (a salted, keyed hash or nothing).
- Open question for the owner or a later card: a member whose contact was `rejected` (Failed at Crowded, for example a typo in the email) can never be re-ensured with corrected data, because a changed mobile, name or email is `PARTY_CONFLICT` and the explicit update call does not exist yet. Decide how a rejected member re-applies.
- Nits: DOB validation still accepts future dates and year 0001 (an unconfirmed assumption about Crowded).

## Open questions
- **How does a rejected member re-apply?** A member whose contact is `rejected` (Failed at Crowded, for example a typo in the email) cannot be re-ensured with corrected data: a changed mobile, name or email is `PARTY_CONFLICT`, and there is no update call yet (a contact PATCH exists at Crowded, but the port has no verb for it). This card does not decide a policy and invents none; a test pins today's behaviour (`PARTY_CONFLICT`, still one contact). The options for the owner or a later card: an explicit `updateContact` on the port (PATCH, which Crowded may refuse for a Failed contact), archiving the Failed contact and re-creating (the mobile may stay claimed), or a support path outside the app.
- Does Crowded limit the number of wallet accounts (billing, the sandbox's few sub-accounts, README question 22)? Recorded in the ADR as an open question to Crowded.
- Does Crowded replay a failed per-item account result under the same body `idempotencyKey` (the key is `uuidFromKey` of the contact id, so a retry sends the same one)? Unknown; the fake does not cache a failure.

## As-built
- `member-accounts.ts`: `CrowdedMemberAccounts` implements `FundingAccountPort`. `ensureAccount` checks the key (bound to a keyed MAC of party and data: another input is `IDEMPOTENCY_CONFLICT`), the party (other data is `PARTY_CONFLICT`), then the mobile in our records (another party is `DUPLICATE_MOBILE`, no request sent), then looks the contact up by exact mobile at Crowded before it creates one, then reads the contact (Failed is `rejected`, Archived `archived`) and creates the `wallet` account if there is none, and reads the account (Open, Frozen, Closed). Each call reads the vendor's current status. No retries; a 429, 5xx, transport error, a per-item `error: true` or an unreadable answer is `PROVIDER_UNAVAILABLE` with fixed text. A vendor 400 or 422 on the contact create is `VALIDATION_FAILED`. The account is not polled: the fake and the documented bulk answer are synchronous, so a provisioning account is not handled (unconfirmed against the sandbox).
- Refs are self-describing: `c:<id>` contact, `a:<id>` wallet account, `t:<id>` transaction (`fundingRef` and `parseFundingRef` in `map.ts`; only `[A-Za-z0-9_-]` ids). `account('c:7')`, an unprefixed id or a malformed ref is `UNKNOWN_ACCOUNT` with no request. A non-wallet account is `UNKNOWN_ACCOUNT` too. The fake numbers contacts and wallet accounts separately, so contact 1 and account 1 coexist in the tests.
- No personal data is persisted and no unsalted hash of it: the `KeyLedger` keeps vendor ids and HMAC-SHA256 values under a required secret `hmacKey` (at least 32 characters, no default; the constructor throws without it). That covers the key binding, the party's data MAC and the mobile-to-party record (keyed by the MAC of the mobile). A test scans everything the ledger is given for the made-up mobile, email, names and date of birth and for their bare SHA-256 forms. The T-0698 simulator's in-memory unsalted hash is not copied. A persistent ledger is wired later, as for the other Crowded adapters (T-0650).
- A ledger lost entirely: a contact found by mobile is adopted whoever it was made for (Crowded holds no party), so `DUPLICATE_MOBILE` is then only as good as our records. Two concurrent first calls for one party are not serialised here (the caller's workflow id is the key and workflows are single-flight).
- Date of birth: the adapter also refuses a date after today and before 1900 (`VALIDATION_FAILED`); a sanity bound, Crowded's own range is unconfirmed (the T-0698 nit).
- `account()`: `balance`, `hold` as bigint cents, `available = balance - hold`. The account and routing numbers are never read into a result and no message carries any digits of them. `chapter()` returns the open `checking` account's balance only: the port dropped `held` (T-0698), so the `hold` product account is not read. `transactions()` uses `GET /chapters/{id}/accounts/{id}/transactions` with `filter[createdAt]` min and max, drops rows outside `from <= postedAt < to` (the vendor's max is inclusive), lists each row once, and keeps the vendor's `collectPaymentId` on a row that has one (`CrowdedFundingTransaction`, a subtype of the port's row: the port has no such field, so a caller that wants it narrows the type).
- `fake-crowded.ts`: contacts (bulk create, list with `search`, get) and wallet accounts (bulk create, list with `product`, get, transactions), the fixtures `rejected@`, `frozen@`, `outage@example.test`, `accountProvisionError`, and controls `seedChapter`, `setChecking`, `failContact`, `freezeAccount`, `setAccountStatus`, `setBalance`, `creditWallet`, `debitWallet`, `holdWallet`, `contactId`, `walletId`, `walletNumber`; `count('contacts' | 'accounts')`.
- `member-accounts.contract.test.ts` runs the port's `fundingAccountContract` against the fake (18 cases) plus 19 Crowded cases (one contact and one account for two calls, a contact found by mobile, a lost ledger, a second adapter on the same ledger, `DUPLICATE_MOBILE` with no request, `PROVISION_FAILED` and `CONTACT_NOT_FOUND`, a Failed and a Frozen contact, the open question pinned, no account number in a result or an error, a 503 not retried, a stable UUID v4 account key, transaction paging and `collectPaymentId`, untrusted descriptions, `chapter()`, date of birth bounds, no personal data in the ledger, the key requirement).
- `sandbox.contract.test.ts` gains a Crowded block, skipped unless `SANDBOX_CONTRACTS=1`, `CROWDED_SANDBOX_TOKEN` and `CROWDED_SANDBOX_CHAPTER_ID` are set (`CROWDED_BASE_URL` optional): one contact with a made-up unique mobile and one wallet account, a second call that sends no POST, and a fresh adapter that finds the contact by mobile. It was not run here (no sandbox credentials).

The secret itself is the owner's to add.
