## As-built
- `atRail` and the sweep's `resumable()` clause accept a vendor ref of **any** provider for the `transfer` entity: a payout the old rail took before a market changed provider is at a rail, and is neither sent again on the new one nor re-listed. The webhook lookup (`byVendorPayout`) still matches the current provider, which is right for an event from that provider.
- Tests: the replay-after-outage test creates its own outage payout (`po-outage-2`) instead of leaning on `po-outage-1`; the 30-minute boundary is pinned to the second (retried at 29:59, failed and reversed at 30:00, reversal once); a payout with a previous provider's ref is at a rail, is not listed and is not sent again (fails on the old code).
- Not done: the institution-payout stranded test. It needs an institution with KYB, a verified payout account and balance set up; it is better written with T-0550's institution reserve work, which sets that fixture up anyway.
