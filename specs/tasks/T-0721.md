# T-0721 [WAL] The mirror planner: from a journal's postings to the Crowded legs by net member delta, USD only, with every journal kind classified; wired to the ledger hook
REQ: REQ-LED-004, REQ-WAL-002, REQ-PAY-001 (after T-0716, T-0717) · Sprint: later (ADR-018 decision of 2026-11-11, proposed)
Files: apps/api/src/mirror/plan.ts (new), apps/api/src/mirror/plan.test.ts (new), apps/api/src/mirror/index.ts (new), apps/api/src/app.ts (shared composition root: the two hooks are passed to `PostgresLedger` when `wallet.mirror` is on and the funding binding has a `MirrorPort`), specs/tasks/T-0721.md, specs/tasks/README.md
Source: ADR-018, M1, M2, M4 and the inventory. One pure function replaces a card per flow: for each mirrored member (a party with a Crowded account ref in `ops.external_ref`), the net change of their USD `wallet` plus `pending` across the journal's postings. Negative: a `to_checking` leg for that amount. Positive: a `from_checking` leg. Zero: nothing (holds inside one member). A journal touching two members yields two legs, the debit first (seq 1) and the credit second (seq 2, `after` seq 1): the two-leg wallet-to-wallet of M2.
Assumption: M1 (net delta), M2 (debit leg before credit leg), M4 (a non-USD posting is `excluded_non_usd`, never queued, and counted by currency), M10 (a member with no Crowded account is not mirrored: the journal is classified `unmirrored_member` and the planner reports it, the guard of T-0723 and T-0724 refuses the movement up front). A journal whose metadata says `source: 'crowded'` (T-0726, if it is built) is `ingested` and queues nothing. Kinds `load`, `load_hold`, `load_release` are `vendor_first`: they are credited by T-0704 and T-0705 before the journal, so the planner never queues them (a double credit would be the bug this card exists to prevent).
Test first: TST-LED-003, TST-PAY-001. Table-driven over one fixture per journal kind in the ADR inventory, each asserting `classify(journal)` and the legs: `p2p` USD to USD between two mirrored members is `[to_checking(A), from_checking(B)]` with B's `after` A; `p2p` from a mirrored member to an unmirrored one is one leg and `unmirrored_member`; `p2p_hold`, `auth_hold`, `auth_release` and the reversal of a hold net to zero: no legs, class `internal`; `p2p_release` is two legs; `card_clearing` is one `to_checking` leg for the cleared amount (the hold's pending leg and the wallet leg together net to the member's loss); `addon` and its failure reversal are one leg each way; `fee` is one `to_checking` leg and `allowance` one `from_checking` leg (the institution side is not a member); `fx` USD to ILS is one `to_checking` leg for the USD side and the ILS posting is `excluded_non_usd` with its minor units counted under `ILS`; a cross-currency `p2p` likewise; an all-ILS `p2p`, `payout` and ILS `fee` produce no legs and class `excluded_non_usd`; `refund` is one `to_checking` leg; `deposit` and the institution `payout` reserve move nothing for any member; `reversal` of a mirrored journal yields the negated legs; the sum of `to_checking` minus `from_checking` over a journal equals the sum of the member `wallet` and `pending` postings (an invariant test over generated postings, bigint, with the `property-based-testing` skill: any journal's legs net to its members' wallet-plus-pending delta, and a journal of members only (no system account) nets to zero across the legs); the hook `onPosted` calls `enqueue` once per leg in the journal's transaction, so a failing enqueue rolls the journal back (asserted with a forced failure); a replayed journal key enqueues nothing new; `wallet.mirror` off: the hooks are not installed and no row is written.
- `classify(journal)` returns `{ class: 'mirrored' | 'internal' | 'excluded_non_usd' | 'vendor_first' | 'ingested' | 'not_member' | 'unmirrored_member', legs }` and is exported for T-0732, which proves coverage with the same function.
- No vendor, no `Date.now()`; the member's account ref is looked up once per journal through `ops.external_ref`.
