## Carried from the T-0718 review (relax on the first adapter run)
- The MirrorPort contract still asserts three vendor behaviours the 2026-11-11 probe does not prove: refusals with NOT_FOUND or ACCOUNT_UNAVAILABLE have no `paymentRef` and are never listed; a generic PAYMENT_FAILED is persisted and listed as failed; a checking-side overdraw is persisted (the probe's overdraw was a wallet draw). The probe evidences only two cases: a validation-refused wallet-to-wallet leg gets no id and is not listed, and a Rejected overdraw gets an id and is listed as failed. If Crowded behaves differently, relax the contract (make `paymentRef` optional and accept listed-or-not provided `readBack` and `payments()` agree) as this card's first edit.
- `mirrorItemResult` takes `paymentStatus`; a `status: failed` item with only an `errorCode` has none, so the adapter maps a failed item by `errorCode` itself, and `DUPLICATE_PAYMENT` goes through `readBack`.
- The simulator header says a pre-persistence refusal "leaves nothing for the key: the vendor decides afresh on a resend". That is unevidenced (the vendor may consume the key): a resend of the same key and body is safe either way.
- Carried from the T-0697 review for T-0700: take the payer IP from the connection, not the request body; the redirect-host allowlist is the adapter's job; send the normalised `new URL(...).href`, not the raw string; persist `expiresAt` per load.
- Carried from the T-0700 review (port follow-ups, 2026-11-12): `isPublicIp` treats `2002::/16` (6to4) and `2001:db8::/32` (documentation) as public; fix in a small card against `packages/ports` (see T-0703 follow-ups).

## As-built
- `packages/adapters/src/crowded/mirror.ts`: `CrowdedMirror` implements `MirrorPort` over Book Payment. An account ref is `a:<id>` (T-0699); the chapter ref stands for the checking account, whose Crowded id is found once per chapter. A ref of another shape, a memo the vendor would refuse, or an amount past 2^53 - 1 cents refuses that leg without a request.
- `book.ts` did not exist (T-0704 is not merged), so this card creates it: `postBookPayments`, `listBookPayments` (newest first, no total, stops at an empty page, a row seen twice is yielded once), `getBookPayment`, `bookWireKey` (`uuidFromKey('mirror:' + key)`, a v4; derived, not stored: the leg result has no slot for it and `paymentRef` is the vendor's id). T-0704's `creditWallet` should call the same functions.
- Item mapping: `paymentStatus` through `mirrorItemResult`; a `status: failed` item (no `paymentStatus`) by its `errorCode`, an unknown code `PAYMENT_FAILED`; `DUPLICATE_PAYMENT` is read back by memo and the leg and answered with the first payment, else `unknown`. The item carries no stored leg, so a booked item's leg is read with `GET .../payments/{id}`; if that fails the leg is `unknown` (money moved, never claimed booked).
- Failures: 429, 401, 403 (never processed) are `PROVIDER_UNAVAILABLE`; 400 or 422 `VALIDATION_FAILED`; any other failure after the write (5xx, transport, unreadable answer) `OUTCOME_UNKNOWN`. A resend is the same key and body. Reads fail `PROVIDER_UNAVAILABLE`. Errors have fixed text.
- `readBack` scans the book-payment list by memo as sent, narrowed by leg, bounded by `since`. A read-back of a failed payment carries the reason the row has, else `PAYMENT_FAILED` (the list's reason is unproven). `payments()` and `movements()` buffer the whole list, so an outage on a later page throws with nothing yielded; `payments()` yields oldest first.
- The fake (`fake-crowded.ts`) gains book payments, the list and Get, two transaction rows per payment, and the fixtures `refuseBook`, `bookStatus`, `loseBookAnswer`, `loseNextResponse`, `rateLimited`, `duplicateKeys`; amounts past 2^53 - 1 go out as exact strings.
- Contract relaxed first (carried from T-0718): `mirror.contract.ts` asserts only what the 2026-11-11 probe proves (a Rejected wallet overdraw is persisted; a validation-refused wallet-to-wallet leg is not). For NOT_FOUND, ACCOUNT_UNAVAILABLE, PAYMENT_FAILED and a checking-side overdraw it asserts agreement: a `paymentRef` means listed as failed and found by memo, none means neither. The persisted cases it needs use `vendorStatus(Rejected)`. A read-back of a failed payment may carry any reason from the closed set. The simulator still passes.
- Shared files touched, not on the card: `packages/ports/src/mirror.contract.ts` (the relaxation above) and `packages/ports/src/contracts.ts` (one export line so adapters can import `mirrorContract`). The `isPublicIp` fix (2002::/16 and 2001:db8::/32) is NOT done: the card lists no `packages/ports` files for it; it stays a small card of its own.
- Gated sandbox suite in `sandbox.contract.test.ts` (needs `SANDBOX_CONTRACTS=1`, `CROWDED_SANDBOX_TOKEN` (the existing wired secret; the workflow test refuses an unwired `CROWDED_API_TOKEN`), and `CROWDED_SANDBOX_CHAPTER_ID`): one cent out and back, a replay, and the POST statuses printed for the desktop handoff to record in `docs/setup/sandbox-contracts.md`. No handoff request is committed by this card.
- Not touched: `map.ts`, `map.test.ts` (nothing needed), `docs/ops/autopilot-log.md` (the row records the review result).
