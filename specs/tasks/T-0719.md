## Carried from the T-0718 review (relax on the first adapter run)
- The MirrorPort contract still asserts three vendor behaviours the 2026-11-11 probe does not prove: refusals with NOT_FOUND or ACCOUNT_UNAVAILABLE have no `paymentRef` and are never listed; a generic PAYMENT_FAILED is persisted and listed as failed; a checking-side overdraw is persisted (the probe's overdraw was a wallet draw). The probe evidences only two cases: a validation-refused wallet-to-wallet leg gets no id and is not listed, and a Rejected overdraw gets an id and is listed as failed. If Crowded behaves differently, relax the contract (make `paymentRef` optional and accept listed-or-not provided `readBack` and `payments()` agree) as this card's first edit.
- `mirrorItemResult` takes `paymentStatus`; a `status: failed` item with only an `errorCode` has none, so the adapter maps a failed item by `errorCode` itself, and `DUPLICATE_PAYMENT` goes through `readBack`.
- The simulator header says a pre-persistence refusal "leaves nothing for the key: the vendor decides afresh on a resend". That is unevidenced (the vendor may consume the key): a resend of the same key and body is safe either way.
- Carried from the T-0697 review for T-0700: take the payer IP from the connection, not the request body; the redirect-host allowlist is the adapter's job; send the normalised `new URL(...).href`, not the raw string; persist `expiresAt` per load.
