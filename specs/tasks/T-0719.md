# T-0719 [WAL] The Crowded adapter implements MirrorPort with Book Payment, against the fake and the gated sandbox suite
REQ: REQ-WAL-002, REQ-LED-004 (after T-0704, T-0718) · Sprint: later (ADR-018 decision of 2026-11-11, proposed)
Files: packages/adapters/src/crowded/mirror.ts (new), packages/adapters/src/crowded/mirror.contract.test.ts (new), packages/adapters/src/crowded/fake-crowded.ts, packages/adapters/src/crowded/book.ts (from T-0704: the shared Book Payment call; this card extends it), packages/adapters/src/crowded/map.ts, packages/adapters/src/crowded/index.ts, packages/adapters/src/sandbox.contract.test.ts, specs/tasks/T-0719.md, specs/tasks/README.md
Source: ADR-018, M2, and the snapshot ("Book Payment", line 10654; "Transaction Events", `finance.transaction.created`). `POST /chapters/{chapterId}/payments` with `data: { type: 'book', payments: [...] }`, at most 10 per request, continue-on-error, per-item `status: success | failed`, `paymentStatus: Pending | Sent | Rejected`, `errorCode`, `idempotencyKey` "UUID v4" scoped to the chapter. A `Rejected` book payment is HTTP 201 (T-0704 already reads it that way). The README says book payments were "not tested" in our sandbox.
Assumption: M2 (the docs say one-sided checking; unconfirmed: payment limits, order of items, whether `Pending` book payments exist), and the key shape: `uuidFromKey` (`map.ts:105`) makes a version 5 UUID while the docs say "UUID v4": the gated suite proves Crowded accepts it, else `book.ts` derives a v4-shaped one (set the version nibble to 4); the unit tests assert whichever is chosen is stable per key.
Test first: TST-WAL-003, TST-LED-003 (the port's `mirrorContract` against the fake, plus a Crowded-only block). One `bookBatch` of three legs makes one HTTP request with three items; a fake answer with item 2 `failed` / `INSUFFICIENT_FUNDS` gives `booked, refused, booked` and the first and third are booked once; `DUPLICATE_PAYMENT` on a key is read back and answered `booked` with the first payment's id, never a second movement; an item `Rejected` is `refused` with its reason; `ACCOUNT_UNAVAILABLE` maps to `ACCOUNT_UNAVAILABLE`; a response whose shape is unknown, or a timeout after the request was sent, is `OUTCOME_UNKNOWN` and `readBack` finds the payment by key (the fake keeps keys per chapter, as Crowded does, so the same key in another chapter is another payment); the description sent is our reference only (no member text), at most 80 characters; `movements` reads `GET /chapters/{id}/transactions` with `filter[createdAt]`, keeps `type: bookTransaction`, returns the two legs of a payment as two rows (`accountId`, `counterparty.accountId`), pages with each row once, and a Crowded outage on a later page throws with no partial list; `accountBalance` reads `balance` from the account (not `available`); an amount that is not USD or a `Money` over 2^53 cents is handled as bigint end to end (a test at 9007199254740993n cents); the gated sandbox suite (skipped without `CROWDED_API_TOKEN`) books one cent member to checking and back, replays the key, and records the observed status codes in `docs/setup/sandbox-contracts.md` via the desktop handoff, not here.
- The fake gains book payments with the documented validation (`NOT_FOUND`, `VALIDATION_ERROR` for an account outside the chapter, `INSUFFICIENT_FUNDS` when the source lacks the amount, `ACCOUNT_UNAVAILABLE` for `Frozen` or `Closed`, `DUPLICATE_PAYMENT`) and a transactions feed with two rows per payment. T-0704's `creditWallet` and this adapter share `book.ts`; nothing is called twice.
