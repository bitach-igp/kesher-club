# T-0932 [ADM] Plan versions: finance proposes, a second finance member approves, and only then is a version offered

Test first: TST-ADM-006.
**Why.** The demo cannot seed a funder: `POST /memberships` needs a row in `billing.plan_version`, and nothing creates one.
- **Propose.** Finance proposes a version: plan code and name, market, price in the market's currency, term in months, entitlements, FX markup and a reason. It waits as `proposed`, the way price rules do (T-0928): proposer first, a different finance member second.
- **Offer.** On approval the version is offered from its effective date; on rejection it never is. `billing.plan_version` gains a state and the approval, with a migration and a desktop handoff.
- **Never edited.** A newer version supersedes an older one for new members. A member keeps the version they joined on (`PlanService` already pins it).
- **Endpoints.** List, propose, approve, reject (`/admin/plans`); support reads.
**Tests.**
- the 409 for the proposer, then B approves and a new membership quotes the price;
- a member on the earlier version keeps theirs;
- bad price, term or currency answer 422;
- a rejected version is never offered.
