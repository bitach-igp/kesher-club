Source: the independent review of #422 (T-0792), non-blocking finding (b), two defects in the invite path of link completion:
1. Not atomic: if `links.link` fails after `team.acceptById`, the invite is consumed and the party is active staff with no IdP link; the member can never finish through the invite again.
2. A control-character IdP subject answers `#error=unavailable` AND consumes the invite. That breaks the uniform refusal (it is distinguishable) and burns the invite on a subject that could never link.
Shape: validate the subject's shape (the same rule the link store enforces) BEFORE `acceptById`, and/or run accept and link in one transaction so a failed link rolls the acceptance back; the card chooses and says why (the `TransferService`-style single unit of work if the stores share a connection). A refusal here is the same uniform 302 `#error=refused` with the same headers, and nothing is written.
Test first: (1) a link that fails after accept leaves the invite unconsumed and the party not staff; (2) a subject with a control character is refused uniformly and the invite is still usable; (3) the refusal bytes equal every other refusal cause; (4) the happy path still consumes the invite and writes the link once.
