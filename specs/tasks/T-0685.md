## As-built
- `LedgerCorrections` (`apps/api/src/approvals/ledger-correction.ts`): `POST /v1/admin/ledger-corrections` (staff token with the finance role, Idempotency-Key 8 to 128 characters) creates a `ledger_correction` approval, subject `ledger-correction:{key}`, payload `{ journalId, reason }`, two required. The same key and journal returns the same request; the same key for another journal answers `IDEMPOTENCY_CONFLICT`. An unknown journal is `NOT_FOUND`; a journal that already has a reversal is `IDEMPOTENCY_CONFLICT` at the proposal and again at execution (`ledger.journal.reverses_id` is unique).
- Two routes the card did not name, because a proposal could not otherwise be decided over HTTP: `POST /v1/admin/ledger-corrections/{id}/approve` and `.../reject` (reason required). Operations: `proposeAdminLedgerCorrection`, `approveAdminLedgerCorrection`, `rejectAdminLedgerCorrection`; schema `LedgerCorrection`. No new error code, no MCP entry, no migration (`ledger_correction` is already in the kind check).
- `ApprovalService.decide`: a `ledger_correction` is approved by finance only, and its proposer cannot approve it (FORBIDDEN); the proposer may reject (withdraw). The proposal itself counts as no approval, so two other finance users approve.
- The approval that reaches two runs `PostgresLedger.reverse(journalId, '{approvalId}:reverse', { effectiveAt: now })` (negated postings, balanced per currency, `reverses_id` set) and records `ledger_correction.execute` in the admin audit chain (actor = the completing approver, reason = the proposal's). A repeat approve on an approved request finishes a reversal a crash left undone and otherwise returns the same journal with no second audit entry. Reject posts nothing. Ledger rows are only inserted.
- No step-up: staff tokens last 5 minutes and no admin approval route takes `X-Step-Up`; `HEADER_OPS` gains only the idempotency entry for the propose route.
- Tests (`ledger-correction.test.ts`, over HTTP and PGlite, a USD plus ILS journal): role and key checks, proposer refused, one approval executes nothing, second posts one balanced reversal with the exact negated postings and balance, audit entry, replay, double reversal 409, rejection and withdrawal.
- Not decided here (owner): reversing a journal in a closed period or one whose funds are held or already spent. The reversal is dated when it posts and ignores wallet overdraft, as `reverse()` always has. A crash between the reversal and its audit entry loses the entry (the retry sees the journal and skips it).
