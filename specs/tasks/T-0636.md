## As built
- **Schema.** `org/0009_cohort.sql`:
  - `org.cohort`: name, `starts_on` and `ends_on` dates, with `ends_on > starts_on` checked in the database too.
  - `org.cohort_member`: insert-only placements, ordered by `seq`. A member's cohort is their latest placement.
  - A placement's key is unique per (key, member), so a retried batch places nobody twice.
  - Desktop handoff: `handoff/desktop/T-0636-cohort.md`.
- **Endpoints.**
  - `GET /institutions/{id}/cohorts` lists cohorts, newest start first. Each carries `memberCount`, the members
    whose latest placement it is.
  - `POST /institutions/{id}/cohorts` (Idempotency-Key) creates one. Dates are real calendar dates, so 2026-02-30
    answers 422.
  - `POST /institutions/{id}/cohorts/{cohortId}/members` (Idempotency-Key) places 1 to 500 roster member ids, all
    or none, inside one transaction. A member or cohort of another institution answers 404 and places nobody. The
    answer is the cohort plus `placed`, the placements this call made.
- **Who.** Any active staff role of the institution; another institution's staff get 404.
- The roster filter by cohort lands with the roster list (T-0638). The test checks the placements and the
  cohort's count directly.
