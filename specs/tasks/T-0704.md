# T-0704 [WAL] The funding port can move vendor-held money into the member's wallet account: release-funds, then a book payment
REQ: REQ-WAL-003 (after T-0700) · Sprint: later (ADR-018, proposed)
Files: packages/ports/src/funding.ts, packages/ports/src/funding.contract.ts, packages/ports/src/funding.simulator.ts, packages/ports/src/funding.simulator.test.ts, packages/adapters/src/crowded/funding.ts, packages/adapters/src/crowded/book.ts (new: the Book Payment call, shared with the mirror adapter T-0719), packages/adapters/src/crowded/funding.contract.test.ts, packages/adapters/src/crowded/fake-crowded.ts, specs/tasks/T-0704.md, specs/tasks/README.md
Source: ADR-018 and the snapshot ("Release Funds", "Book Payment", "Payments" intro). With fund holding enabled, collected money sits at Crowded until the partner releases it to the chapter checking account; a book payment then moves it into the contact's account. Release is a pooled amount, not per payment; a book payment's rejection is HTTP 201 with `status: Rejected`.
Assumption: A4 (release then book, after our hold; both idempotent on a UUID; fund holding enabled for our partner is unconfirmed), A1 (the member's wallet account). Amended 2026-11-11 (ADR-018, "Decision, 2026-11-11"): `creditWallet` is the load's own mirror leg (M1), done vendor first because the load starts at Crowded; the generic planner (T-0721) skips the load kinds so the credit is never made twice. The ACH branch (credit at the hold date) is the DEFAULT of D2 (M3 option 1); instant access for ACH is a different card (T-0733), BLOCKED on D2. The book payment is the `type: book` call of T-0719's port; its key must be a UUID that Crowded accepts as "v4" (`uuidFromKey` makes a version 5; the gated suite proves it, M2).
Test first: TST-WAL-003. `creditWallet({ loadId, accountRef, amount }, key)` on a settled hosted load calls release-funds once for the amount and one book payment from the chapter checking account to the member's account, answers `credited`, and the fake shows the member's balance up by the amount and the held pool down; the same key twice makes one release and one book payment (the keys are `uuidFromKey("release:" + loadId)` and `uuidFromKey("book:" + loadId)`); a release that was done by a first call that died is not repeated (the collect payment's `isReleased` is read first), and the book payment is still made once; a `Rejected` release or book payment (`InsufficientFunds`, `AccountFrozen`, ...) answers `refused` with the `reasonCode` and moves nothing further; a book payment still `Pending` answers `pending` and the same key later answers `credited`; an amount above the load's, or a currency other than USD, is `VALIDATION_FAILED`; a load that converted into the shekel purse (the instrument leg is USD, the credit is ILS) calls `release-funds` once and makes NO book payment (nothing lands in a member USD account; the ILS credit is excluded, M4); the Book Payment call lives in `book.ts` and the memo, key and result mapping are the ones T-0719 reuses; a hold not enabled (403) is `PROVIDER_UNAVAILABLE` with a message that names fund holding; the description of the book payment is our load reference (at most 80 characters) and carries no member text; a Crowded outcome the adapter cannot read after the call is `OUTCOME_UNKNOWN`-style: the call is read back before any retry, never resent under a new key.
- A new interface `FundingCreditPort { creditWallet(...) : Promise<{ status: 'credited' | 'pending' | 'refused'; reasonCode?: string }> }`, separate from `FundingPort` as `FundingStatementPort` is. A vendor with no such step (Stripe, where the simulator also does not implement it by default) does not implement it. The simulator implements it behind a constructor option so existing tests are unchanged; its contract block runs only for adapters that implement the interface.
- The fake gains `release-funds` against a held pool (a bank transfer's `Succeeded` adds to it, a card's goes to the pool too) and book payments between the chapter checking account and a contact account with the documented per-payment results.
