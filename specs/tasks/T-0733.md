# T-0733 [WAL] BLOCKED on D2 (ACH access policy): an ACH load credits the wallet at once up to the member's instant-access limit, from platform float, with a reserve
REQ: REQ-WAL-003, REQ-WAL-002 (after T-0705, T-0724, T-0729) · Sprint: later (ADR-018 decision of 2026-11-11, proposed). BLOCKED: do not start unless the owner picks M3 option 2 (instant access up to a per-member limit, the platform bearing returns) in D2. Under the default (option 1, hold for ACH) this card is not built. Option 3 (instant for all) needs a legal view first and is not carded.
Files: apps/api/src/wallet/loads.ts, apps/api/src/wallet/loads.test.ts, apps/worker/src/wallet/release-holds.ts, apps/worker/src/wallet/release-holds.test.ts, apps/api/src/config.ts, apps/api/src/config.test.ts, specs/tasks/T-0733.md, specs/tasks/README.md
Source: ADR-018, M3. With fund holding, collected money is at Crowded only after `Succeeded`, which "can persist for several business days" for a bank transfer; `release-funds` moves only collected money. Instant access before that is a book payment from checking: the platform's own float. A return after `Succeeded` "may arrive for a few business days" and nobody has said who bears it (question 34).
Assumption: M3 option 2; M7 (the limit and the reserve are configuration with no default). Unconfirmed: who bears a return after release (question 34), and Crowded's early-availability options (question 46).
Test first: TST-WAL-003. `MIRROR_INSTANT_ACH_LIMIT_MINOR` (per member, USD cents) and `MIRROR_INSTANT_ACH_TOTAL_MINOR` (all members) have no default; with `wallet.mirror` on and the option enabled either missing, boot refuses; an ACH load of $100 for a member with $200 of instant room credits the wallet in the ledger at `Pending` or `Processing` (journal `load_instant`, `partner_settlement -> wallet`, a new kind with an inventory row) and queues one `from_checking` leg under the load's key, and the later `Succeeded` posts no second credit (the hold journal is skipped, `release-funds` moves the pooled money into checking, which repays the float); a load over the member's room credits the room and holds the rest by the existing path; a `Returned` before release reverses the instant credit with a `reversal` and a `to_checking` leg back; a `Returned` that arrives after release, or when the wallet no longer holds the amount, opens one `load_returned_after_release` case (T-0705) with the shortfall and leaves the wallet negative-free by posting nothing blind; the member's instant room is the limit less instant credits not yet cleared; money asserts compare `bigint`; with the option off every load test passes unchanged.
