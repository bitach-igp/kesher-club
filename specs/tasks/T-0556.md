## As-built
- **Contract:** `POST /payouts/{id}/cancel` (`cancelPayout`, `Id` and `IdempotencyKey`, no step-up, 200 `Payout`). The `Payout` state enum gains `cancelled` (the view had no state for it and would have shown a cancelled payout as `failed`). `routes.ts`, `HEADER_OPS` (`idempotency: true, stepUp: false`) and the generated SDK follow.
- **`PayoutService.cancel(transferId, partyId, now)`:** 404 unless the payout is the caller's own member payout (unknown or malformed id, another member's, an institution's). `cancelled` answers the payout as it stands (replay); anything but `pending_hold` (`created`, `submitted`, `pending`, `settled`, failed) answers 409 `IDEMPOTENCY_CONFLICT`.
- **Order differs from the card's text on purpose:** the card lists unreserve, expire, then `apply('cancel')`. Built as `apply('cancel')` first, then `unreserve` (`{key}:unreserve`), then `ApprovalService.expire` for any open request. The transition runs under the transfer's row lock and the state machine refuses `cancel` from `pending`, so when the release wins the race the cancel fails and the reserve is untouched (the release posts from it). With the card's order the reserve could be given back and the cancel then refused, leaving a released payout posting with no reserve. The cost: a crash between the transition and the unreserve leaves a cancelled payout holding its reserve until the member's replay of the cancel (which re-runs the idempotent unreserve); the sweep does not look at cancelled payouts.
- **Handler:** `cancelPayout` requires the principal and the Idempotency-Key (8 to 128 characters, else 422), answers `payouts.view`.
- Tests (service.test.ts, handlers.test.ts): cancel returns the purse and reverses once with no partner journal or vendor ref; replay posts no second reversal; the sweep and `runPayout` leave it alone; another member, unknown id and malformed id are 404; `pending` and `settled` are 409 and keep their journals; the release landing between the cancel's read and its transition (mocked `transfers.get`) refuses the cancel and keeps the reserve; HTTP needs a session and a valid key; member and institution-staff tokens naming an institution payout get 404.
- Not done: the mobile screen and copy (own card); the same-millisecond race on real Postgres with several connections is covered by the row lock and the state machine, not by a `*.pg.test.ts`.
