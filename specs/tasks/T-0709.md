# T-0709 [WAL] PROVIDER_FUNDING can be `crowded`: config keys, registry binding, production guard, Terraform and setup docs
REQ: REQ-WAL-002 (after T-0699, T-0700, T-0703, T-0704, T-0705, T-0718, T-0719) · Sprint: later (ADR-018, proposed)
Files: apps/api/src/config.ts, apps/api/src/config.test.ts, apps/api/src/registry.ts, apps/api/src/registry.test.ts, apps/api/src/app.ts (shared composition root: pass the member chapter and the credit and account ports), infra/terraform/envs/staging/main.tf, infra/terraform/envs/prod/main.tf, docs/setup/secrets.md, docs/setup/sandbox-contracts.md, specs/tasks/T-0709.md, specs/tasks/README.md
Source: ADR-018, following how T-0041 bound Stripe (`PROVIDER_PORTS`, `createRegistry`, the secret containers) and T-0043 (production refuses a simulator whose flag is on). Findings from reading the code: `PROVIDER_FUNDING` is `sim | stripe` and `stripe` binds funding AND billing together, so `crowded` binds funding only and leaves billing on its simulator; with `billing.stripe` `defaultProd: true` the T-0043 guard names `billing` and refuses to boot production. That refusal is correct and is kept, not worked around (turning those flags off needs the owner's say-so, AGENTS.md). Terraform has no Crowded secret containers or `PROVIDER_INSTITUTION_BANKING` variable today (only docs mention Crowded), so this card adds the ones funding needs.
Assumption: A9 (billing is out of scope and stays on its own decision), A1 and A4 (the member chapter and the hold days are configuration). Amended 2026-11-11 (ADR-018, M1 and M7): the same Crowded funding binding also carries the `MirrorPort` (resolved by checking the method exists, no new capability); `CROWDED_CHECKING_ACCOUNT_ID` has no default and is required when `wallet.mirror` is on (the exposure caps and the cutoff are T-0724's keys).
Test first: TST-WAL-002. With `PROVIDER_FUNDING=crowded` and the full set (`CROWDED_BASE_URL`, `CROWDED_API_TOKEN`, `CROWDED_WEBHOOK_SECRETS`, `CROWDED_MEMBER_CHAPTER_ID`, `CROWDED_ACH_HOLD_DAYS`) `createRegistry` returns the Crowded funding adapter (with its account, credit and mirror capabilities) for `funding` and the simulator for `billing`; a partial set is refused at boot naming only what is missing, never a value, even while `PROVIDER_FUNDING` is `sim`; `CROWDED_ACH_HOLD_DAYS` is an integer from 1 to 10 with no default; with `wallet.mirror` on, `CROWDED_CHECKING_ACCOUNT_ID` is required (missing is refused at boot naming the key only) and a funding binding that is not `crowded` or has no `MirrorPort` is refused in prod (`wallet.mirror` is in the funding flag list, T-0717), so the rail fails closed; `PROVIDER_FUNDING=stripe` still binds both ports exactly as before; the production guard with `crowded` funding and `billing.stripe` on reports `port billing is a simulator but flag billing.stripe is on` (asserted, so the refusal cannot disappear silently) and passes once the billing flags are off; `wallet.enabled` on with funding on `sim` is still refused in prod; the shared Crowded keys are read once for both `institution-banking` and `funding` when both are `crowded`; the startup `providers` log shows `funding: crowded`; `infra-contract.test.ts` (generic over `PROVIDER_PORTS`) passes unchanged.
- `PROVIDER_PORTS.funding.adapters` gains `crowded` with its five keys; `PROVIDER_FUNDING` is `z.enum(['sim', 'stripe', 'crowded'])`. A Crowded key is never accepted with a live binding unless `CROWDED_BINDING=live` is set explicitly, as today.
- Terraform (staging and prod): `provider_funding` accepts `crowded`; secret containers `crowded-api-token` and `crowded-webhook-secrets` are mapped only when it is chosen (a half-set credential refuses boot, as for Stripe); `CROWDED_BASE_URL`, `CROWDED_MEMBER_CHAPTER_ID`, `CROWDED_ACH_HOLD_DAYS` and `CROWDED_CHECKING_ACCOUNT_ID` are plain environment values, not secrets. The jit service stays forced to `sim`.
- `docs/setup/secrets.md` and `docs/setup/sandbox-contracts.md` list the new names and say the webhook registration must name `collect.payment.*` events explicitly.
- Nothing flips a flag: `wallet.enabled`, `funding.ach` and the billing flags stay as `config/flags.json` says.
