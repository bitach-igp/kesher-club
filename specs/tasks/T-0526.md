As built:
- **The phases.** The model derives what to offer from `getClosure`:
  - money left: "Refund {amounts} and close", never a bare close;
  - nothing left: "Close account";
  - refunding: the refunds on their way and "Check again";
  - blocked: the reason in the member's words, with nothing to press;
  - closed.
  The close runs once per attempt key; a step-up refusal keeps the key for the retry. `IDEMPOTENCY_CONFLICT` (another attempt is already closing) reloads and shows where things stand, with a notice instead of an error.
- **Where the refund goes.** The contract does not name each refund's destination; the API refunds against the member's own loads. So the screen says "goes back to the card or account it was loaded from" rather than naming a card.
- **Verified in Chromium** with Ruth (T-0032: $30.00 loaded by card, no holds):
  - the in-app link to `/settings/close` shows "USD purse: $30.00 … $30.00 goes back to the card…" and only "Refund $30.00 and close";
  - the passkey step-up runs (`POST /auth/step-up` ×2, then `POST /me/closure` 200);
  - the simulator settles the refund at once, so the account closes: "Refund of $30.00: arrived".
