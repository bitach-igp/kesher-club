# T-0725 [CRD] A card clearing is mirrored as one leg to checking, a stand-in overdraw is exposure and a case, and the cap declines an authorisation instead of waiting for Crowded
REQ: REQ-CRD-003, REQ-CRD-004 (after T-0721, T-0722, T-0724) · Sprint: later (ADR-018 decision of 2026-11-11, proposed). Mirror path of M5: the existing code assumes an external just-in-time issuer, so this card holds whoever the issuer is (ADR-006). The ingest path, if Crowded issues the cards, is T-0726.
Files: apps/api/src/cards/jit.ts, apps/api/src/cards/jit.test.ts, apps/api/src/cards/settlement.ts, apps/api/src/cards/settlement.test.ts, specs/tasks/T-0725.md, specs/tasks/README.md
Source: ADR-018, M5 and M7. `JitService` posts `auth_hold` (`wallet -> pending`) before it answers approve (`apps/api/src/cards/jit.ts:222`); `CardSettlementService.applyClearing` posts `card_clearing` (`apps/api/src/cards/settlement.ts:84`); a stand-in approval can leave a wallet negative (`AccountService`, "overdrawn", invariant 5). The decision has a 300 ms p99 budget (REQ-CRD-003): no Crowded call may sit on this path, so the card path is Design B whatever D1 says. The planner (T-0721) already queues the clearing leg; what is card-specific is below.
Assumption: M5 (mirror: a clearing debits the wallet in our ledger and books member to checking; checking then funds the issuer's settlement, a platform-level movement for ADR-006), M7 (a clearing is a fact and always posts), M4 (a clearing in ILS is excluded; a converted authorisation mirrors its USD leg only).
Test first: TST-CRD-003, TST-CRD-004. With `wallet.mirror` on: an authorisation approved by the existing rules posts `auth_hold` and queues nothing (internal); its clearing posts `card_clearing` and queues one `to_checking` leg for the cleared amount, once, however often the clearing webhook repeats; a clearing for less than the hold queues the cleared amount, not the hold; a late clearing (after the 7-day release) queues the whole amount; a release (`auth_release`) queues nothing; the cap reached for the member declines a new authorisation with `LIMIT_EXCEEDED` (no new reason is added to the port's `JitDecision`; the metric `jit_declined_mirror_cap` is counted) and no `auth_hold` is posted, and the decision still answers inside the existing latency budget (the guard is one indexed read: a unit assertion counts one query; `jit.bench.test.ts` is not touched); a clearing over the cap still posts and queues its leg and raises `mirror.over_cap` (the merchant is owed); a stand-in approval that overdrew the wallet: the leg is booked up to the member's Crowded balance and the rest is refused by Crowded with `INSUFFICIENT_FUNDS`, which the drain turns into one `mirror_leg_dead` case naming the shortfall, not a retry loop (the shortfall is platform exposure, listed in the case); an authorisation paid from the other purse converts and queues the USD side only; with `wallet.mirror` off every card test passes unchanged.
- A card refund or reversal after clearing has no journal kind in the code today (`CLEARING_JOURNAL_KIND`, `RELEASE_JOURNAL_KIND` are the only card kinds after the hold): when one is added it is a `from_checking` leg through the same planner and needs its own row in the inventory; this card names it in a comment and a test that fails when a new card kind has no inventory entry.
Done when: listed tests pass; `pnpm typecheck`, `pnpm lint`, `pnpm test`, `pnpm trace` green; only listed files touched
Out of scope: funding the issuer from checking (ADR-006, a later card per port); Crowded-issued cards (T-0726); card refunds; the issuer's own limits.
