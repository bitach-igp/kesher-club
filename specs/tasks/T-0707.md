# T-0707 [LED] The nightly partner reconciliation can read Crowded's collect payments as the funding statement
REQ: REQ-LED-004 (after T-0700, T-0704) · Sprint: later (ADR-018, proposed)
Files: packages/adapters/src/crowded/funding.ts, packages/adapters/src/crowded/funding.contract.test.ts, packages/adapters/src/crowded/fake-crowded.ts, apps/worker/src/recon/statement-source.ts, apps/worker/src/recon/statement-source.test.ts, apps/worker/src/recon/recon.ts, apps/worker/src/recon/recon.test.ts, specs/tasks/T-0707.md, specs/tasks/README.md
Source: ADR-018 and the snapshot ("Collect Payments", "Transactions"). T-0683 gave the nightly job a `FundingStatementSource` over `FundingStatementPort.listSettled`, and T-0684 matches it to our journals. The Crowded adapter must implement `listSettled`. Crowded's collect payments listing is cross-chapter and filters by `createdAt`, not by settlement date; a row carries `status`, `amount` (gross), `fee`, `isReleased`, `releasedAt` and `collectRequest.title`, which is our load reference.
Assumption: scope unchanged by the 2026-11-11 decision (ADR-018, M6): `listSettled` stays the LOADS statement (collect payments and refunds). Book payments we originate are read by `MirrorPort.movements` (T-0718, T-0719) and proved in T-0732; balances in T-0708. A7 (the vendor's listing is the statement; transactions trace a credit to its payment by `collectPaymentId`, `collectRequestId`, `collectIntentId`), A4 (our hold journal posts when a bank transfer `Succeeded`, so that is the instant a charge is "settled" on our side). The instant a payment succeeded is not a documented field of the listing: this card uses `updatedAt` of a `Succeeded` row and a lookback of seven days on `createdAt`, and the ADR lists it as a question to Crowded.
Test first: TST-LED-004. With the fake holding three member payments that succeeded on the statement day (one card, two bank transfer), `listSettled` returns three `charge` entries with the loadId, bigint cents and the success instant, paged across two pages with each once; a payment that succeeded the day before and one created a week earlier that succeeded on the day are placed by the success instant, not `createdAt`; a `Returned`, `Failed`, `Cancelled` or still `Processing` payment is not listed as a charge; a refunded card payment lists the charge and one `refund` entry on the refund day; only chapters named as member chapters are read (an institution fee payment in another chapter is never listed, so it cannot become a `missing_journal` break); a Crowded outage on a later page throws `PROVIDER_UNAVAILABLE` and no partial list (as the Stripe adapter does); the nightly job run with the Crowded fake and our journals for the same loads opens no case; a loaded payment that Crowded says was returned after we released it (our side settled, the statement without it) opens one `missing_partner` case, which is the loss this card exists to surface; a `Succeeded` payment with no load of ours opens one `missing_journal` case.
- `fundingStatementContract` (T-0683) is the shared suite and the Crowded adapter passes it unchanged; nothing in `FundingStatementPort` changes.
- The statement is read in USD (Crowded is USD only); a shekel purse load posts `partner_settlement` in USD already (T-0517), so the match is by the instrument leg.
