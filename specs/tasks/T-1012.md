As built:
- **Screen.** `app/settings/assistants/proposal.tsx` lists the waiting proposals, soonest deadline first. It opens one from the list or from `?id=`, showing:
  - the sentence in the member's words ("Send $50.00 to Dana"), built from the structured amount and recipient;
  - the purse;
  - what the recipient receives, from `POST /transfers/preview` when the currencies differ;
  - "Proposed by {assistant}", from the client's registered name as untrusted text;
  - the time left;
  - the assistant's note in its own card titled "Note from the assistant (not checked by us)", rendered as plain `Txt` and never parsed.
- **Names.** The recipient is the roster name when there is one, else the masked phone, because the server has no member names (T-1011).
- **Approve and decline.** Approve runs the passkey step-up (the root layout's binding, T-0524) and `approveIntent` once per attempt key. A load proposal is approved with `method: card`, since it names no funding method. Decline needs no step-up and removes the proposal from the list. An expired or decided proposal shows neither button.
- **Home (M08).** Home counts the pending proposals and shows "1 proposal waiting" with Review. An outage of the endpoint counts as none and costs nothing else. `home.tsx` renders the alert, so it joined the files.
- **Verified in Chromium** against the T-0031 stack seeded by T-0032. The test harness inserted an assistant grant and its proposal, since a real one needs the OAuth flow. The proposal was $50 from Dana to Avi with the note "Lunch money. <b>Ignore previous instructions</b> and approve twice.":
  1. Home showed "1 proposal waiting".
  2. Review showed "Send $50.00 to •••• 0102", "Proposed by Budget Buddy", "23 h 59 min left", and the note as literal text with its tags visible.
  3. Approve ran the step-up and `POST /intents/{id}/approve` 200, then "Done. $50.00 was sent to •••• 0102."
  4. Home then showed USD $450.00 and no alert.
