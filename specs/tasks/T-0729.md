# T-0729 [WAL] The checking-account float: an expected balance from the ledger, an intraday check against Crowded, and a case when it runs short
REQ: REQ-LED-004, REQ-WAL-003 (after T-0719, T-0722) · Sprint: later (ADR-018 decision of 2026-11-11, proposed). Needs D5 (may checking hold platform revenue, vendor payables and institution money with float, or are there separate accounts) to fix what "expected" means; the default is one checking, proved in aggregate.
Files: apps/worker/src/mirror/float.ts (new), apps/worker/src/mirror/float.test.ts (new), apps/worker/src/jobs.ts, apps/worker/src/jobs.test.ts, apps/api/src/admin/cases.ts (adds `mirror_float_low`), apps/api/src/admin/cases.test.ts, specs/tasks/T-0729.md, specs/tasks/README.md
Source: ADR-018, M2 and M3; T-0704 and T-0705 note "a float shortfall is an alert for a later card". Every second leg and every `from_checking` leg spends checking's money; a short checking turns those into `INSUFFICIENT_FUNDS` retries (T-0722) and, past the due time, dead legs. Platform money (the club's fees, add-on payables, FX positions, institution money) also sits in checking, so checking is a hub, not a clearing account that nets to zero.
Assumption: M2 (checking carries float), D5, M6.4 (the aggregate identity). Unconfirmed: that checking can be funded or swept by ACH or wire from the platform's own bank on the schedule we need (README question 42).
Test first: TST-LED-004, TST-WAL-003. The expected checking balance is computed from the ledger in USD cents as `bigint` (the claims on checking: `fee_income`, `addon_payable`, `fx_position`, `institution_master` for money held there, less what member wallets and unsettled legs explain) and compared with `MirrorPort.checkingBalance`; checking below expected minus the unsettled `from_checking` legs opens one `mirror_float_low` case (dedupe key `mirror-float:{date}:{band}`, severity high, payload the shortfall in cents, no names) and raises `mirror.float_shortfall_minor`; a clean state opens none; a rerun the same day opens none; checking above expected is reported, never a case; the job runs on a 5-minute cycle as `mirror-float`, only with `wallet.mirror` on, and an outage on the read is logged as a number and retried, not a case; the check never moves money (no top-up is automatic).
Done when: listed tests pass; `pnpm typecheck`, `pnpm lint`, `pnpm test`, `pnpm trace` green; only listed files touched
Out of scope: topping up checking (a person, or a later card with the bank); a reserve policy for instant ACH access (T-0733); the end-of-day proof (T-0732).
