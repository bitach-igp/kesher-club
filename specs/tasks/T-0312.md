## As-built
- `POST /memberships` calls `requireStepUp` and answers 404 `FEATURE_OFF` when `billing.stripe` is off, then `MembershipService.createForCaller`: the member must be the caller or a member who accepted the caller's invite (403); `termMonths` must be a whole number 1 to 12 and the plan one on offer (422, before the billing vendor is called); a member who already holds a non-cancelled membership is not displaced (409).
- A key is its caller's: `create` answers 409 `IDEMPOTENCY_CONFLICT` when the key's membership belongs to another funder (this also covers the invite path, whose key is the invite's).
- The allowed term is the contract's 1 to 12 (`MembershipRequest`), not the plan's own `termMonths`, which is only the default.
- The tests are in the new `billing/memberships.test.ts` (the card named it but it did not exist). `termMonths: 0` used to be dropped silently by a truthiness check in the route; it is now refused.
- Shared plumbing touched: `app.ts`, `contracts/openapi.yaml` (step-up parameter, description), `packages/sdk/src/client.ts` (`HEADER_OPS`: step-up on `post /memberships`), the generated schema, `contracts/oasdiff-ignore.txt`, `docs/ops/autopilot-log.md`.
- The one other consumer, `apps/demo/src/seed.ts` (outside the card's file list, changed so `pnpm test` stays green), posted a membership for another member; it now goes the way the apps do: Ben opens the card sheet and invites Dana, Dana reads the link from the panel's notify outbox and accepts. After a dev restart the link is gone; the seed then finds the membership an earlier accept left, or says so.
- Review round 1: the no-displacement check (`held`) is check-then-act. The only unique constraint is on `idempotency_key`; `billing.membership (member_party_id)` has a plain index. Two requests for the same member under different keys (or two funders who both hold an accepted invite for that member) that overlap can both pass the check, both reach the billing vendor and both insert. Accepted in this card, because the fix is a partial unique index `(member_party_id) where status <> 'cancelled'`, which is a migration (outside the card's files, with a real-Postgres handoff and Squawk review). Follow-up card needed; until then the window is two simultaneous, step-up-authenticated requests.
