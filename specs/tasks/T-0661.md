# T-0661 [INS] An allowance payment cannot overdraw the institution's account
REQ: REQ-INS-003, REQ-INS-008 · Sprint: fix (small; T-0550 review follow-up)
Files: apps/api/src/institutions/allowance/activities.ts, apps/api/src/institutions/allowance/overdraw.test.ts, specs/tasks/T-0661.md, specs/tasks/README.md
Source: Sonnet review of PR #222 (T-0550): `payMember` debits `institution_master` with a plain `ledger.post`, no lock and no guard; `startRun` checks headroom once at the start, so money spent between that check and the member payments (a payout reserve, another run, a P2P) is not seen.
Test first: an institution with ₪10,000.00 and no credit starts a run owing ₪8,000.00; before the payments a ₪5,000.00 payout reserve takes its money: on main every member is paid and the master ends at -₪3,000.00 (overdrawn past a zero credit limit).
- `payMember` posts through `postNoOverdraft` (advisory lock on the master) with a guard that re-reads, under the lock, what the master holds plus the approved credit limit (only while KYB lets credit open, as `headroom` does): refuse INSUFFICIENT_FUNDS when the payment would take it past that.
- A refused payment is the existing failure path (retried, then recorded `failed`; a rerun pays the rest); it leaves no journal.
**Tests.** the case above: the members the money covers are paid, the one it does not is `failed`, the master never goes below the credit limit (bigint); an institution with a credit limit still pays within it; a replayed payment posts once.
Done when: listed tests pass; `pnpm typecheck`, `pnpm lint`, `pnpm test`, `pnpm trace` green; only listed files touched
Out of scope: reserving the run's money up front. Stop and ask on conflict with the spec.

## As-built
- `payMember` posts through `postNoOverdraft`; its guard reads `InstitutionCredit.headroom` inside the posting transaction (master balance and the credit limit the KYB gate lets open) and refuses INSUFFICIENT_FUNDS past it. The refusal is the workflow's existing failure path (retried, recorded `failed`); the transfer stays `submitted` and a rerun pays it.
- Tests live in `allowance/overdraw.test.ts`. No real-Postgres handoff: the lock is the one T-0544 already proved there.
