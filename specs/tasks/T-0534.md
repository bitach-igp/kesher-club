## As built
- `GET /households/{id}` replaces the FEATURE_OFF stub. The household id is the funder's own party id; any other id,
  malformed or not, answers 404 alike, so no one learns whether another household exists (THR-09).
- Members come from the caller's active, past-due or paused memberships, newest per member, sorted by name. Each has
  the available balance of both purses and the last card spend: the latest `auth_hold` on the member's wallet, as the
  amount that left the purse, when, and the merchant's category and country. The ledger does not keep the merchant's
  name, so it is not shown here; merchant enrichment belongs to the transactions list (P06, Sprint 14).
- Open invites (T-0306) appear under `invites` as `invited`. The `Household` schema gains fields only.
- `app.ts` builds `HouseholdService` after `FunderInvites` and exposes it as `services.households`.
