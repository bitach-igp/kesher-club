# T-0533 [FX] The rate lock counts down on the quote's own clock, not the phone's
REQ: REQ-FX-001 (found by T-0923) · Sprint: 12
Files: contracts/openapi.yaml, packages/sdk/src/schema.generated.ts, apps/api/src/fx/service.ts, apps/api/src/fx/service.test.ts, apps/mobile/src/quote-lock.ts, apps/mobile/src/quote-lock.test.ts, apps/mobile/app/convert/fx.model.ts, apps/mobile/app/convert/fx.model.test.ts, apps/mobile/app/fund/add-money.model.ts, apps/mobile/app/fund/add-money.model.test.ts, apps/mobile/app/fund/add-money.test.tsx, specs/tasks/T-0533.md, specs/tasks/README.md
Test first: TST-FX-001 at the screen, with the phone's clock off.

**Why.** The convert screen (M11) and add money into the other purse (P07) both counted the 30-second lock as `expiresAt` minus the phone's clock. A phone that runs fast showed a fresh quote as nearly or already expired; one that runs slow showed a lock the server no longer honours. The full run of the eighteen recordings (T-0923) moved the stack's clock five days ahead. The screen then read "Rate locked for 438696 s", never dropped the quote, and never offered another, so REC-11 could not ask for its second quote.

**Change.**
- The `FxQuote` carries `quotedAt`, the server's time when it priced the quote (the row's `created_at`).
- The app holds a quote with a deadline on its own clock: arrival time plus `expiresAt − quotedAt` (`src/quote-lock.ts`, shared by both screens).
- The server still decides: a conversion it finds late answers QUOTE_EXPIRED, and the screens already quote again on that.

**What the tests pin:**
- the API's quote body carries `quotedAt`, 30 seconds before `expiresAt`;
- with the phone's clock days behind, the same, or 90 seconds ahead, the lock reads 30 s on arrival and 0 thirty seconds later;
- the convert model, with the server five days ahead, drops the quote after 31 s of phone time.
