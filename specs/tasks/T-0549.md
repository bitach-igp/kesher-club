# T-0549 [PO] Payout reserve hardening and one place for approval expiry

Source: Opus reviews of PR #199.
Test first: a new-payee payout whose run dies between the cool-off hold and the reserve (the reserve step throws once): spend its amount by P2P during the cool-off. The reserve is made right after the hold on a normal run, but after that crash nothing reserves it until the cool-off ends (up to 24 h), so the spend succeeds and the payout later fails INSUFFICIENT_FUNDS.
- The sweep reserves a payout that is still cooling off and has no reserve yet (`resumable()` returns it for that, without submitting it), so the crash window closes on the next pass instead of at the end of the cool-off.
- `reserve()` reloads the transfer and returns unreserved if it is terminal (the narrow lock-forever race on clock skew).
- `ApprovalService.expire(id, now)` marks a request expired in one place; `expireIfDue` in the payout service calls it instead of writing `ops.approval_request` itself.
- Tests the review found missing: spend during cool-off by real P2P; a reserved payout that is approved posts from pending and leaves holds at 0; a resumed funded-but-never-reserved payout; `INVALID_PAYEE` after a reserve releases it once.
