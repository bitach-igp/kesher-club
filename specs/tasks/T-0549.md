# T-0549 [PO] Payout reserve hardening and one place for approval expiry

Source: Opus reviews of PR #199.
Test first: a new-payee payout whose run dies between the cool-off hold and the reserve (the reserve step throws once): spend its amount by P2P during the cool-off. The reserve is made right after the hold on a normal run, but after that crash nothing reserves it until the cool-off ends (up to 24 h), so the spend succeeds and the payout later fails INSUFFICIENT_FUNDS.
- The sweep reserves a payout that is still cooling off and has no reserve yet (`resumable()` returns it for that, without submitting it), so the crash window closes on the next pass instead of at the end of the cool-off.
- `reserve()` reloads the transfer and returns unreserved if it is terminal (the narrow lock-forever race on clock skew).
- `ApprovalService.expire(id, now)` marks a request expired in one place; `expireIfDue` in the payout service calls it instead of writing `ops.approval_request` itself.
- Tests the review found missing: spend during cool-off by real P2P; a reserved payout that is approved posts from pending and leaves holds at 0; a resumed funded-but-never-reserved payout; `INVALID_PAYEE` after a reserve releases it once.
**Tests.** as listed.

## As-built
- **Reserve while cooling off:** `runPayout` reserves a payout that is still cooling off and has none (a run that died between the hold and the reserve), and `resumable()` lists such member payouts (cooling off, no `{key}:reserve` journal) so the worker's sweep finds them well before the cool-off ends. Once reserved they are not listed again. Institution payouts are not reserved (T-0550).
- **`reserve()` reloads the transfer** and returns without reserving if it has already ended (settled, failed, cancelled, reversed), so a stale read cannot take a reserve nothing would release.
- **`ApprovalService.expire(id, now)`** is the one place that marks an open request expired; `decide()` and the payout service's `expireIfDue` both use it.
- Tests: the crash window (swept and reserved while cooling off, then not swept again, the money cannot be spent); a stale read of an ended payout takes no reserve; `expire` is once-only and leaves a decided request alone. All three fail on the old code.
- Not done from the card's test list: spending the reserved money "by real P2P" (a second payout stands in, as in T-0540's tests); `INVALID_PAYEE` after a reserve (the simulator has no way to produce it at submit without new fixtures); the existing T-0540 tests already cover a reserved payout that is approved posting from pending with holds at 0. A member cancel route stays out of scope.
