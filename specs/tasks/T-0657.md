## As-built (2026-11-09, checked against the real sandbox)
- **What Crowded keeps:** only the name. Against the sandbox (read-only calls, then four creates): an organization takes `ein`, `taxId` and `metadata` with a 201 and returns none of them; filters on them are ignored (they return every organization); two organizations may share a name (it did not refuse the second "Israel Global Pay"); `search` is a case-insensitive substring match on the name, anywhere in it. So the card's "ein is sent" and "metadata" options are out: the id has to live in the name.
- **Organizations and chapters** are named `<legal name> [<our institution id>]` (`createEntity`), found again by a search on the id and an exact match on that full name, and created with it. Two institutions with the same legal name get two of each; a retry with an empty ledger finds its own. The id must be a plain identifier (so it cannot fake the tag) and the tagged name at most 255 characters (`VALIDATION_FAILED` before anything is sent). The same id under another legal name is another entity, as `institutionBankingContract` says (kept: that contract is in `packages/ports`, outside this card).
- **EIN:** still validated, still not sent. Sending it does nothing observable (accepted, not kept).
- **Collections** carry no tag (the title is payer-facing and capped at 50): a retry reuses a same-titled, same-amount collection that no other key has claimed, and every collection is claimed in the ledger (`claim:<chapter>:<id>`). Two look-alike fee items get two collections, and each retry (ledger kept) answers its own. Limit: with the ledger lost entirely, two look-alike fee items cannot be told apart (the collections are interchangeable, but a retry may be given the other's ref); production persists the ledger (T-0650).
- Tests (`institution-banking.contract.test.ts`, red first): same-name institutions, retries with namesakes, refused ids and over-long names, look-alike fee items, and a retry before the collection was noted. `FakeCrowded.names()` lets a test see the names a lookup sees.
- Left in the sandbox by the investigation (a person may delete them in the portal): a second "Israel Global Pay" and three "Kesher probe (…), safe to delete" organizations.

- Also noted by review: an organization or chapter already created under an untagged name is not found by id, so a retry would make a new one (fine before production, nothing is live). And the collection claim is not atomic: with the persisted ledger, two concurrent different keys that see the same unclaimed collection may both claim it and share it; a workflow's retries are serialised by its id, so this needs two look-alike fee items racing. Whether Crowded's collections endpoint honours `search` is unchanged by this card (the code filters by exact title anyway).
