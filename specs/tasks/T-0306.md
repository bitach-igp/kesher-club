Test first: TST-BIL-002.
- **Invite (P04).** `POST /funder-invites` (Idempotency-Key): the member's name, a phone or an email, the plan code and the saved payment method. Needs the funder's approved T1 (403 otherwise). The plan's offered version is pinned on the invite.
- **Delivery.** NotifyPort sends the link (`https://app.kesher.example/join?token=`), SMS for a phone and email otherwise; only the token's hash is stored. Resend sends the same link again; cancel ends it.
- **Expiry.** 7 days, then `expired`; nothing is charged before the member accepts (T-0307). One open invite per funder and contact.
- **List.** `GET /funder-invites`: the funder's own invites with status (open, accepted, expired, cancelled).

- a T0 funder answers 403; a second open invite for the same contact answers 409; an unknown plan answers 422;
- 7 days later it reads expired; cancel and resend by another funder answer 404.
