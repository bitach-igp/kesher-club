Test first: TST-INS-012. An institution has one verified payout account per currency. Compliance records it with the IBAN (or US routing and account), the holder name as the bank's ownership letter states it, and a check that the name matches the registered name. An institution payout to any other account is refused and opens a compliance case. Changing the account sets the KYB decision back to `needs_more_info` for payouts until compliance approves the new one. The tests cover:
- a ₪3,000.00 payout to a different IBAN is refused and opens a case;
- a payout to the verified IBAN succeeds;
- a holder name that does not match the registered name cannot be verified;
- changing the account blocks payouts until it is re-approved.
