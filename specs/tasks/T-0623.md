Test first: TST-INS-012. An institution has one verified payout account per currency. Compliance records it with the IBAN (or US routing and account), the holder name as the bank's ownership letter states it, and a check that the name matches the registered name. An institution payout to any other account is refused and opens a compliance case. Changing the account sets the KYB decision back to `needs_more_info` for payouts until compliance approves the new one. The tests cover:
- a ₪3,000.00 payout to a different IBAN is refused and opens a case;
- a payout to the verified IBAN succeeds;
- a holder name that does not match the registered name cannot be verified;
- changing the account blocks payouts until it is re-approved.

Done when: listed tests pass; Spectral clean; `pnpm typecheck`, `pnpm lint`, `pnpm test`, `pnpm trace`, `pnpm db:lint` green; desktop handoff requested; only listed files touched
Out of scope: automated account verification by a vendor (later, through the port). Stop and ask on conflict with the spec.

As built:
- **Decision, 2026-10-20 (Zvi: "Own account only"):** until the bank answers question 9, an institution pays out only to its own verified account and pays its suppliers from there. Supplier payouts through Kesher (T-0527, T-0612) are refused for institutions. The console's payout screen becomes "withdraw to your account" in T-0625.
- Recording an account needs the registered name the KYB identifier check found (T-0622's `registeredName`), so the identifier check comes first. The holder name must match it, with punctuation, case, Hebrew quote marks and trailing legal forms ignored (ע"ר, חל"צ, בע"מ, Ltd, Inc). The rule is `sameHolder`.
- Every account, the first included, takes effect only on a second compliance member's approval, never the recorder's: where an institution's money goes is never one person's call. The latest recorded account is the one that counts, so a replacement stops payouts until it is approved. That is how "changing the account re-opens review" works without closing the institution's other capabilities.
- A payout to another IBAN answers 422 with `reason: not_own_account` and opens one `payout_account_mismatch` case per payout key. The case holds the IBAN's last four and a hash, never the full number. The other refusals are `no_verified_account` and `account_pending_approval`.
- The full IBAN is stored, since the payout must match it, but every response masks it.
- Five suites that pay out for institutions get `verifiedPayoutAccount()` from `test-institution.ts`.
