# T-0706 [WAL] A closure refund of a load Crowded cannot refund goes to a case, not to a refund call
REQ: REQ-WAL-005 (after T-0700) · Sprint: later (ADR-018, proposed)
Files: packages/adapters/src/crowded/funding.ts, packages/adapters/src/crowded/funding.contract.test.ts, packages/adapters/src/crowded/fake-crowded.ts, apps/api/src/wallet/closure.ts, apps/api/src/wallet/closure.test.ts, specs/tasks/T-0706.md, specs/tasks/README.md
Source: ADR-018 and the snapshot ("Refund" eligibility table). Crowded's refund call works only for a card payment with status `Succeeded`, no older than 30 days, with no refund already pending; the refund is paid by the chapter and answers `Processing`, then a webhook. A bank-transfer load, or a card load older than 30 days, has no refund call. `ClosureService.refundAgainst` calls `FundingPort.refund` against the member's settled loads, newest first, and a `failed` refund today ends that refund transfer and leaves the closure unfinished with nothing telling anyone.
Assumption: A6 (card within 30 days: refund call; anything else: an outbound ACH credit or a book payment, which we cannot send because we hold no verified bank account of the member; open question 3 in ADR-018).
Test first: TST-WAL-005. A member closing with $30 from a card load of 10 days ago: one refund call, the refund transfer settles when the vendor says so, the wallet reads zero and the account closes (the existing flow, unchanged); a member closing with $30 from a bank-transfer load: no refund call is made at the fake (`count('refunds')` is 0), the refund transfer fails with `failureCode: 'REFUND_NOT_ELIGIBLE'`, one `closure_refund_manual` case opens (dedupe key `closure-refund:{transferId}`, payload: member, load, amount, reason), the closure answers `blocked` with reason `REFUND_NEEDS_PAYOUT`, and the account stays open; a card load of 31 days: the same as the bank-transfer case; a closure retried opens no second case; a refund the fake refuses for another reason (`Insufficient funds in chapter account`) comes back `failed` with that code and is also a case; a partial refund is not offered (the Crowded refund is for the payment; the adapter refunds only an amount equal to the payment and refuses a smaller one with `REFUND_NOT_ELIGIBLE`); Stripe's closure tests pass unchanged.
- The adapter decides eligibility from the payment it reads (status, method, age at the call's clock, an existing refund), so no outbound call is made for an ineligible one; `refund` returns a `failed` `Refund` with the code instead of throwing, as the contract already allows. It sends the required `reason` (1 to 500 characters) from a fixed string, never from member text. `getRefund` maps `Processing`, `Succeeded` and the reversal events.
- `ClosureService` opens the case on `REFUND_NOT_ELIGIBLE` (and the other vendor-refused codes) through the existing case insert; it does not decide where the money goes.
Done when: listed tests pass; `pnpm typecheck`, `pnpm lint`, `pnpm test`, `pnpm trace` green; only listed files touched
Out of scope: the outbound ACH credit or book payment to a member bank account (needs a verified account and an owner decision, ADR-018 open question 3); money received by transfer (already `blocked` and waits on a payout); a partial refund of a card payment; the fee a refund returns (`feeCovered` rules are Crowded's).
