# T-0741 [WAL] The Crowded funding adapter implements FundingCreditPort: release-funds when fund holding is on, then one book payment checking to the member's wallet account
REQ: REQ-WAL-003 (after T-0704, T-0719, T-0735 item 9) · Sprint: later (ADR-018, proposed; split from T-0704 on 2026-11-12)
Files: packages/adapters/src/crowded/credit.ts (new), packages/adapters/src/crowded/funding.ts (a `creditWallet` method and two optional constructor options only), packages/adapters/src/crowded/funding.contract.test.ts, packages/adapters/src/crowded/fake-crowded.ts, packages/adapters/src/crowded/book.ts (only to export a shared `checkingAccountId(client, chapterId)`; `bookWireKey` takes its namespace after T-0735 item 9), packages/adapters/src/crowded/mirror.ts (only to call that shared helper instead of its private copy), packages/adapters/src/crowded/index.ts, specs/tasks/T-0741.md, specs/tasks/README.md
Source: ADR-018 A4 and the snapshot ("Release Funds", "Book Payment", "Collect Payments"). Verified 2026-11-12 against `origin/main` at 90fe882. What is already there: `book.ts` exists (T-0719: `postBookPayments`, `listBookPayments`, `bookWireKey`, shared with `CrowdedMirror`); the old card called it "new", which was stale. `CrowdedMirror.bookBatch` already does a leg `from_checking` to a member account with a unique memo, a UUID v4 wire key, the vendor's per-item result mapped to `booked | refused | unknown` and `readBack` by memo, so this card BOOKS THROUGH A `MirrorPort` passed in as an option and does not write a second book client. What is NOT there: the fake has no `release-funds` and no held pool (`grep release fake-crowded.ts` finds only the card-credit helper), and its `GET /collect-payments` lists only card payments.
Findings that shape the card (from the sandbox probe of 2026-11-11, `docs/vendors/crowded/README.md` question 29): fund holding is NOT enabled for our partner, `release-funds` answers `403 "Held collection payments are not enabled"` and "hosted-page collections release to the chapter on their own". So the adapter has two modes chosen by an option with no default, `fundHolding: boolean` (config `CROWDED_FUND_HOLDING`, wired in T-0709): `true` = `release-funds` for the amount, then the book payment; `false` = no release call, only the book payment (the collected money is expected in the chapter's own account already; WHICH account and WHEN is README question 35 and is unverified). Nothing here may be called live until Crowded answers question 29, and the live sandbox cannot drive a collection payment (README question 37), so this card is fake-only; the release key replay (does Crowded replay a reused `release-funds` key?) cannot be probed while the 403 stands and is an assumption, written down in the code header.
Assumption: A4, M1, M3 option 1 (hold stays the ACH default), A1 (the member's wallet account ref comes from T-0740's `ops.external_ref` row, never from the load).
Test first: TST-WAL-003 (the port's credit block of T-0704 against the fake, plus the Crowded-only cases). With `fundHolding: true`: `creditWallet({ loadId, accountRef, amount }, key)` on a load whose payment is `Succeeded` calls `release-funds` once for the amount, then books one `from_checking` payment of the amount to the member's account with memo `load-credit:<short load ref>` (at most 80 ASCII characters, unique per load AND attempt, no member text), answers `credited`, and the fake shows the member balance up by the amount and the held pool down by the amount; with `fundHolding: false` the same call makes NO release request (the fake's release counter stays 0) and the book payment alone; the same key twice makes one release and one book payment; a first call that died after the release is finished by the next call without a second release (the adapter reads the payment's `isReleased` from `GET /collect-payments` filtered by chapter and a creation window around the payment, matched by id, before it sends one; and the release `idempotencyKey` is the same UUID v4, `bookWireKey('release:' + key)` style, so a replay is safe either way), and the book payment is made once; a release the fake answers `Rejected`, or a book payment `Rejected` (`INSUFFICIENT_FUNDS` at checking, `ACCOUNT_UNAVAILABLE`, ...) answers `refused` with the port's reasonCode and moves nothing further; a book payment answered `Pending` or an answer we cannot read answers `pending` and the SAME key later answers `credited` (the leg is found again by `readBack` on its memo, never resent under a new key); a payment that is `Returned`, `Failed` or not yet `Succeeded` answers `refused` `NOT_SUCCEEDED` with no money call; an amount other than the load's, or not USD, is `VALIDATION_FAILED`; `release-funds` answering 403 is `PROVIDER_UNAVAILABLE` with fixed text that names fund holding and no vendor text; an ACH load asked before its hold date is NOT checked here (the caller owns the hold, T-0705); the credit never converts: a load into the shekel purse is not asked (T-0714 refuses it, T-0704 out of scope).
- `credit.ts` exports `creditWallet(deps, input, key)`: deps are the Crowded client, the chapter id, the `MirrorPort`, `fundHolding`, and a `loadPayment(loadId)` seam that `CrowdedFunding` provides from its existing ledger and `getLoad` (so a load id we never made is `UNKNOWN_LOAD` with no request). `CrowdedFunding` gains `creditWallet` only when the optional `credit: { mirror, fundHolding }` option is given, so the T-0700 constructor tests and every other caller are unchanged and `isFundingCreditPort(new CrowdedFunding(...))` is false without it.
- Memo and keys: the wire key for the release and for the book payment come from `bookWireKey` with a credit namespace (T-0735 item 9 makes the namespace an argument; until it is merged this card may not land); a mirror leg and a credit with the same workflow id derive different wire keys; the memo carries the attempt number (`load-credit:<ref>:a<n>`, n from the caller's idempotency key suffix `:credit:<n>`, T-0705) so a retry after a refusal under a NEW key reads back its own payment and never another's (the port documents this, `mirror.ts` MirrorLeg.memo).
- The fake gains: a held pool (a bank transfer's or a card's `Succeeded` adds to it), `POST /chapters/{id}/payments/release-funds` (403 while `fundHolding` is off at the fake, `Rejected` when the amount exceeds the pool, idempotent on the key, moves pool to the chapter checking balance), `isReleased` and `releasedAt` on `GET /collect-payments` rows for ACH as well as card, and the per-payment results the book route already has. Pool semantics are the docs' ("All held funds are consolidated", a release is a pooled amount): the fake sets `isReleased` on the oldest unreleased payments that the released amount covers; this is the fake's model of an unverified rule and a comment says so.
