## As-built
- **F42 (the card's default, no Kesher-finance approval route):** `PayoutService.request` refuses a member payout that would need a second approver (over ₪5,000) with `VALIDATION_FAILED` and `limit: { minor: "500000", currency: "ILS" }`, after the funds check and before anything is created or reserved. An institution's payout is unaffected. The T-0540 expiry and rejection paths stay in the engine (an approval that is still decided or expires fails the payout and releases its reserve); the tests that need such a member payout skip that one refusal with a spy on the private `refuseUnapprovable` (`allowBigMemberPayouts` in `payouts/service.test.ts`).
- **F43:** `ApprovalService.request` now works out `required` inside the insert transaction. For a `payout` with an institution-id scope it locks the institution row, sums the institution's payout requests over the rolling 24 hours (rejected and expired ones excluded) and requires two approvers once the sum plus this payout passes ₪5,000. The first requests that keep the day's total at or under the threshold stay single-approver; a payout that is itself over the threshold already needed two.
- Not in this card: a split across several institutions, or payouts in another currency (payouts are ILS only); the sum counts requests, not settlements.
- Tests: member over the limit → 422 with the limit and nothing created; the rolling sum crossing the threshold (a second, different approver); twenty ₪4,999 payouts by one person; other institutions, rejected, expired and day-old requests not counted.
