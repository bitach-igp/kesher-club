# T-0722 [WAL] The mirror drain: a worker job books queued legs at Crowded in order, retries with backoff, reads back an unknown outcome, and opens a case when a leg dies
REQ: REQ-LED-004, REQ-WAL-002 (after T-0717, T-0718, T-0719, T-0721) · Sprint: later (ADR-018 decision of 2026-11-11, proposed)
Files: apps/worker/src/mirror/drain.ts (new), apps/worker/src/mirror/drain.test.ts (new), apps/worker/src/jobs.ts, apps/worker/src/jobs.test.ts, apps/api/src/admin/cases.ts (adds the case kinds `mirror_leg_dead` and `mirror_half_pair` to `OPS_CASE_KINDS`), apps/api/src/admin/cases.test.ts, specs/tasks/T-0722.md, specs/tasks/README.md
Source: ADR-018, M1 and M7; the `outbox-publish` job (`apps/worker/src/jobs.ts:195`, 10 s cycle) is the model: claim with `skip locked`, one result per row. Here the vendor call is made AFTER the claim transaction ends (never inside it), and a row is settled, retried or killed in a second short transaction.
Assumption: M1 (idempotent key `{journalKey}:m{seq}`, same-day due time), M2 (leg 2 only after leg 1 is `settled`), M7 (backoff, dead-letter), A1 (the member's account ref from `ops.external_ref`).
Test first: TST-LED-003, TST-WAL-003. A queued pair is drained in two steps in order: leg 2 is not sent while leg 1 is `pending`; both settle and the Crowded fake shows the sender down, the recipient up and checking unchanged; a Crowded outage (`PROVIDER_UNAVAILABLE`) leaves the row `pending` with `attempts + 1` and a later `next_attempt_at`, and the next cycle after the outage books it once (same key); an `OUTCOME_UNKNOWN` is read back by key before any resend and never booked under a new key (asserted with `loseAnswer`); a permanent refusal of a single leg (`ACCOUNT_UNAVAILABLE`, `NOT_FOUND`) kills that leg and opens one `mirror_leg_dead` case (dedupe key `mirror:{journalId}:{seq}`, severity high, payload: journal, seq, amount, reason code, no names); a refusal of leg 2 after leg 1 settled opens one `mirror_half_pair` case for the pair (T-0723 adds the automatic repair for a P2P; every other journal stays a case); `INSUFFICIENT_FUNDS` at checking on a `from_checking` leg is a retry, not a death, until the due time (the float may be topped up: T-0729), then dead; a row past `due_by` is dead and counted in the report as `overdue`; two worker instances never book one row twice (`skip locked`); the job reports `{ booked, retried, dead, pending, oldestPendingMs }` as numbers only and exports the gauge `mirror.oldest_pending_ms` and `mirror.unmirrored_minor` (the scheduler copies numbers only, `scheduler.ts`); the job is registered as `mirror-drain`, scope `club`, a 10 s cycle, and does nothing when `wallet.mirror` is off or no `MirrorPort` is bound; it never logs an account number, name or mobile.
- Sends one leg per request in this card (T-0728 batches). A pair never shares a request.
