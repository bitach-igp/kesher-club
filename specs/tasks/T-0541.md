## As built
Decided by Zvi (2026-11-04: "if it doesn't contradict our plan, push it through").
- **Retry** (`payouts/service.ts` `runPayout`): a `submitted` payout is no longer returned as it stands. If the vendor has a payout for it (an `ops.external_ref` row, new activity `atRail`) its webhook finishes it as before; if not, the rail was down when it was submitted, and the run goes on to `submit`, which posts the journal under its key (a replay finds it) and asks the vendor again under the same key: exactly one vendor payout results. A replay of the request now reports the outage (503) instead of a misleading "processing".
- **Sweep** (`resumable`): also selects `submitted` payouts with a journal and no vendor ref older than 5 minutes (`RETRY_AFTER_MS`), for any payout, member or institution; the worker's sweep (`payout-workflow.ts`) needed no change (it runs `runPayout` for each).
- **"After N attempts" became "after 30 minutes"** (`STRANDED_AFTER_MS`): nothing records an attempt count (`ops.transfer` has no such column and a failed retry writes no event), so adding one would be a migration. Age does the same job and is the card's own RUN-01 threshold: a submitted payout with no vendor ref 30 minutes old is failed (`PROVIDER_UNAVAILABLE`), its journal and any reserve are reversed once, the payer is told (the existing failed-payout push), and an `ops.case` of kind `payout_stranded` (high, deduped per transfer) is opened for ops.
- **The RUN-01 alert is not wired:** no alert emitter exists for it in this repo (infra has no port alert yet, T-0655); the case is the signal ops get until that exists.
- **The post-cool-off check:** a member payout under the approval threshold that cooled off is resumed by the sweep (the approval auto-approves); a test pins it. No code change was needed there.
- **Files outside the card's list:** `docs/ops/autopilot-log.md`. `apps/worker/src/payouts/payout-workflow.ts` (listed) needed no change.
