# T-0120 [AUTH] Passkey ceremonies declare the install's deviceId; the app sends it
REQ: REQ-AUTH-001, REQ-AUTH-002, REQ-AUTH-007 (found in T-0032) · Sprint: 10
Files: contracts/openapi.yaml, contracts/oasdiff-ignore.txt, packages/sdk/src/schema.generated.ts, apps/api/src/auth/handlers.ts, apps/api/src/auth/handlers.test.ts, apps/mobile/src/api.ts, apps/mobile/src/api.test.ts, apps/mobile/src/session.ts, apps/mobile/app/(auth)/signin.model.ts, apps/mobile/app/(auth)/signin.model.test.ts, apps/mobile/app/(auth)/sign-in.test.tsx, apps/demo/src/seed.ts, specs/tasks/T-0032.md, specs/tasks/T-0120.md, specs/tasks/README.md
Test first: TST-AUTH-001, TST-AUTH-002, TST-AUTH-007. `POST /auth/passkeys/register` and `POST /auth/passkeys/authenticate` key their ceremonies by `deviceId`; the register flow also stores it on the passkey and the device row, and refresh needs it later. Yet `PasskeyRegisterRequest` declared neither `deviceId` nor `purpose`, and the mobile app sent neither. As a result:
- every app registration stored `deviceId: undefined`;
- every app sign-in shared one pending challenge, so two members signing in at once failed each other;
- a recovery enrolment went out as a plain registration.

The contract now declares a `DeviceId` (1 to 128 characters, the install id kept in the secure store) on register, authenticate and refresh. It also declares `purpose` (`register` or `recovery`) on register. Step-up gets its own `StepUpRequest`, since it needs no device. The API refuses a missing, empty, over-long or non-string `deviceId` with 422 `VALIDATION_FAILED` and never defaults it. A test shows two devices signing in at once each keep their own challenge.

On the app side:
- `Session.deviceId()` gives the install id.
- `sdkApi()` sends it on both steps of each ceremony.
- The sign-in model passes the flow's `purpose`.

The accepted breaking changes are listed in `contracts/oasdiff-ignore.txt`. The only client is the mobile app, updated here.
