Test first: TST-INS-010. `kybGate(institutionId, capability)` reads the current decision and answers `FEATURE_OFF` naming the status when the capability is closed (ADR-017 table):
- deposit approval: the deposit stays pending and nothing posts;
- allowance runs: the preview and the run, checked again inside `startRun` under the institution's lock;
- credit: setting a limit, and any run that would draw on it;
- payouts: the request.

The tests cover:
- `needs_more_info` leaves a ₪20,000.00 deposit pending with the balance at 0;
- `approved_with_limits` runs a ₪800.00 allowance within the balance, refuses a run that needs credit, and refuses a new limit;
- `rejected` refuses a payout;
- `approved` opens everything.

Those five tests get `approvedInstitution()` (`apps/api/src/test-institution.ts`), which records an approved decision; collections, rosters, invites and exports are not gated and stay as they are. The demo seed approves the demo yeshiva, so REC-14 and REC-15 still run.
Done when: listed tests pass; `pnpm typecheck`, `pnpm lint`, `pnpm test`, `pnpm trace` green; the T-0920 recordings still pass
Out of scope: holding deposits that arrive for a rejected institution (returned by hand as the bank instructs). Stop and ask on conflict with the spec.

As built:
- `KybGate` (`kyb-gate.ts`) holds ADR-017's table as `OPEN` and answers FEATURE_OFF with `kyb` and `capability` in the problem body.
- Credit is gated inside the headroom: under `approved_with_limits` the drawable limit is zero, so a run that would need credit answers INSUFFICIENT_FUNDS with `creditClosed: true`. Its message says "no credit until the institution's KYB is fully approved" instead of quoting a limit it cannot draw. It runs under the institution's lock in `startRun`, like the balance check, which leaves room for the REQ-INS-013 suspension (T-0624).
- A deposit's second approval is gated before anything is decided, so a blocked deposit stays pending with no approval written. Once the institution is approved, the same approval posts it.
- Setting a credit limit is gated at the proposal and again at the second admin's approval, since the decision may change in between.
- The demo seed decides the yeshiva's KYB as Cleo Compliance (`staff-compliance-1`), with Rivka as its control person and signatory. That made Rivka T2 (`personas.ts`, `docs/setup/demo.md`); the staff client now signs the subject's roles. REC-01 to REC-09 pass on the new seed (9 of 9, 3.6 minutes).
- The worker's payout test builds `PayoutService` by hand, so it gained the gate too. The other institution tests (collections, rosters, invites, exports, consent) are not gated and are unchanged.
