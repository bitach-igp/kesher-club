# T-0705 [WAL] A load credits the vendor wallet before its journal: card settle and ACH release, with a refused credit as a case and a numbered retry

Files: apps/api/src/wallet/loads.ts, apps/api/src/wallet/loads.test.ts, apps/worker/src/wallet/release-holds.ts, apps/worker/src/wallet/release-holds.test.ts, specs/tasks/T-0705.md, specs/tasks/README.md
Source: ADR-018 and `LoadService` (`settle`, `release`) verified 2026-11-12 at 90fe882. Today `settle()` posts the `{workflowId}:settle` journal straight away for a card load, and `release()` asks the vendor then posts `{workflowId}:release` (pending -> wallet). With a vendor that holds the money the vendor step (T-0704) must come first and the journal only after it answers `credited`, or our wallet shows money the vendor never moved. Re-cut: the old card also held the account gate (now T-0740) and the return-after-release case (now T-0742), about 500 lines with tests in a 635-line file. `apps/worker/src/wallet/load-workflow.ts` is NOT edited: its sweep goes through `applyVendorState`, which calls `settle`.
Assumption: A4, M1 (the load is vendor first and the planner T-0721 skips it), M3 option 1 (ACH keeps the hold; this card gives no instant access, T-0733 is BLOCKED on D2), A5 is T-0742. Needs no D1, D3, D4 or D5.
Test first: TST-WAL-003. Deps: `LoadService` takes an optional `credit?: FundingCreditPort` (found by `isFundingCreditPort` on the funding binding, passed by T-0709 in `app.ts`). A held hosted load whose hold date passes: the sweep reads the payment, calls `creditWallet` once with the member's `funding_account` ref (the `ops.external_ref` row T-0740 writes), then posts ONE release journal `{workflowId}:release`; a card load credits at the vendor before its single `{workflowId}:settle` journal; `pending` posts nothing and the next cycle retries with the same key (a due hold is asked every cycle, `stale()`); `refused` posts nothing, the hold or submitted state stays, and opens one `ops.case` of kind `load_credit_refused` (severity high, dedupe key `load-credit:{transferId}`, payload: transferId, loadId, amount, the reasonCode, `attempt`), and while that case is `open` or `investigating` the load is not credited again (as `unknownLoadOpen` does for `load_unknown`); a crash between the vendor call and the journal is finished by the next sweep with the same key and one journal; a missing `funding_account` ref for a hosted load is `refused` `NO_ACCOUNT` in the same case with no vendor call; a vendor with no `creditWallet` (Stripe, the plain simulator, `credit` not passed) posts exactly the journals it posts today, so every existing test passes unchanged; `ReleaseHoldsJob.run()` reports `credited` and `refused` counts beside `released`, and `lagMs` and the SLA are unchanged.
- Attempt rule (a refusal is final for its key at the vendor, so a retry needs a new key and a new memo): the credit key is `{workflowId}:credit:{n}` and `n` is read from the case, no migration: no case means n = 1; a case `open` or `investigating` means do not call; a case `resolved` or `dismissed` means n = `payload.attempt + 1` and the refusal that follows reopens the same case (the upsert of `openUnknownLoadCase`) with `payload.attempt = n`. Test: refuse, resolve the case, the next sweep calls with `:credit:2` and credits, one journal; refuse again reopens the one case, never two.
- The case kinds are text with no check constraint (`db/migrations/ledger/0002_ops_cases.sql`), so no migration. The runbook line for `load_credit_refused` is ops's to add with `club-runbook` (not this card).
- `creditWallet` only runs once the vendor reports the load `succeeded` and its hold date has passed (amended 2026-11-12 after T-0704, and again after T-0745: the port refuses an `on_hold` load with `NOT_SUCCEEDED`, and the Crowded adapter reads an ACH `succeeded` only once its hold date has passed, so the caller re-reads after the date, sees `succeeded`, then credits with the numbered retry key `{workflowId}:credit:{n}`); `failed` still fails the load first (unchanged).
