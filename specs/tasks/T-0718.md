# T-0718 [WAL] MirrorPort: book a USD amount between a member's vendor account and the chapter checking account, read it back by key, list the movements; simulator and contract
REQ: REQ-WAL-002, REQ-LED-004 · Sprint: later (ADR-018 decision of 2026-11-11, proposed)
Files: packages/ports/src/mirror.ts (new), packages/ports/src/mirror.contract.ts (new), packages/ports/src/mirror.simulator.ts (new), packages/ports/src/mirror.simulator.test.ts (new), packages/ports/src/index.ts (exports), specs/tasks/T-0718.md, specs/tasks/README.md
Source: ADR-018, M2 and M4. `InstitutionBankingPort.bookTransfer` is the sub-account fund call (parent to sub-account only, `packages/adapters/src/crowded/institution-banking.ts:474`), not the Book Payment between checking and a contact account. A member mirror needs its own port, resolved from the `funding` binding by checking the method exists (as T-0684 and T-0698 do), so no new registry capability, catalogue row or provider key.
Assumption: M2 (checking on one side, up to 10 independent items per request, continue-on-error, per-item result), M4 (USD only, refused otherwise), M10 (the member's account must be `open`).
Test first: TST-WAL-003, TST-LED-003 (the port's `mirrorContract`, run against the simulator). `bookBatch(legs, chapterRef)` takes 1 to 10 legs `{ direction: 'to_checking' | 'from_checking', accountRef, amount: Money, memo, idempotencyKey }` and returns one result per leg in order: `{ status: 'booked' | 'refused' | 'unknown', reasonCode?, paymentRef? }`; a leg in ILS, zero or negative is `VALIDATION_FAILED` before any call and refuses the whole batch (a non-USD amount never reaches the vendor); more than 10 legs is `VALIDATION_FAILED`; the same key twice books once and answers `booked` with the same `paymentRef`; a memo over 80 printable ASCII characters is refused; a refused leg does not stop the others (continue-on-error) and the result says which; reason codes are a closed set `INSUFFICIENT_FUNDS | ACCOUNT_UNAVAILABLE | NOT_FOUND | VALIDATION_ERROR | PAYMENT_FAILED`; `readBack(idempotencyKey)` answers `booked | refused | not_found` without moving anything, so a leg whose answer was lost is read before any resend; a vendor outage is `PROVIDER_UNAVAILABLE`, an unreadable answer on a call that may have moved money is `OUTCOME_UNKNOWN` (never a blind retry, same as `InstitutionBankingError`); `checkingBalance(chapterRef)` and `accountBalance(accountRef)` return `balance` as bigint cents; `movements({ from, to })` pages `{ transactionRef, accountRef, direction, amount, counterpartyAccountRef?, description, at }` each exactly once, each book payment appearing as two rows (one per account) as `finance.transaction.created` does; simulator controls: `refuse(key, code)`, `outage(on)`, `loseAnswer(key)` (books, then answers `unknown`), `freeze(accountRef)`.
- Money is `Money` at the edge, `bigint` inside; no error message or log line carries a name or mobile. The port knows no ledger.
Done when: listed tests pass; `pnpm typecheck`, `pnpm lint`, `pnpm test`, `pnpm trace` green; only listed files touched
Out of scope: the Crowded adapter (T-0719); who calls it (T-0722); any ledger or queue code; ILS (M4).

## As-built
- `packages/ports/src/mirror.ts`: `MirrorPort` (`bookBatch`, `readBack`, `checkingBalance`, `accountBalance`, `payments`, `movements`), `MirrorError` (`VALIDATION_FAILED | PROVIDER_UNAVAILABLE | NOT_FOUND | OUTCOME_UNKNOWN`), `validateMirrorBatch`, `isMirrorMemo`, `mirrorItemResult` (the vendor `paymentStatus` mapping an adapter and the simulator share) and `isMirrorPort(port)`, the guard a caller uses on the `funding` binding's port (an optional capability found by its methods, as `FundingStatementPort` is). No registry capability, catalogue row, flag or provider key. Exported from `index.ts`, with the simulator.
- Shaped by the live Crowded sandbox (2026-11-11), after review of the first cut:
  - Every leg result echoes the leg the vendor holds (`leg`: accountRef, direction, amount, memo, with `paymentRef` and `status`). A reused key with another body replays the FIRST payment, so the caller compares `leg` with what it sent.
  - The vendor has no lookup by key, so `readBack({ chapterRef, memo })` finds a leg by memo: `found` (with the stored result), `not_found` or `ambiguous`. The memo is required on every leg and must be unique: the caller's contract (the planner embeds a leg reference); a repeat inside one batch refuses the batch. A leg whose answer is lost (`loseAnswer`, `loseBatch`) is recovered through this read.
  - Whole-batch `VALIDATION_FAILED`, nothing sent: 0 or over 10 legs, non-USD, zero or negative, `amount.minor` not a bigint, empty memo, empty accountRef, a key outside 8 to 128 characters or outside letters, digits and `._:-` (the 8 to 128 rule is CLAUDE.md's API rule), a key repeated in the batch, a memo repeated in the batch. A memo over 80 printable ASCII characters refuses that leg only (`VALIDATION_ERROR`); the vendor never saw it, so a read-back finds nothing.
  - Reason codes: `INSUFFICIENT_FUNDS | ACCOUNT_UNAVAILABLE | NOT_FOUND | VALIDATION_ERROR | PAYMENT_FAILED | DUPLICATE_PAYMENT`. `DUPLICATE_PAYMENT` is the vendor's answer to a reused key; the adapter resolves it by reading the payment back, never as a final refusal.
  - `mirrorItemResult`: `Sent` is `booked`; `Rejected` (even on an item the vendor called a success) is `refused`, never `booked`; `Pending` or an unrecognised status is `unknown`, resolved by `readBack`.
  - Failure rule: any failure after the request may have been sent is `OUTCOME_UNKNOWN`; `PROVIDER_UNAVAILABLE` only when it provably never left; resend only the same key and the same body, never a new key. The adapter derives the wire key as a v4 UUID (T-0734); the vendor key is scoped to the chapter.
  - Balances are `{ status: 'open' | 'frozen' | 'closed', balance, hold, available }` in bigint; `hold` is card authorisations, so a balance proof compares `balance` and treats `hold` apart.
  - `payments({ chapterRef, from, to })` mirrors the vendor's book-payment list (paymentRef, status `sent | failed | pending`, amount, memo, source and destination refs, createdAt), paged, each once: what a same-day reconciler joins on. `movements` is kept for the bank-side feed but carries no paymentRef (the vendor's transaction rows have none and cannot be joined to a payment by id).
- Simulator (`MirrorSimulator`): `openChapter`, `openAccount`, `setStatus` (error on an unopened reference), `setHold`, one-shot fixtures `refuse`, `vendorStatus(key, 'Rejected' | 'Pending')` (with `settle(key)`), `loseAnswer`, `loseBatch`, `clearFixtures`, and `outage`. Precedence on one key: refuse, then vendorStatus, then loseAnswer (dropped). Fixtures are consumed when their key is processed; a refused batch consumes none. `OUTCOME_UNKNOWN` is thrown only by `loseBatch`; a lost answer on one leg is the per-leg `unknown`.
- Second review round (live probe, 2026-11-11): the vendor PERSISTS a Rejected payment (an id, listed as `failed`) but not a pure validation refusal (no id, never listed). `MIRROR_PERSISTED_REFUSALS` is `INSUFFICIENT_FUNDS` and `PAYMENT_FAILED`; `VALIDATION_ERROR`, `NOT_FOUND` and `ACCOUNT_UNAVAILABLE` refuse before persistence: no `paymentRef` or `leg`, not in `payments()`, `readBack` answers `not_found`, and a resend is decided afresh. The contract asserts only this.
  - `readBack({ chapterRef, memo, accountRef?, direction?, amount?, since? })`: the leg narrows the match (the adapter compares source, destination and amount), `since` bounds the scan; `payments` takes an optional `since` (the later of `from` and `since` applies). Documented: vendor description normalisation or truncation is unknown; a Pending payment reads `unknown`.
  - `ambiguous` returns every candidate result (paymentRef, status, leg). A retry of a refused leg under a new key MUST use a different memo (an attempt suffix); tested.
  - `mirrorItemResult`: a Rejected item with no reason, or an unknown one, is `PAYMENT_FAILED`; the vendor's `InsufficientFunds` is `INSUFFICIENT_FUNDS`.
  - `DUPLICATE_PAYMENT` left `MirrorReasonCode` (type `MirrorDuplicateCode`, adapter-internal); `refuse()` rejects it.
  - `setHold` takes a non-negative bigint at most the balance. A batch refused by validation or an outage consumes no fixture; `loseBatch` is consumed by the next batch that passes validation, even one of replays only (tested).
- `specs/tasks/README.md` is unchanged: the index row needs no edit for a card that stays `later`.
