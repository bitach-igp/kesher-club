## As-built
- Audit by script over both apps: key parity and placeholder parity were already enforced (mobile's `i18n.test.ts`, and the console's `Record<CopyKey,string>` typing), so the card's "parity fails today" did not hold; a console `i18n.test.ts` now pins parity, placeholders and non-empty strings as a test, not only a type. Untranslated English: 5 Hebrew strings equal their English; 3 are names (Apple Pay, Google Pay, CSV) and IBAN is the standard's own name, each now listed in a test with its reason. The real one was the US routing number: `apply.account.routing` and its error sentence now read `מספר ניתוב (ABA)` (the sentence had a raw English phrase in it). Tests: no Hebrew string equals the English except the listed names; no Hebrew sentence carries an English phrase of three words or more.
- Plan and schedule names: staff-entered data (a plan code like `ohr-club`, a schedule's name), shown as written, so there is nothing to translate. This card's and T-0676's first drafts listed "plan names translated"; T-0676's As-built records why it was not done, and this one agrees.
- Currency sign: not changed, and the card's premise is not in the specs. `format(Money, 'he-IL')` in `packages/domain/src/money.ts` writes the sign after the number (`1,284.50 ₪`) and its own test pins that. Changing it would be a product decision about Hebrew money display across every screen; left for the owner (the design review only noted it).
