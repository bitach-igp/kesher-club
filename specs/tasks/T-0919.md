# T-0919 [ADM] Staff sign in to a local console through a simulator identity provider
REQ: REQ-AUTH-009 (after T-0031 and T-0918) · Sprint: 10
Files: apps/simulators/src/oidc.ts, apps/simulators/src/oidc.test.ts, apps/simulators/src/server.ts, apps/admin/src/config.ts, apps/admin/src/boot.test.ts, docs/setup/demo.md, specs/tasks/T-0919.md, specs/tasks/README.md
Test first: the console signs staff in only through Identity Center (`apps/admin/src/auth/oidc.ts`: discovery, authorization code, token, JWKS), so a local console cannot be opened without real SSO secrets. There is no bypass to add and none is wanted: the console keeps its full OIDC path, and the simulator panel gains a standards-shaped issuer at `/oidc` with `/.well-known/openid-configuration`, `/authorize`, `/token` and `/jwks`. It signs ES256 ID tokens with a key generated at start, for fixed staff personas with `roles` (`support`, `compliance`, `finance`, `admin`) and a second `admin` for two-person flips. `/authorize` shows a persona picker in place of a password form. `oidc.test.ts` drives the console's own `discover`, authorization-request and token-exchange helpers against it: the ID token verifies against `/jwks`, carries the nonce and the chosen roles, and a wrong `code_verifier` or a reused code answers `invalid_grant`. `config.ts` refuses an `ADMIN_OIDC_ISSUER` on the simulator host unless `ADMIN_ENVIRONMENT=local`; `boot.test.ts` pins that `preview` with that issuer exits 1 with a `boot_failed` line naming `ADMIN_OIDC_ISSUER`.
Done when: listed tests pass; `pnpm typecheck`, `pnpm lint`, `pnpm test`, `pnpm trace` green; with T-0031 running, `ADMIN_ENVIRONMENT=local ADMIN_DATA=api` console signs in as each persona (stated in the PR); only listed files touched
Out of scope: Identity Center itself, staff-token claims (ADR-013, unchanged), new console screens. Stop and ask on conflict with the spec.
