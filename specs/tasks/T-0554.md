Test first: an institution payout waiting for its second finance approver, clock past the approval's `expires_at`: on main it stays waiting forever.
- Extend `expireIfDue` and `resumable()` (T-0540/T-0549's one place for approval expiry) to institution payouts: rejected, expired, or open past `expires_at` → fail with `REJECTED` / `APPROVAL_EXPIRED`, then mark an open lapsed approval `expired` (after the fail, as for members).
- No reserve exists yet for institution payouts, so the fail has nothing to release; keep the `unreserve` call path the member side uses so T-0550 only adds the reserve.
- The institution's finance staff are told, as the payer is for members (existing notification path; no new copy if one fits).
**Tests.** an expired institution payout fails once with `APPROVAL_EXPIRED` and the approval ends `expired`; a rejected one fails `REJECTED`; a replayed sweep does nothing more; the institution's balance is unchanged (bigint).

Out of scope: the reserve (T-0550), cool-off for institution payouts. Stop and ask on conflict with the spec.

## As-built
- `expireIfDue` no longer skips institution payouts, and `resumable()` no longer excludes them from its "approval rejected, expired or open past `expires_at`" clause. A rejected or lapsed institution payout fails with `REJECTED` / `APPROVAL_EXPIRED` in the sweep, then an open lapsed approval is marked `expired` (after the fail, as for members). There is no reserve for it yet (T-0550), so the fail only ends the transfer; `fail()` already calls `unreserve`, which T-0550 will make real for institutions.
- Finance staff are told by the console's payout list (I09): `tellPayer` returns early for an institution by design (no devices), so no new copy was added.
- An institution payout to a new payee over the threshold still cools off for a day first (unchanged, out of scope); the tests cover cool-off, then waiting, then expiry.
- Tests (`payouts/service.test.ts`, new describe T-0554): an expired approval fails the payout once with `APPROVAL_EXPIRED`, the approval ends `expired`, the institution's money is unchanged and the payout is not listed again; a rejected approval fails it `REJECTED`. Both fail on the old code.
