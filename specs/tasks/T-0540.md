## As built
Decided by Zvi (2026-11-04: "if it doesn't contradict our plan, push it through"): build the member-payout reserve the card describes; split off the two parts the scout found the card did not anticipate.
- **Reserve** (`payouts/service.ts` activity `reserve`, `unreserve`; keys `{key}:reserve` and `{key}:unreserve`): a **member's** payout that has to wait (cooling off, or for an approver) moves the amount wallet → pending, so `available` drops at once and the same money cannot be spent again (INSUFFICIENT_FUNDS). It is its own workflow step: `runPayout` reserves right after the cool-off hold, again (idempotently) when the cool-off ends, and when an approval is still not given. When the purse no longer holds the amount on a resumed run (a crash between the hold and the reserve leaves the money spendable until the cool-off ends, up to 24 hours, when the reserve is retried; or a payout older than the reserve) the payout is **failed** with `INSUFFICIENT_FUNDS` instead of overdrawing the wallet. Journal kind is `payout` (not a new kind): the member's transaction history reads the wallet account, so the reserve is the one `payout` row and the later journal, posted from pending, is not a second row.
- **Submit** posts from pending when a reserve exists, from the wallet when it does not (a payout that goes straight to the rail).
- **Release, exactly once:** `fail` (rail failure, webhook failure, `INVALID_PAYEE`, and every path below) reverses the payout journal and then the reserve (`{key}:unreserve`); `decide()` reject releases it too (no-op without a reserve).
- **The approval ends the wait** (`expireIfDue`, run before the reserve is checked): a member's payout whose approval was **rejected** (by finance through the approvals service), **expired** (marked by a refused late decision), or is open past its `expires_at`, is failed (`REJECTED` / `APPROVAL_EXPIRED`), the reserve released and the payer told; an open lapsed approval is then marked `expired` (after the fail, so a crash between leaves it findable). `resumable()` returns such payouts so the worker's existing sweep drives it; no worker change. Institution payouts keep waiting as before.
- **Not done, own follow-up cards:**
  - *Institution payouts are not reserved.* Their money is one account (`institution_master`); reserving into `pending` would collide with the account the ACH holds use (T-0617, `institutionHolds`), so it needs a distinct account type: a migration the card does not list (with a real-Postgres handoff).
  - *Expiry for institution payouts.* They keep waiting for their second finance member as before.
  - *Cancel.* There is no member cancel route for payouts, so a cancel path has nothing to call; `unreserve` is ready for it.
  - *Concurrency.* The funds check and the reserve are not a lock (T-0544): two simultaneous requests can both pass the check.
- **Files outside the card's list:** `apps/worker/src/payouts/payout-workflow.test.ts` (two expectations: the money is reserved from the start) and `docs/ops/autopilot-log.md`. `wallet/balances.ts` does not exist; the helpers live in `payouts/service.ts`.
