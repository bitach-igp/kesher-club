Test first: TST-INS-016.
- Decision 2 (self-serve KYB). Behind the new flag `institutions.selfServe` (default false): off answers FEATURE_OFF.
- A signed-in person with no institution `POST /institution-applications` (Idempotency-Key): legal name, market, registration number, address. It creates the institution, makes the applicant its admin (an `org.staff` row) and opens a draft. One open application per person.
- While a draft: add signatories (name, role, date of birth, ownership share; stored as `org.kyb_person` with the applicant as recorder; identity documents are never stored, per AGENTS.md) and propose the own-name payout account through the existing payout-account path (T-0618), so REQ-INS-012 still holds.
- `POST /institution-applications/{id}/submit`: needs at least one signatory and the proposed account. It moves the case into the admin KYB queue (T-0620), where the existing checks and the decision run (REQ-INS-011). The money gate (REQ-INS-010) keeps deposits, allowances, credit and payouts shut until a decision approves.
- The application's status (draft, submitted, needs more info, approved, approved with limits, rejected) is read back for I01. "Needs more info" reopens the draft with the reviewer's note.
