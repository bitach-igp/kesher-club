## As built
- **Schema.** `org/0008_institution_application.sql` adds two insert-only tables: `org.institution_application`
  (one per applicant, by a unique index on the applicant) and `org.institution_application_event`
  (`account_proposed`, `submitted`). The status is read from them, not stored: draft until the first submit; after
  it, the institution's latest KYB decision if one came after the submit, else `submitted`. Draft and
  `needs_more_info` are editable; the reviewer's reasons come back as `reviewerNote`. Desktop handoff
  `handoff/desktop/T-0632-institution-application.md`.
- **Endpoints.** `POST /institution-applications` (key), `GET /me/institution-application`,
  `POST …/{id}/signatories` (key), `POST …/{id}/account`, `POST …/{id}/submit`.
  - Creating one makes the institution (through `org.institution_onboarding`, as an admin-created one), the
    application, and the applicant's `org.staff` admin row, all in one transaction. It is audited as
    `institution.apply`, and the applicant gains the `org_admin` token role.
  - An applicant who is already staff, or already applied, gets 409. Someone else's application reads 404.
- **Signatories** are `org.kyb_person` rows recorded through `InstitutionKyb.addPerson` with the applicant as
  recorder, so compliance sees them where it already works. The card's date of birth and ownership share are not
  collected. `kyb_person` has no columns for them, and the KYB vendor checks identity from the person's own
  member KYC (`self: true` links the applicant). That keeps PII out of this table.
- **The account** is a proposal held in the event:
  - IL: an Israeli IBAN, through `checkPayee`.
  - US: an ABA routing number (checksum) and a 4 to 17 digit account number.
  - The holder must be the organisation's registered name (`sameHolder`, REQ-INS-012). It reads back masked.
  - Compliance still records the verified account from the bank's letter (T-0623). The proposal shows what to
    expect.
- **Submit** needs a `signatory` role and the account (422 lists what is missing). It opens a
  `kyb_application` ops case (medium) carrying the institution and application ids and a one-line summary. A
  resubmit after "needs more info" opens a new case.
- **Outside the card's list:**
  - `apps/api/src/admin/cases.ts` adds `kyb_application` to `OPS_CASE_KINDS`.
  - `apps/admin/src/ops/view.ts` adds its words.
  - `apps/admin/src/data/types.ts` adds it to the kind union.
  - The contract's case-kind enum gains the value.
  - Together these put the application in the queue compliance already works.
- The money gate is unchanged: an applied institution has no KYB decision, so the gate reads `needs_more_info`
  and deposits, allowances, credit and payouts answer FEATURE_OFF until approval.
