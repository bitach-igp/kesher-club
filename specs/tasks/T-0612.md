As built:
- **Whose money.** I09 pays out of the institution's own account (T-0613, T-0527), never a staff member's purse, and shows what that account holds.
- **The form** checks the IBAN with the ports' mod-97 checksum and requires an Israeli account (`@kesher/ports/payout`, types and pure functions only). It also checks a name, an amount in shekels, a reference, and that the amount fits the account. A failing draft never calls the server; the browser run counted zero server actions. The payout then takes a step-up and keeps its attempt key across it.
- **The list** gives each payout's state in words:
  - "Cooling off until {Jerusalem time}" for a new payee over ₪2,000;
  - "waiting for a second finance approver";
  - "on its way";
  - "paid";
  - "the bank returned it ({code}); the money is back in the institution account": the payer's notice, since an institution has no device to push to;
  - "rejected… nothing was paid".
  The requester sees "a different finance member approves it" and no buttons. A second finance member gets Approve, and Reject with a reason field; a blank reason is never sent.
- **Verified in Chromium** with the seeded yeshiva. A scratch harness, in its own PGlite only, funded its account with ₪20,000 and made one payee known through a settled ₪100 payout.

  - ₪6,000 and ₪5,500 to the known payee both wait, with no Approve for Rivka.
  - Shimon: Reject without a reason is held back, then sent with one ("Rejected… Nothing was paid").
  - His Approve sends the ₪6,000 out, the account goes from ₪19,900.00 to ₪13,900.00, and the payout lists both approvers.
  - The bank-returned (`reversed`) copy is rendered from the state; the browser run did not drive a failing webhook.
