# T-0723 [PAY] A send between mirrored members checks both Crowded accounts first, and a send whose second leg is permanently refused is compensated, not left
REQ: REQ-PAY-001, REQ-PAY-003, REQ-PAY-005 (after T-0698, T-0722) · Sprint: later (ADR-018 decision of 2026-11-11, proposed). Design B only (D1): under Design A the compensation is a book payment before any journal and this card is rewritten.
Files: apps/api/src/p2p/send.ts, apps/api/src/p2p/send.test.ts, apps/api/src/p2p/holds.ts, apps/api/src/p2p/holds.test.ts, apps/worker/src/mirror/drain.ts (the P2P repair hook: one function added), apps/worker/src/mirror/drain.test.ts, specs/tasks/T-0723.md, specs/tasks/README.md
Source: ADR-018, M2 and M10. The send posts its journal in `P2pSendService.post` (`apps/api/src/p2p/send.ts:281`) and the release in `holds.ts:88`; the planner (T-0721) queues the two legs. What is P2P's own: refusing a send up front when a side cannot be mirrored, and what a dead second leg means for a transfer. Requests and splits (`apps/api/src/p2p/requests.ts:147`) pay through the same send, so they are covered here without a change.
Assumption: M2 (two legs through checking, partial failure), M10 (a `Failed` contact or a `Frozen` or `Closed` account cannot be mirrored), M1 (Design B: the ledger is ahead of Crowded for a while, so the repair is a reversing journal plus a compensating leg).
Test first: TST-PAY-001, TST-PAY-003, TST-PAY-005. With `wallet.mirror` on: a send where the recipient's Crowded account is `Frozen` is refused before any transfer is created, with `FEATURE_OFF` naming the status (not the member), and the same for the sender; a send between two open accounts posts one `p2p` journal and queues two legs; a replay of the same Idempotency-Key queues nothing more (TST-PAY-005); a new-counterparty hold (`p2p_hold`) queues nothing, and its release queues two legs once; a cancelled hold queues nothing; if leg 1 settled and leg 2 is dead with `ACCOUNT_UNAVAILABLE` or `NOT_FOUND` (the recipient's account froze in between), the repair queues a compensating `from_checking` leg to the sender under `{journalKey}:comp` AND posts a reversing journal `{key}:repair` (`reverse()`), both only while the recipient's wallet still holds the amount (the reversal is guarded by `postNoOverdraft` on the recipient), the transfer ends `reversed`, the sender is told once; if the recipient already spent it, no reversal is posted and the case `mirror_half_pair` stays for a person with the amounts; a leg 2 that fails for an outage is retried, never repaired; the repair is itself idempotent (a second run posts nothing); money asserts compare `bigint`; with `wallet.mirror` off every existing P2P test passes unchanged.
- The up-front check reads the account status through `FundingAccountPort.account` (T-0698) at most once per send and caches nothing across sends; a vendor outage on that read does not refuse the send (Design B: the cap in T-0724 is the brake), it is logged as a number.
Done when: listed tests pass; `pnpm typecheck`, `pnpm lint`, `pnpm test`, `pnpm trace` green; only listed files touched
Out of scope: cross-currency P2P (the planner mirrors its USD leg only); the exposure cap (T-0724); a recipient who never had a Crowded account (M10, onboarding is T-0698 and T-0699); notifications copy.
