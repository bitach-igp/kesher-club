Source: T-0548 Out of scope. In `AddonOrderService.create` the credit is applied (`credit.apply(..., id, ...)`, an entitlement consumption keyed `addon:{id}` with a fresh `uuidv7` id) before the order row is inserted. Two creates with the same Idempotency-Key both pass the `byKey` check and both consume; the loser's insert hits the unique violation, returns the winner's order, and its own `addon:{loserId}` consumption stays for good: the member loses plan credit for an order that does not exist. Re-read `create`, `restoreCredit` and `credit.ts` first.
Test first: TST-MKT-001. A member with eSIM credit in their plan sends two `create` calls with one key at once (`Promise.all`): one order row, one charge journal, but the entitlement usage is two orders' units on main.
- When the order insert fails (the unique violation, or any other error), the credit applied for that attempt is handed back: the same negative consumption row `restoreCredit` writes (`addon:{id}:refund`, idempotent on conflict), for the attempt's own id. Factor `restoreCredit` to take an order id and the credit amount so the refund path and this one share it.
- The winner's credit stays consumed once; a replay of the key later (the `existing` path) consumes nothing.
- Known window left open and named here: a crash between `credit.apply` and the insert still strands one consumption. Closing it means moving the consumption after the row (the quote is stored on the row); only do that if it fits the 400 lines, else leave it in the card's As-built as a follow-up.
