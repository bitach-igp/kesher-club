Decision (2026-10-20): an institution's payout moves the institution's money, out of its own ledger account (T-0613), never the requesting staff member's purse. So the operations are `POST /institutions/{id}/payouts` and `GET /institutions/{id}/payouts`, plus `POST /payouts/{id}/approve` and `/reject`, all for org finance of that institution. The member's own `POST /payouts` is unchanged.

As built:
- **The payout source.** `PayoutService` takes a payout source, either a member or an institution and its requester.
  - An institution's payout checks the institution's balance (`INSUFFICIENT_FUNDS` below zero).
  - Its journal debits the institution's account and carries `institutionId` and the payee's name.
  - Its approval request is scoped to the institution, with the finance requester as the first approver.
  - A failed one is reversed as before but pushes nothing (an institution has no device); the console's list shows `reversed`.
  - The worker's resume rebuilds the source from the transfer.
- **Decide.** The requester's approve answers 409 (one decision per person). The same approver approving again returns the payout as it stands. Approve and reject while cooling off answer 409, since the approval opens after the cool-off.
- **A rejected payout** fails its transfer with `REJECTED`, shown as `rejected`; no journal was ever posted.
- **The monthly export** learns the `payout` kind ("Payout to {payee} •••• {last4}: {reference}") and a `payoutsOut` total; until now a payout journal would have been counted as a fee. `ExportFile.totals.payoutsOut` is additive.
- **The list** is cursor-paged on (created_at, id), and `available` is the institution's balance. Staff appear by display name through `institutions/staff-names.ts`, now shared with the allowance history. Payouts made in the same millisecond tie on time, so the test checks that the pages partition the list, not their order.
