- `LoadService` records `last_asked_at` (upsert) after every vendor read of a held load, including an `UNKNOWN_LOAD` answer, in `release()` and in the worker's `resume()`.
- `stale()` returns a `pending_hold` load only when it has no marker, its marker is older than `HELD_RECHECK_MS` (6 hours, a named constant), or its hold is due (`holdReleasesAt` passed: asked every cycle until it releases). `submitted` and `pending` loads are unchanged.
- The trade-off is stated in the code comment: a return with no webhook is found within `HELD_RECHECK_MS` instead of within 10 minutes; a webhook still fails the load at once.
**Tests.** a not-due held load is read once, not again inside the interval, read again after it; a due hold is read every cycle; a never-asked hold is read on its first sweep; an `UNKNOWN_LOAD` hold records its marker and still opens one case; a return during the hold is found by the first sweep after the interval and fails the load with the hold reversed once; the worker's sweep report counts (`attempted`, `vendorReads`) match, updating the T-0539 expectations in `load-workflow.test.ts` that count held loads every pass.
