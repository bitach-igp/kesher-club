Test first: TST-INS-011 (screen rows). The institution page shows its KYB decision and what each capability does under it, from the ADR-017 table. The KYB page lists:
- the people with their KYC tier;
- each check with its result and evidence reference;
- the open hits, each with Dispose (false positive or true match, with a note);
- Decide (outcome, risk rating and reasons, with each blocking reason listed in words before it is sent);
- Approve for a second reviewer when the decision is high risk.

The tests cover:
- the blocking reasons read as words;
- the proposer of a high-risk decision gets no Approve;
- support sees the page with no buttons.

Done when: listed tests pass; `pnpm typecheck`, `pnpm lint`, `pnpm test`, `pnpm trace` green; the flow verified in Chromium against the T-0031 stack; only listed files touched
Out of scope: document upload. Stop and ask on conflict with the spec.

As built:
- **Two API additions the screen needed.** Both are additive to the contract, and both are listed in Files above:
  - `KybPerson.kycTier`: the highest tier the linked member has passed;
  - `KybRecord.proposedByMe`: set with `pendingApproval`, and true for the proposer, who is offered no Approve.
- **The form handler lives at `kyb/actions/route.ts`.** Next.js serves either a page or a route handler at a path, not both, so `kyb/route.ts` beside `kyb/page.tsx` broke the dev server. The page posts `action=dispose|decide|approve`.
- **What the screen shows.**
  - Each blocking code reads as words naming the institution or the person. An unknown code is shown as it came.
  - Each capability is open or closed per ADR-017's table. It also shows as closed while a sanctions match is open (T-0624's suspension of payouts and credit), so a suspended institution reads correctly here.
- **The memory binding** seeds the yeshiva with one open hit on a board member and Rivka at T1. Locally the page shows two blocking reasons until both are dealt with.
- **Checked in Chromium** against the T-0031 stack, with a scratch-only second institution carrying an open sanctions hit on its chair:
  - Support sees the reasons and no buttons.
  - Cleo disposes of the hit as a false positive.
  - An approval with no risk rating is caught before the call.
  - Cleo's high-risk proposal shows "You proposed it", with no Approve.
  - Carl approves it, and deposits, allowances, credit and payouts all read open on both the KYB page and the institution page.
- **Not on this screen yet:**
  - recording the own payout account and approving it (T-0623's API exists; the seed drives it);
  - running the automated checks.
  Both are follow-ups.
