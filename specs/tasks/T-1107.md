Done when: the shape in T-1106 is built and its tests pass with the standard gates and a desktop real-Postgres handoff
Out of scope: Real Crowded movement between chapters (T-0755 to T-0758). Stop and ask on conflict with the spec.
Crowded cross-reference (2026-11-25, docs only, no vendor call): the sequence sender, organization, recipient's account is not supported by documented Crowded calls: a book payment cannot cross chapters (Q45), `subaccounts/fund` has no documented amount unit or response, and movement between sub-accounts is blocked on counsel (ADR-018). Model the chain in our ledger first and bind Crowded legs only after N4 and N5 are answered.
