Test first: TST-INS-011. An institution's KYB is a record made of four parts:
- the people in it: board or vaad members, control persons and signatories, each linked to a member party once they sign up;
- the checks: registry, identifier, exempt status, sanctions, PEP and individual KYC, each with its result (`clear`, `hit`, `missing`, `excepted`) and its evidence reference;
- the dispositions of hits;
- the decisions: `needs_more_info`, `approved_with_limits`, `approved`, `rejected`, with a risk rating (`low`, `medium`, `high`) and reasons.

Checks, dispositions and decisions are insert-only; the current state is the latest row. `InstitutionKyb.decide` refuses an approval while:
- any registry or identifier check is missing or a hit;
- the exempt-status check is missing and not excepted with a reason;
- any sanctions or PEP hit is undisposed;
- any control person lacks T2.

A `high` approval opens an approval request (kind `kyb_decision`) that a second, different compliance reviewer must approve. Every decision is audited. The tests cover:
- an amuta with an active registry entry and a T2 chair is refused while its CEO's sanctions hit is undisposed, and is approved once it is disposed as a false positive;
- a US school missing from IRS data is approved only with an exception and a reason;
- a high-risk approval waits for a second reviewer, and the proposer cannot be the second;
- a new institution starts at `needs_more_info`.
