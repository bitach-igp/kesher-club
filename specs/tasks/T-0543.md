## As built
Decided by Zvi (2026-11-04: "if it doesn't contradict our plan, push it through"). Two of the card's premises did not match the code, so it was built to the card's *goal* (a stuck `pending` send or load is finished once and never posted twice) by a different route:
- **No single transaction.** The card asks for release and settle "in one transaction" (`holds.ts:85-86`, `send.ts:272-273`). `ledger.post` and `TransferService.apply` each open their own transaction and neither takes one, so a single transaction would mean changing `ledger/post.ts` and `transfers/service.ts` (the latter is on the card's list, the former is not) for every money path. Not done here (review F09/F13 is the systemic fix, its own card). Instead the workflow branches on the stored state, and the journal is posted under a key, so a replay finds the first journal: nothing is posted twice, and the only step left on a `pending` row is the settle.
- **The sweeps did not pick up `pending`** (the card says they do): `P2pSendService.stale` and `LoadService.stale` selected `submitted` (and `pending_hold` for loads) only, and `expiredHolds` selects `pending_hold` only. Both `stale()` queries now include `pending`, and the worker's existing sweeps (`send-workflow`, `load-workflow`) finish those rows through `runSend` / `applyVendorState` with their stored keys; no worker change.
- **P2P** (`send.ts`, `holds.ts`): `runSend` sends a `pending` row to `release`; both release paths (same-currency and converted) accept `pending`, post (or find, by key) the release journal, skip `release_hold` and settle. The expiry check applies to `pending_hold` only. A replay after the payee became known does not re-decide the hold (it never did: `shouldHold` runs only for `submitted` rows); a test pins it.
- **Loads** (`loads.ts`): the same shape was real, and worse: a `payment_intent.succeeded` webhook (or a retry, or the sweep) on a `pending` load went through `settle()`, which posted `{key}:settle` from the partner account **on top of** the hold and release journals, crediting the member twice. Now `settle()` and `applyVendorState` treat `pending` as a release in progress and finish it through `release()` (which asks the vendor again and fails the load, reversing both journals, if it was returned: T-0539). Tests: webhook, retry and sweep each credit once.
- **Files:** the card lists `transfers/service.ts`; it needed no change. Outside the card's list: `docs/ops/autopilot-log.md`.
- **Not done:** a client retry racing the sweep on one `pending` row gets IDEMPOTENCY_CONFLICT from the second `settle` (no double credit); real concurrency is T-0544. A missed "you received" notification on a send that settled but whose notify step died is not resent (the card does not list it).
