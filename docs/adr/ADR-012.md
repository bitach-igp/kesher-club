# ADR-012: Core platform ("mothership")

Status: Proposed · Decide by: Nov 2026, after Money20/20 · Reviewed: 2026-10-16

## Context
Ezra wants to hand regulatory and financial risk to a licensed partner and launch by Jan–Feb 2027.
On the call he named two "mothership" candidates: Nium ("a neobank giving you a full package") and
SDK.finance ("613 integrations"). Research of 2026-10-16 is in
`C:\dev\bitach\IGP\02-plans-and-research\sdk-finance-mothership-assessment.md`; summary in the live spec, Tab 1.

## Options
1. **Licensed partner for regulated rails + our own ledger/app** (recommended). The partner holds or
   sponsors the FBO account, BIN, card processing, KYC/AML program and money-transmission cover.
   We keep the NestJS monolith, Temporal and the Postgres ledger as a sub-ledger.
2. **SDK.finance core + a separate BaaS.** SDK.finance is software only: its SaaS agreement §12.5 says it is
   not a bank, EMI, processor or compliance provider. It would replace our ledger, wallets and back office
   (in Java), but every rail is still a separate contract. Pricing: EUR 10,000 for the first 3 months, then
   EUR 5,500/month + EUR 0.05/txn. §11.3 lets it reuse customizations we pay for.
3. **Build every rail ourselves under our own licences.** Not before the Israeli licence exists; years, not months.

## Decision
_Pending._ Recommended: option 1. Gate: a partner's written acceptance of a consumer, parent-funded,
cross-border student program under its licences, plus US and Israeli counsel opinions.

## Consequences
- `CardIssuingPort`, `PayoutPort`, `FxPort`, `KycPort` keep the partner swappable; the simulator stays default.
- Ledger reconciles daily against the partner's records (`external_ref`), breaks to `suspense`.
- SDK.finance stays a fallback accelerator if we ever abandon our own ledger.
