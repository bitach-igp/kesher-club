# ADR-003: Ledger engine

Status: Proposed · Decide by: Sprint 4

## Context
See the live spec, Tab 3 §4.

## Options
- Own Postgres ledger, run as a sub-ledger reconciled daily to the licensed partner's system of record (see ADR-012); funds of record live at the partner, entitlements/allocations/subscriptions with us
- TigerBeetle if over 1k TPS
- Rejected: SDK.finance ledger (Java, outside our codebase, still needs every rail; reviewed 2026-10-16)

## Recommendation (draft, 2026-10-18, for Zvi to accept or amend)
Our own Postgres ledger, as built (T-0201 to T-0206): insert-only `ledger.journal` and
`ledger.posting`, the balance cache in `ops.account_balance`, the nightly proof and reconciliation
against the partner's records. It is a sub-ledger: funds of record live at the licensed partner
(ADR-012), and the reconciliation job is the contract between the two. TigerBeetle stays the answer
above 1,000 transfers per second, which is years away for a membership of students; revisit when the
JIT decision service is the bottleneck, not before. SDK.finance stays rejected (reviewed 2026-10-16).
