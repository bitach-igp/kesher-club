# ADR-014: Pricing and the spec-versus-code conflicts the bank-review package found

Status: Accepted · Decided: 2026-10-19 (Zvi asked Claude to decide on best judgement; Zvi may overturn any line)

## Context
The bank-review package (T-0912, `docs/bank-review/`) wrote every gap between the live spec and the code
as an `Open:` line. Nine were conflicts the card said to stop and ask about, and two pricing choices from
the currency cards (T-0515, T-0517) were waiting on a decision. Zvi asked for best guesses, with weight on
the currency work from ADR-007. Each decision below names who it favours, why, and the card that carries
any code. Incident copy on `/status` stays English only (Zvi, 2026-10-19).

## Decisions

### Pricing
1. **A converted send charges the recipient's plan markup.** The recipient chose to receive in the
   other currency (REQ-PAY-007), so the recipient pays for the conversion, at their own plan's rate.
   The sender sees exactly what lands before confirming (the send preview, T-0518). No change.
2. **A load into the other purse carries the purse owner's plan markup**, like every conversion.
   ADR-007 says the plan markup applies on top of the club rate for conversions. At the mid rate, a
   load through the other purse would be a free conversion that undercuts the convert screen: load $100
   into the shekel purse instead of converting. Card **T-0519**.

### Workflows and keys
3. **Workflow ids are the client's Idempotency-Key as received; the spec follows the code.** Tab 3 §7
   writes `load:{key}`, `transfer:{key}`, `fx:{quoteId}`, `payout:{key}`. The prefix adds nothing: the
   transfer's kind already namespaces it, and a key reused for another kind is refused with
   `IDEMPOTENCY_CONFLICT` (tested in every flow). Journals keep the derived keys (`{key}:settle`,
   `{key}:hold`, `{key}:release`). Tab 3 §7 is updated to match.

### Partner legs (wait for ADR-006, no code now)
4. **Loads (SEQ-02) and P2P book transfers (SEQ-06): the ledger stays a shadow until the processor is
   chosen.** That is constitution article 2 and ADR-006's build decision: the money epics run against the
   simulators, and the processor adapter is one later card per port. When the processor is chosen, the
   funding transfer and the book transfer arrive as port calls inside the existing workflows, before the
   journal, with SEQ-06's compensation (processor failure, no journal, `PROVIDER_UNAVAILABLE`). The flags
   `wallet.enabled` and `p2p.enabled` stay off until then.

### Limits
5. **The new-counterparty hold threshold is ₪500 or $150.** Tab 3 §3 names only shekels. $150 is the
   round dollar line just above ₪500 at the rates we see (₪555 at 3.70). REQ-PAY-003 now names both.
   Compliance may lower either.

### Agent intents
6. **An approved agent send runs the member's own send path.** Approval calls the same send as
   `POST /transfers`, keyed `intent:{intentId}`, so eligibility, limits, balance, the new-pair hold and
   the receiving-currency conversion all apply. Card **T-1008**. `mcp.money.propose` stays off until it
   merges.

### FX
7. **A failed vendor leg closes the conversion; nothing stays `submitted`.** A vendor reject voids the
   quote and fails the transfer with no journal (SEQ-08). An outage keeps the transfer `submitted` for a
   retry with the same key while the quote lives; a retry after the quote expired fails it, and a worker
   sweep fails any FX transfer still `submitted` five minutes after its quote expired. If the vendor did
   fill, the hedge sits on `fx_position`, which reconciliation already watches. Card **T-0520**.
8. **FX quotes stay in process memory while one API instance runs.** Before a second instance, quotes
   move to a table. Card **T-0522**, scheduled with the scale-out.

### Cards
9. **Card settlement is built against the simulator before `card.virtual` turns on.** The clearing
   event moves a hold from `pending` to `partner_settlement`; a clearing above the hold draws the
   difference from the wallet; an authorisation that does not clear within 7 days releases. Card
   **T-0410**.
10. **A shekel card payment draws on the shekel purse, as ADR-007 says.** The purse to try first is the
    shekel purse for a merchant in Israel, and the authorisation's own currency elsewhere. When that
    purse is short, the other purse pays, converted at the club rate with the plan markup, the rate
    version on the hold journal. One purse pays the whole amount; there is no split. Without a valid
    rate the conversion path is skipped and the purse check decides. Card **T-0409**.

### Institutions
11. **Allowance runs get their endpoint, a transfer row per payment, and the money flag.**
    `POST /institutions/{id}/allowance-runs` starts the existing run under the two-person approval it
    already has; each payment becomes an `ops.transfer` of kind `allowance` so monitoring sees it; and
    `institutions.money` gates runs as it gates collections. Card **T-0607**.

### Closure
12. **A load that converted into the other purse refunds to its source in the instrument currency,
    pro rata at the load's own rate.** A member who loaded $100 and received ₪367.22, and closes with
    ₪183.61 of it left, gets $50.00 back to the card. The member neither gains nor loses from rate moves
    between load and closure; the club unwinds its own position. Card **T-0521**.

## Consequences
- Cards T-0519, T-0520, T-0521 and T-0409 are built now; T-0410, T-0522, T-0607 and T-1008 are written
  and scheduled.
- The bank-review package marks each of these lines `Decided (ADR-014)` instead of `Open:`.
- The live spec's Tab 3 §7 drops the key prefixes, and REQ-PAY-003 names both thresholds.
- Every decision here is reversible by a later ADR; none changes a migration.
