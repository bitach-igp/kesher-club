# ADR-015: Closure keeps the conversion markup

Status: Accepted · Decided: 2026-10-19 (Zvi: decide on the company recovering its money) · Revises ADR-014 decision 12

## Context
ADR-014 decision 12 refunds a load that converted into the other purse to its source, pro rata at the
load's own rate. As built in T-0521, that rate included the markup, so a closing member got the markup
back on money they never spent: $100 loaded as ₪367.22 at 3.70 less 75 bps refunded $100.00, and fee
income gave back its ₪2.78. Zvi asked for the choice that lets the company recover its money.

## Decision
**The club keeps the load's markup.** The conversion was delivered when the member loaded, and the markup
paid for it. On closure, what is left in the purse goes back to the source at the load's club rate before
markup, rounded down, and never more than the load paid. No second markup is charged on the way back, and
the load's own rate still applies, so a rate move between load and closure neither helps nor hurts the
member.

- $100 loaded as ₪367.22, none spent: the card gets $99.24 back; fee income keeps ₪2.78.
- The same load with ₪183.61 left: the card gets $49.62 back.
- A same-currency load refunds exactly as before; nothing was converted, so there is no markup.

## Consequences
- Card **T-0523** changes `apps/api/src/wallet/closure.ts`: the refund journal has no fee-income leg, and
  after a whole refund the club's `fx_position` holds the shekels (or dollars) the markup earned.
- The member-facing closure screen must say a converted balance returns at the load's rate without its
  conversion fee; the copy belongs to the closure screen's own card.
- Reversible by a later ADR; no migration changes.
