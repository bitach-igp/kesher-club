# ADR-007: ILS handling

Status: Accepted · Decided: 2026-10-18 (Zvi) · Decide by: With ADR-006

## Context
See the live spec, Tab 3 §4. Members earn, spend and are paid in both USD and ILS: parents fund in
USD, yeshivot pay allowances and collect fees in ILS, landlords and shops take ILS. A product that
converts everything to one currency at the card edge loses the member money on every hop and hides
the rate. The ledger already keeps a USD and an ILS purse per member (REQ-WAL-001), so the question
was never the books; it was whether ILS is a first-class currency members choose, or a shadow of USD.

## Options
- Launch: USD card + shadow ILS sub-balance converted at authorisation (US-issued cards are USD at launch)
- Native ILS purse now, every movement names its currency, conversions between purses at a club-published rate
- Later: native ILS card settlement with an Israeli-licensed issuer (Rapyd, Airwallex, or our own licence)
- ILS payouts to yeshivot via the payout partner (Nium Masav or Rapyd)

## Decision
ILS is native. Every member has a USD purse and an ILS purse as equal ledger accounts, and every
money movement names the currency it moves in:

- **Loads** credit the purse the funder chooses; when the instrument settles in the other currency,
  the load converts at the club rate and the receipt shows both amounts (REQ-WAL-006).
- **Sends** are paid from the purse the sender chooses and received in the recipient's receiving
  currency, converted at the club rate at send time when the two differ; the transfer shows both
  legs and the rate version (REQ-PAY-006, REQ-PAY-007).
- **Allowances, fees and payouts** post in the institution's currency, ILS, with no conversion unless
  the member's purse of that currency is short and they choose to convert.
- **Conversions** between purses are internal journals at the club-published rate: one active rate
  per pair, versioned, sourced from the FX port on a refresh interval or set by hand, a manual rate
  needing two finance approvers and an audit entry (REQ-FX-003, REQ-FX-004, REQ-ADM-003). The plan
  markup (REQ-FX-002) applies on top of the club rate and stays on the journal. The vendor FX leg
  (FxPort) hedges the club's position; any difference between the vendor rate and the club rate lands
  on `fx_position`, which reconciliation watches.
- **Cards**: whether the processor settles ILS natively is a requirement we put to every ADR-006
  candidate. Until an issuer holds ILS for us, an ILS card authorisation draws on the member's ILS
  purse converted at the club rate at authorisation time. The shadow conversion moves from the purse
  to the card edge; the member's balances stay honest.

## Consequences
- The convert screen (M11) stays; the pay and load flows gain a currency choice (M14, M15, M12's
  load) and the settings screen (M33) a receiving-currency preference.
- `FxService` quotes against the club rate, not the vendor's live rate; a stale or missing rate refuses
  the quote with `PROVIDER_UNAVAILABLE` rather than guessing.
- Finance gets rate management in the admin API (list, propose, approve), two-person for manual rates.
- `fx_position` becomes a watched account: reconciliation reports its drift against vendor fills.
- ADR-006 gains a question for every candidate: native ILS settlement, or USD settlement with our
  conversion at authorisation.
- Cards T-0512 to T-0518 carry the work; `fx.enabled` and `p2p.enabled` gate it as before.
