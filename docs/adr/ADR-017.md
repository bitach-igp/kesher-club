# ADR-017: An institution's money waits on its KYB decision, one capability at a time

Status: Proposed for the bank's review, built against the simulator · Raised: 2026-10-20 (Zvi: research the KYB checks and continue) · Requirements REQ-INS-010 to REQ-INS-014

## Context
Institutions now hold money:
- they pay in by Masav, wire or ACH (T-0614, T-0617);
- they pay students from their balance (T-0604);
- they draw on a two-admin credit line (T-0616);
- they pay out to a bank account (T-0527).

Their staff pass individual KYC, but nothing checks the institution itself. The research in
`docs/bank-review/institution-kyb.md` sets out the US and Israeli obligations: the CDD rule with a control person
for nonprofits; for an amuta, the Banking Corporations Order and Directive 411, or the 2024 order for payment
companies. It finds that the sponsor bank owns the standard and Kesher runs it.

## Decision
- **Each capability waits on the decision.** An institution starts at `needs_more_info`. Deposits post,
  allowances run and payouts leave only once it is `approved` or `approved_with_limits`. A credit line can be
  set or drawn only when it is `approved`. `rejected` stops everything. A refused action answers `FEATURE_OFF`,
  naming the status. This is not behind a flag: it is a safety rule, like the overdraft rule.
- **Evidence before a decision.** Each check records what it saw: registry, exempt status, sanctions on the
  institution and every board member, control person and signatory, and individual KYC. That record is
  insert-only. An approval is refused while any screening hit is undisposed. A high-risk approval takes two
  different compliance reviewers, through the approval service.
- **The port before the vendors.** A `KybPort` with a simulator comes first. Vendors (the data.gov.il registry
  APIs, a US KYB vendor, an exempt-status source, a screening provider) bind to it behind
  `institutions.kyb.checks` only after the bank answers the questions in the research document.
- **Own-name payouts only.** A payout goes only to an account verified as held in the institution's
  registered name. Changing it reopens review.
- **Keeping it current.** A daily sanctions rescreen, and a full refresh every 12, 24 or 36 months by risk.
  An overdue refresh drops the decision to `approved_with_limits`.

## Consequences
- Cards: T-0619 (record and decision), T-0620 (HTTP), T-0621 (the gate), T-0622 (the port and simulator), T-0623
  (own-name payout account), T-0624 (rescreen and refresh sweep) and T-0926 (the console's review screen).
- T-0621 changes behaviour already merged: deposits, allowance runs, credit and payouts gain the gate, and
  the demo seed approves the demo yeshiva's KYB so the walkthroughs keep running.
- The refresh periods, the screening lists and threshold, third-party deposits and the credit lender are
  the bank's answers to give. Until then the code uses the values in this ADR as configuration defaults,
  not as policy.
- Reversible by a later ADR. The migration is additive.
