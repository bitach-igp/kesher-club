# ADR-016: A card hold refunds the markup on what was not spent

Status: Accepted · Decided: 2026-10-19 (Zvi: refund it if that's what the customer expects) · Revises T-0410's release and under-clearing legs

## Context
A converted card hold (T-0409) pays for a dollar authorisation from the shekel purse at the club rate plus
the plan's markup. T-0410 settled it. When the merchant cleared less than the hold, or nobody cleared it
within 7 days, the difference went back at the club rate and the club kept the markup on the whole hold,
following ADR-015's principle. Under that rule a $10 hold that never cleared cost the member ₪0.28.
ADR-015 concerns closure, where the member received and held the converted money. A hold that is released
or cleared lower is a purchase that did not happen, or happened for less. A member expects to pay
conversion only on what they spent.

## Decision
**The markup follows what was spent.** When a converted hold clears below the hold, or is released, the
purse that paid ends up charged exactly what the cleared amount costs at the hold's own rate and markup.
The rest of the gross and the rest of the markup go back to it. ADR-015 stands for closure.

- A $10 hold at 3.70 and 75 bps paid ₪37.28 and is released unspent: all ₪37.28 comes back, and fee income
  gives back ₪0.28.
- A $50 hold paid ₪186.39 and clears at $45: $45 costs ₪166.50 plus ₪1.25, so ₪18.64 comes back, ₪0.14 of
  it markup.
- A clearing above the hold is unchanged: the extra converts with the markup, as the hold did. So is a late
  clearing after a release, which converts the whole amount afresh.

## Consequences
- Card **T-0411** changes `apps/api/src/cards/settlement.ts`. The settling journal gains a fee-income leg
  whenever markup goes back.
- The hold's own rate still applies, so a rate move between authorisation and clearing neither helps nor
  hurts the member.
- Reversible by a later ADR; no migration changes.
